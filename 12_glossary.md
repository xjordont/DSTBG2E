# 21. Glossary

Terms set in *italics* in this book are defined here. Where a definition and a chapter disagree, the chapter governs.

**Adjacent node.** A node directly next to another node horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Nodes separated by a wall, including a wall printed across a tile or a mega-boss game board, are not adjacent, and nodes on different tiles are not adjacent (see §5.1).

**Aggro.** The state of being the enemies' preferred target. Enemy behaviours marked with the skull icon target the character who holds the aggro token.

**Aggro token.** The single token that marks the character with aggro. It is placed on the character with the highest threat at the start of each encounter and moves to the character who placed the most wound tokens on enemies during the preceding round of character turns (on a tie the current holder keeps it); in boss encounters it moves to any character who damages the boss during their turn.

**Ambush card.** A face-down card dealt beside each encounter tile when playing with mimics. It is revealed when the characters choose to open that tile's chest and shows either Treasure or Mimic.

**Area attack.** A boss attack marked with the area icon. It targets every character within range who occupies a node in one of the arcs marked on the behaviour card.

**Area-of-effect (AoE).** A character attack option marked with the AoE icon. One attack roll is compared against the block or resist value of every enemy on the targeted node.

**Armour.** An equipment card bearing the armour icon. One armour card may be equipped in the armour slot. Only the armour card's block or resist dice are rolled after a failed dodge.

**Armour slot.** The dashboard slot that holds one equipped *armour* card (see §3.2).

**Attack (major action).** The major action in which a character makes up to one attack with each weapon or spell equipped in a weapon slot, each attack using one attack option and paying its stamina cost (see §10.3).

**Attack option.** One line of a weapon or spell card, showing a stamina cost, dice and modifiers. Using an attack option is the *Attack* major action.

**Attack value.** The number of sword icons rolled on an attack, after modifiers and rerolls. For enemies the attack value is the fixed number printed in the attack icon.

**Back-up slot.** The dashboard slot that holds up to two treasure cards of any kind, carried but not equipped (see §3.2). During an encounter, cards in the back-up slot may be exchanged with the weapon and armour slots only by the *Equip* minor action; an *ember card* must be in the back-up slot to be used.

**Barrel.** A terrain token or model with an intact side and a destroyed side. No model may *move* into a node containing an intact barrel; a model that *dodges* or is pushed into the node destroys the barrel. Barrels are restored when the characters rest at the *bonfire* (see §5.5, Barrels).

**Basic node.** The most common node type. Basic nodes adjacent to a doorway are also entry nodes.

**Behaviour card.** A card drawn from a boss, mimic, invader or phantom behaviour deck at the start of that model's turn. Its icons are resolved left to right and it sets the model's attack range and dodge difficulty until the next card is drawn.

**Behaviour deck.** The face-down deck of behaviour cards built for a boss, mimic, invader or phantom at the start of its encounter. When it is empty, the discard pile is turned face down without shuffling to form the new deck.

**Behaviour icon.** A movement or attack icon on an enemy data card or a behaviour card. Icons are resolved left to right; an icon with no valid target is skipped.

**Bleed.** A condition. A model may hold one bleed token. If a second bleed token would be placed, the model suffers three damage that ignores block and resist and all bleed tokens are removed. Bleed tokens are removed only when the encounter ends.

**Block.** The defence rolled against a physical attack. The defence total is the sum of the sword icons on the two highest block dice rolled from all equipped cards. For enemies, block is the fixed value subtracted from a physical attack value.

**Bonfire.** The characters' safe haven, represented by the *bonfire* tile. Resting at the *bonfire* costs one *spark* and fully restores the characters, resets every encounter and allows visits to the Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre.

**Bonfire ascetic.** In campaign play, a one-time communal payment of sixteen *souls* made while resting at the *bonfire* that unlocks tier two treasure and increases every non-boss enemy's base health by two for the rest of the campaign, cumulative with the health scaling for character count (see §18.7).

**Boss.** A single powerful enemy whose turns are driven by a behaviour deck and whose base is divided into four arcs. There are three ranks: *mini-boss*, *main-boss* and *mega-boss*. Bosses do not scale with the number of characters and may not be pushed by characters.

**Boss arc.** One of the four sectors (front, left, right, back) of a boss's base. Each node on the tile lies in at least one arc; nodes on the line between two arcs lie in both.

**Boss tile.** The mini-boss tile or main-boss tile on which a boss encounter is fought. A main-boss encounter uses both boss tiles joined as one large tile. Mega-bosses use the mega-boss game board supplied with their expansion.

**Boss treasure.** A treasure card supplied with a boss. One of a boss's treasure cards is awarded at random when the boss is defeated (see §16.5).

**Calamity.** A condition from the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion. A model may hold one calamity token, which reduces its block, resist and dodge roll results by one. Calamity tokens are removed only when the encounter ends.

**Catalyst.** A weapon card that stores up to three spells of a matching school: a pyromancer flame stores pyromancies, a staff or catalyst stores sorceries, and a talisman or chime stores miracles. A stored spell occupies no equipment slot; its attack options are used as if it were equipped, and its block, resist, and dodge dice are ignored (see §4.3).

**Character dashboard.** The board that records a character's class: the heroic action, the four equipment slots, the stat progression track, the endurance bar, the threat value, and the four token slots (see §3.1).

**Chest.** A terrain token or model with a closed side and an open side; no model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into its node. A chest holds one face-down treasure card of the current tier, revealed and awarded when the encounter is *cleared* (see §5.5, Chests; §7.7). With mimics in play, a chest is opened only as §13 describes.

**Class-specific treasure.** A treasure card marked with a small white class emblem. It is sorted into the tier one or tier two deck by its stat requirements exactly as common treasure is.

**Cleared.** The result of an encounter in which every enemy model is defeated and at least one character survives. A *cleared* encounter card is marked with a black cube, rewards *souls* and (the first time only) treasure, and may thereafter be skipped.

**Common treasure.** A treasure card that is not starting, class-specific, transposed, legendary or boss treasure. Common treasure with any stat requirement of 25 or more is tier two; all other common treasure is tier one.

**Condition.** A lasting effect marked by a token on a model: *bleed*, *poison*, *frostbite*, *stagger*, *calamity* or *corrosion*. Conditions are applied when a model is hit by an attack bearing the matching icon.

**Corrosion.** A condition from the Gaping Dragon expansion. A model may hold one corrosion token, which reduces its block roll results by one. The token is removed when the affected character suffers damage.

**Crystal lizard.** An optional enemy from the Iron Keep expansion placed on a terrain node. It flees rather than fights, does not scale its health, does not count towards clearing the encounter, and awards a Titanite Scale card to the character who kills it (see §14).

**Damage cube.** A red cube. Each point of damage a character suffers places one damage cube on the endurance bar, filling from the right. A character who must place a damage cube and has no space dies.

**Data card.** The card that defines an enemy, boss, invader or phantom: name, threat, range, dodge difficulty, block and resist, behaviour icons or deck size, health and special ability.

**Defence total.** The sum of the sword icons on the two highest block or resist dice rolled, after modifiers. Damage suffered equals the enemy's attack value minus the defence total, to a minimum of zero.

**Dodge.** A defence against an attack that targets the character. The character pays one stamina, rolls their dodge dice and succeeds if the total equals or exceeds the attack's dodge difficulty. A successful dodge avoids the hit entirely and the character must move to an adjacent node.

**Dodge difficulty.** The number a dodge roll must equal or exceed. It is printed on an enemy data card or boss behaviour card. In player versus player it is derived from the stamina cost of the attacker's attack option.

**Doorway.** A gap in a tile's wall through which characters pass between tiles. Basic nodes beside a doorway are entry nodes.

**Dropped souls.** *Souls* placed on an encounter tile by a character who died there. Only that character may reclaim them, by surviving to *clear* that encounter. They are lost if the character dies again first.

**Ember.** An *ember card* consumed by a character as the *Ember* major action, or the state of being *embered* that results (see §4.4, §7.3).

**Ember card.** A tier one shop card. Discarding an *ember* card from the back-up slot (the *Ember* major action, or outside an encounter) makes the character *embered*. A character may carry only one *ember* card and may not carry one while *embered*.

**Ember token.** The token that marks an *embered* character. It begins the game beside the dashboard, is placed in the dashboard's ember slot when the character becomes embered, and is removed when the character dies (see §3.5, Ember; §7.3).

**Embered.** The state of a character with an *ember* token. An *embered* character is not *hollow*, reduces any single attack's damage of three or more by one, may summon a phantom before a boss encounter, and provokes invaders.

**Encounter.** A battle fought on one or more tiles against the enemies set out by an encounter card. An encounter ends *cleared* or *failed*.

**Encounter card.** The card dealt face down to each tile that lists the encounter's level, enemies, terrain and traps. It is revealed when the characters enter the tile.

**Encounter level.** The difficulty of an encounter, from 1 to 4. The levels of the encounters leading to a boss are printed on that boss's data card. Level 4 encounters precede mega-bosses only.

**Endurance bar.** The track of 10 spaces on the dashboard that holds both stamina cubes (filling from the left) and damage cubes (filling from the right). A *hollow* character's two hollow cubes block the two rightmost spaces, leaving eight (see §3.4).

**Entry node.** A basic node beside the doorway through which the characters entered the tile. Characters are placed on entry nodes at the start of an encounter, no more than three to a node.

**Equip.** A minor action that swaps cards between the back-up slot and the weapon and armour slots. The inventory may not be used during an encounter.

**Equipment card.** A treasure card that a character equips in a weapon or armour slot, stores in a catalyst, or attaches to an equipped card: weapon, shield, catalyst, spell, armour, and upgrade cards (see §4.1).

**Equipped.** The state of a card in the armour slot or a weapon slot of the dashboard. Cards in the back-up slot and the inventory are not equipped (see §3.2).

**Estus flask.** A character token. Flipping it as the Drink Estus major action removes all damage cubes from the endurance bar; stamina cubes, hollow cubes, and condition tokens are not removed. It is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Estus Flask).

**Failed.** The result of an encounter in which every character dies, or in which any *hollow* character dies. A *failed* encounter awards nothing, its card is turned face down, and the characters must rest at the *bonfire*.

**Final boss.** The last boss of the game: the main-boss, or the mega-boss if the players chose to add one. Defeating the final boss wins the game.

**Firekeeper.** The figure at the *bonfire* who raises character stats in exchange for *souls*.

**Fog gate.** The token marking the entrance to a boss encounter. In the standard game it moves to the *bonfire* tile after the first boss attempt and provides a shortcut back to the boss. In campaign play it has no effect.

**Frostbite.** A condition. A frostbitten character cannot recover stamina; a frostbitten enemy reduces its movement values by one. The token is removed at the end of the model's turn.

**Gravestone.** A terrain token or model. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into a node containing a gravestone. When an encounter that contains a gravestone is *cleared*, the players may *scry* one card (see §5.5, Gravestones).

**Health dial.** The dial that records the health of a boss, summon, invader, or mimic. Enemies record damage with wound tokens instead (see §15.4).

**Heat-up.** The point at which a boss, mimic or invader becomes more dangerous. When its health is reduced to or below its heat-up point, a heat-up card (or, for mimics and invaders, an unused standard card) is shuffled into its behaviour deck. Some bosses heat up differently; see Boss Special Rules.

**Heat-up card.** A behaviour card marked with the heat-up symbol. Heat-up cards are set aside when the behaviour deck is built and enter play only at heat-up.

**Heroic action.** The once-per-rest ability printed on the dashboard, resolved as a minor action by flipping the *heroic action* token to its used side. Its printed wording overrides other rules. The token is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Heroic Action).

**Hit.** A model is hit by an attack that was in range and that it did not successfully *dodge* or *parry*, even if the attack dealt no damage. Pushes and conditions apply only to models that are hit.

**Hollow.** The state of a character revived after dying. Two *hollow cubes* block the two rightmost spaces of the endurance bar. *Hollow* is removed only by resting at the *bonfire* or by becoming *embered*. A *hollow* character who dies is *lost* and the encounter is *failed*.

**Hollow cube.** One of eight cubes of a colour distinct from the damage, stamina, and stat cubes. Two hollow cubes block the two rightmost spaces of a *hollow* character's endurance bar (see §7.2).

**Invader.** A hostile phantom drawn by *embered* characters. An invader joins a standard encounter, acts after every character's turn using its own behaviour deck, and awards *souls* and its treasure when killed.

**Invader token.** A token from the Phantoms expansion that shows an invader icon or is blank. When invaders are in play, one is placed face down on each face-down encounter card and is revealed when the characters enter that tile (see §12.2).

**Inventory.** A character's unlimited stack of unequipped treasure cards, kept face down beside the dashboard. Outside an encounter it may be rearranged freely and cards may be traded one for one between inventories; during an encounter it cannot be accessed (see §3.2).

**Legendary treasure.** A rare treasure card marked with an orange light under a chest. Five are shuffled into the tier two deck when the tier two shop is first stocked; in campaign play legendary treasure is obtained only by *transposition*.

**Lost.** The state of a *hollow* character who has died (see §7.2). A *lost* character is removed from play and is revived only when the characters rest at the *bonfire*.

**Luck.** A character token. Flipping it allows the character to reroll any or all dice of one of their own attack or defence rolls; the dice are chosen together and the new results stand. It is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Luck).

**Magical damage.** Damage from an attack marked with the white circle icon. Magical damage is reduced by resist.

**Main-boss.** The second boss of the standard game, fought on both boss tiles after the mini-boss. Its defeat wins the standard game unless a mega-boss follows.

**Major action.** The one principal action of a character's turn: *Attack*, *Rest*, *Drink Estus* or *Ember*.

**Mega-boss.** An optional final boss from a mega-boss expansion, fought on its own game board after a Level 4 encounter. Death and hollowing in a mega-boss encounter follow the usual rules (see §7.2).

**Mega-boss game board.** The double-sided board supplied with a mega-boss expansion. Its Level 4 encounter side is the tile of the Level 4 encounter and its mega-boss side is the tile of the mega-boss encounter (see §6.7, §15.3).

**Mimic.** An optional enemy from the Explorers expansion that masquerades as a chest. The Hungry Mimic is fought before the mini-boss is defeated and the Voracious Mimic afterwards; both are fought as mini-boss encounters without arcs.

**Mini-boss.** The first boss of the standard game, fought on the mini-boss tile.

**Minor action.** An action a character may take during their turn, before or after the major action: *Walk*, *Equip* and *Heroic Action* once each per turn, and *Run* any number of times.

**Move.** A change of node made by a model through the *Walk* or *Run* minor action, a *shift* icon, or an enemy or boss movement icon. The movement after a successful *dodge* and the movement of a *push* are not moves (see §5.2).

**Node.** A circular space on a tile. Every model occupies exactly one node. Nodes are basic, spawn or terrain.

**Node model limit.** At most three non-boss models and one boss model may occupy a node. A model entering a full node forces one occupant to be pushed to a valid node chosen by the players (see §5.3).

**Parry.** A defence available to a character with a shield in the left weapon slot who shares a node with the attacker. The character pays one stamina and rolls dodge dice against the attack's dodge difficulty. Success staggers the attacker and allows a *riposte*; failure deals full damage and staggers the character.

**Phantom.** A summoned ally (summon) or a hostile intruder (invader) from the Phantoms expansion.

**Physical damage.** Damage from an attack marked with the crossed swords icon. Physical damage is reduced by block.

**Pillar token.** A terrain token placed on each of the four pillar nodes of the Asylum Demon mega-boss game board. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into its node until the pillar is destroyed (see §5.5, Pillars; §17.3).

**Poison.** A condition. At the end of its turn a model suffers one damage per poison token it holds, ignoring block and resist, then removes all of its poison tokens.

**Push.** Forced movement of a model to an adjacent node, caused by a push-attack (the hit model moves directly away from the attacker) or a push-move (characters on nodes the enemy enters move away from it). Pushes cost no stamina and may not move a model into a gravestone or chest.

**Range.** The maximum distance between an attacker and its target, counted in nodes along the shortest chain of adjacent nodes. Range 0 is the same node; range 1 is an adjacent node; range ∞ is unlimited. Range is never blocked by terrain or models (see §5.4).

**Repeat.** An icon that causes an entire attack option or behaviour card to be resolved the number of times shown.

**Resist.** The defence rolled against a magical attack, resolved as block is. For enemies, resist is the fixed value subtracted from a magical attack value.

**Rest (major action).** The character removes all stamina cubes from their endurance bar. *Rest* may not be taken in a turn in which the character took the *Run* minor action.

**Resting at the bonfire.** The party action, costing one *spark*, that restores every character, readies all character tokens, removes the hollow cube, revives *lost* characters, and resets every encounter.

**Riposte.** The out-of-turn attack a character makes against an attacker after a successful *parry*, using any attack option of the weapon in the right weapon slot and paying its stamina cost. A *riposte* may not be *parried*.

**Run.** A minor action: move one node for one stamina. It may be repeated. A character who *runs* may not *Rest* that turn.

**Scaled (weapon).** A weapon equipped by a character whose relevant stat exceeds the weapon's highest requirement by five or more. It is marked with a red cube and all its attack options gain +1 attack value. Spells are never scaled.

**Scry.** To reveal one hidden card (the top card of a treasure deck, a face-down encounter card, or the top card of the next boss's behaviour deck) and either return it or send it away (see §5.5, Gravestones).

**Section.** In campaign play, one list of tiles ending in one or more bosses. The shop restocks and a free rest is granted only when a section is completed: when its final boss is defeated or, for a section that ends in a named encounter, when that encounter is *cleared*.

**Shaft.** A weapon icon. Attack options with the shaft icon may not target models on the attacker's own node.

**Shield.** A one-handed weapon card that shows block or resist dice and has no attack option. A shield is a weapon for equipment slots and for two-handed attacks, and a shield for stocking the shop and for parrying (see §4.1).

**Shift.** A weapon or phantom behaviour icon allowing movement of the number of nodes shown, before or after the dice are rolled according to its position on the line. *Shift* movement costs no stamina.

**Shop.** Blacksmith Andre's stock of face-up treasure cards, in tier one and tier two piles. Shops are stocked at setup and restocked only after a boss is defeated (in campaign play, after a section is completed).

**Souls.** The currency of the game, tracked by soul tokens on each character's dashboard. *Souls* are gained by *clearing* encounters and killing invaders, dropped on death, and spent at the *bonfire*. *Souls* may not be transferred between characters.

**Spark.** The *bonfire*'s charges, tracked on the spark dial. The characters begin with six minus the number of characters, gain one for each boss defeated, and spend one each time they rest at the *bonfire*. In campaign play the dial's maximum is one more than its starting value and a *spark* may be bought back for *souls* (see §18.3).

**Spawn node.** A node on which enemy models are placed at the start of an encounter. Boss tiles have a boss spawn node.

**Spell.** An equipment card or upgrade card whose art shows a scroll: a pyromancy, a sorcery, or a miracle. A character may use at most one spell attack option per turn, and spells are never scaled or two-handed (see §4.3).

**Stagger.** A condition. A staggered model subtracts one from its attack, block, resist and dodge totals. The token is removed at the end of the model's next complete turn. A character who must place a stamina cube with no space available is staggered instead.

**Stamina cube.** A black cube. Each point of stamina a character spends places one stamina cube on the endurance bar, filling from the left.

**Starting equipment.** The three or four equipment cards a character begins with. Starting equipment may not be sold.

**Stat cube.** A white cube that marks a character's *stat tier* on a stat progression track (see §3.3).

**Stat requirement.** One of the four values (STR, DEX, INT, FTH) printed on an equipment card or upgrade card. A character may equip a card only if each of their stats equals or exceeds the matching requirement of the card and of every upgrade attached to it (see §4.1).

**Stat tier.** A character's position on each stat progression track: the base tier, or stat tier one, two, three, or four. It is marked with a *stat cube* or, at stat tier four, with a red cube in the stat tier three space. A stat at stat tier four has the value 40 or the stat tier three value plus five, whichever is higher. Tiers are bought in sequence from the Firekeeper (see §3.3, §11.3).

**Strafe card.** A card from the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion drawn when a strafe icon is resolved; it shows the nodes attacked and the node on which Kalameet lands.

**Summon.** A friendly phantom that an *embered* character may call at the fog gate before a boss encounter by paying two *souls* per drawing character. In a boss encounter the summon acts after each character's turn. It leaves play when its health reaches zero or the encounter ends.

**Target.** The model an attack is directed at. Enemies never target enemies; characters target characters only in player versus player.

**Terrain.** Gravestones, barrels, chests, and pillars, placed on terrain nodes, and trap tokens, dealt to basic nodes. Terrain stays on its nodes when encounters are reset (see §5.5).

**Terrain node.** A node on which a terrain token or model is placed at the start of an encounter, as the encounter card directs. A terrain node that holds no terrain is used as a basic node (see §5.1).

**Threat.** The value that fixes turn order, printed on the character dashboard as Taunt Level and on enemy and phantom data cards. Enemies act in descending threat before the characters, who then act in descending threat. The character with the highest threat takes the aggro token at the start of each encounter (see §3.1, §7.5).

**Tier one treasure.** The treasure deck that holds common and class-specific treasure with every stat requirement below 25. Encounter rewards and chests draw from tier one until the mini-boss is defeated (in campaign play, until the *bonfire ascetic* is burnt) (see §6.2).

**Tier two treasure.** The treasure deck that holds every treasure card with a stat requirement of 25 or more, the transposed treasure and the five legendary cards. Encounter rewards and chests draw from tier two once the mini-boss is defeated (in campaign play, once the *bonfire ascetic* is burnt) (see §6.2).

**Titanite.** A weapon upgrade (Titanite Shard, Titanite Chunk, Titanite Slab, or Titanite Scale) that is fused permanently to the weapon it is attached to. When that weapon is sold, the Titanite is returned with it to the bottom of the treasure deck of the weapon's tier (see §4.2).

**Track behaviour card.** A numbered card from the Executioner's Chariot expansion used, in fixed order, for the Chariot's turns before it heats up.

**Transposed treasure.** A class treasure card marked with a blue light. All transposed treasure is tier two.

**Transposition.** In campaign play, exchanging one boss treasure card and four *souls* for one of the five face-up legendary treasure cards while resting at the *bonfire* after the first section's final boss.

**Trap token.** A token, blank or showing a dodge difficulty and a damage value, dealt face down to the basic nodes of a trapped encounter. It is turned face up when a character moves into or is pushed onto its node, and each character there must suffer the damage or *dodge* it; enemies neither trigger traps nor suffer them (see §5.5, Traps).

**Treasure card.** Any card drawn from a treasure deck or bought from Blacksmith Andre's shop: an equipment card or an ember card (see §2.5, §4.1).

**Turn.** One model's activation. Enemies take their turns, then characters take theirs; in boss encounters the boss takes a turn after each character.

**Two-handed.** A weapon bearing the two-handed icon, or a one-handed weapon equipped with the other weapon slot empty. Two-handed attacks may reroll one die. Spells are never two-handed.

**Upgrade card.** A card that enhances a weapon or armour card. Blacksmith Andre attaches it beneath the card, up to the card's upgrade slots, and the character must meet its stat requirements to equip the card. Upgrade spells are instead attached as the *Attack* major action during an encounter and return to the inventory afterwards (see §4.2).

**Upgrade slot.** A space shown by the upgrade slots icon on a weapon or armour card; a card has zero, one, or two. Each attached upgrade card other than an upgrade spell occupies one slot (see §4.2).

**Upgrade spell.** An upgrade card whose art shows a scroll. It is attached to a weapon or armour card equipped to the same character as the *Attack* major action during an encounter, occupies no upgrade slot, and returns to the inventory when the encounter ends (see §4.2).

**Valid node.** A node that a model may enter by a given form of movement: an adjacent node not separated from the model's node by a wall and holding no terrain that blocks that form of entry. A node at its node model limit is a valid node (see §5.2).

**Walk.** A minor action, once per turn: move one node for no stamina.

**Weak arc.** A boss arc shown in red on the most recently resolved behaviour card. A character attacking a boss from a weak arc adds one black die.

**Weapon.** An equipment card that bears the one-handed or two-handed weapon icon and whose art does not show a scroll. Shields, catalysts, bows, crossbows, and throwable weapons are weapons (see §4.1).

**Weapon slot.** One of the two dashboard slots, the left weapon slot and the right weapon slot, that each hold one equipped weapon card. A two-handed weapon requires the other weapon slot to be empty (see §3.2).

**Wind Up.** A behaviour icon. When it is resolved, each character may spend one stamina to move to an adjacent node; the movement counts as a *Run*.

**Wound token.** A token placed on an enemy's base for each point of damage it suffers. An enemy with wound tokens equal to or exceeding its scaled health is killed. Bosses use a health dial instead.
