# 21. Glossary

Terms set in *italics* in this book are listed here with the section that gives their rule.

**Aggro.** The token that marks one character as the target of every enemy movement and attack that shows the skull icon (see §7.5, §9.2).

**Ambush card.** An Explorers card, face down beside an encounter tile, that shows whether the tile's chest holds treasure or a mimic (see §13).

**Arc.** One of the four quarters of a boss's base, front, left, right or back, and of the tile around it (see §15.6).

**Area-of-effect.** An icon on an attack option that makes the attack target every enemy on one node within range (see §10.3).

**Back-up slot.** The dashboard slot for treasure cards that are kept at hand during an encounter but are not equipped (see §3.2).

**Barrel.** A terrain piece that no model walks or runs onto, and that is destroyed by a dodge or a push (see §5.5).

**Basic node.** The most common type of node, a plain one that is neither a spawn node nor a terrain node (see §5.1).

**Behaviour card.** A card from a boss's, mimic's, invader's or summon's deck, whose icons say what that model does on one turn (see §15.5).

**Bleed.** A condition whose token lies on a model until a second bleed token is due there; the model then suffers damage and the token is removed (see §8.5).

**Block.** The defence against physical attacks: dice on a character's equipped cards, or a value on a data card (see §9.4).

**Bonfire.** The safe tile where the party starts the game and rests, and where the Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre wait (see §11.1).

**Bonfire ascetic.** A purchase made once in a campaign, during a rest, that opens tier two treasure and strengthens non-boss enemy attacks (see §18.7).

**Boss treasure.** A treasure card that belongs to one mini-boss, main-boss or mega-boss, marked with that boss's silhouette over an orange light (see §6.2).

**Calamity.** A Black Dragon Kalameet condition that weakens a model's block, resist and dodge rolls until the encounter ends (see §8.5).

**Chest.** A terrain piece that no model may enter, with a treasure card under it for clearing the encounter (see §5.5).

**Class treasure.** A treasure card marked with a small white class emblem alone; every such card belongs to tier one (see §6.2).

**Cleared.** Said of an encounter that the party has won by killing every enemy model on the tile (see §7.5).

**Common treasure.** A treasure card with no mark of another type, sorted into tier one or tier two by its stat requirements (see §6.2).

**Condition.** A lasting effect of an attack, shown by a token on a character's dashboard or an enemy's base (see §8.5).

**Corrosion.** A condition of the Gaping Dragon expansion that weakens a character's block rolls until that character next takes damage (see §8.5).

**Crystal lizard.** An Iron Keep enemy that never attacks, flees the characters and gives Titanite to the character who kills it (see §14).

**Data card.** The card of an enemy, boss, invader or summon that gives its threat, defences, health and other printed values (see §8.2).

**Dodge.** A defence that costs stamina: dodge dice are rolled against a dodge difficulty to avoid an attack altogether (see §9.5).

**Dodge difficulty.** The number on a data card, behaviour card or trap token that a dodge or parry roll must reach (see §9.5).

**Drink estus.** The major action by which a character flips a ready estus flask token to remove their damage cubes (see §10.1).

**Ember.** A card, bought only in the tier one shop, discarded to become embered; also the token marking an embered character (see §4.4).

**Embered.** Said of a character who holds an ember token, gained by an ember card or by a boss's defeat (see §7.3).

**Encounter card.** A card that shows the level, enemies, terrain and traps of one encounter, dealt face down to an encounter tile (see §6.5).

**Endurance bar.** The row of ten spaces on a dashboard that holds a character's black stamina cubes and red damage cubes (see §3.4).

**Entry node.** A basic node along the wall the characters come through, where they are placed when they enter the tile (see §5.1).

**Equipped.** Said of a card in a character's armour slot or a weapon slot, and of no other card (see §3.2).

**Estus flask.** The character token, with a ready side and a used side, that is flipped when the character drinks estus (see §3.5).

**Event card.** A card of the Events Expansion II, dealt beside an encounter card and revealed when the party enters its tile (see §7.5).

**Failed.** Said of an encounter that the party has lost because two characters have died in it, or its last character (see §7.5).

**Fog gate.** The token at a boss tile's doorway, which becomes a shortcut from the bonfire once the party has passed it (see §15.1).

**Frostbite.** A condition that stops a character recovering stamina and slows an enemy's movement, until the end of the model's turn (see §8.5).

**Gravestone.** A terrain piece no model may enter, which lets the party inspect a face-down card once its encounter is cleared (see §5.5).

**Heat-up.** The moment a boss's health first falls to its heat-up point or below, when a heat-up card is shuffled into its behaviour deck (see §15.5).

**Heroic action.** The ability printed on a character's dashboard, used by flipping the heroic action token to its used side (see §3.5).

**Hollow.** Said of a character who has died and returned weaker, with two hollow cubes filling spaces of the endurance bar (see §7.2).

**Invader.** A hostile phantom of the Phantoms expansion that joins one encounter and takes a turn after each character's turn (see §12.2).

**Inventory.** The stack of face-down treasure cards a character keeps beside the dashboard, closed for the length of every encounter (see §3.2).

**Leap.** A movement that places an enemy or a boss directly on its target's node, however far away (see §9.2, §16.2).

**Legendary treasure.** A treasure card of the rarest type, marked with an orange light under a treasure chest at its top right (see §6.2).

**Lost.** Said of a hollow character who has died again and stays out of play until the party next rests (see §7.2).

**Luck.** The character token, with a ready and a used side, flipped to reroll dice of one attack or defence roll (see §3.5).

**Main-boss.** The boss fought after the mini-boss, on both boss tiles; the final boss unless the party chose a mega-boss (see §7.1, §15.3).

**Major action.** The single main action of a character's turn, such as an attack, a rest, drinking estus or using an ember (see §10.1).

**Mega-boss.** A boss with a game board of its own, fought last if the party chose one at setup (see §7.1, §15.3).

**Mimic.** An Explorers enemy that may hide in a chest and is fought as a mini-boss when the chest is opened (see §13).

**Mini-boss.** The first boss the party faces, fought on the single mini-boss tile at the end of the first road (see §15.3).

**Minor action.** An action of a character's turn that may be taken beside the major action: walk, run, equip or heroic action (see §10.1).

**Node.** A circular symbol printed on a tile, where models stand and by which movement and range are counted (see §5.1).

**Node model limit.** The greatest number of models that may share one node; a model entering a full node pushes another off (see §5.3).

**Parry.** A defence rolled as a dodge against an attacker on the character's node: success staggers the attacker, failure the character (see §9.6).

**Phantom.** A summon or an invader of the Phantoms expansion: a friendly or hostile model that joins one encounter (see §12).

**Poison.** A condition whose tokens stack on a model and deal damage at the end of each of its turns (see §8.5).

**Push.** A forced movement of a model from its node to an adjacent node, which costs no stamina (see §8.4).

**Push-attack.** An attack marked with the push icon, which pushes each model it hits away from the attacker (see §8.4).

**Push-move.** A movement marked with the push icon, by which an enemy or boss pushes characters out of its way (see §8.4).

**Range.** The greatest distance, counted in nodes from one model to another, at which an attack or effect reaches (see §5.4).

**Repeat.** An icon that has an attack option, or an enemy's or boss's whole behaviour, resolved the number of times shown (see §9.1, §10.3, §16.3).

**Reset.** To ready a cleared or failed encounter to be fought again, as every rest does; also said of that encounter (see §11.5).

**Resist.** The defence against magical attacks: dice on a character's equipped cards, or a value on a data card (see §9.4).

**Rest.** The party's recovery at the bonfire, which costs a spark; also the major action that removes a character's stamina cubes (see §11.2, §10.1).

**Riposte.** The attack a character may make at once, out of turn, against an attacker whose attack they have parried (see §9.6).

**Run.** A minor action that moves a character one node for each stamina spent, beyond the free walk (see §10.1).

**Section.** One stage of a campaign: its encounters, listed by level in order from the bonfire, and any boss it holds (see §18.1).

**Shaft.** An icon on an attack option that forbids the attack to target a model on the attacker's own node (see §10.3).

**Shift.** An icon on an attack option that lets the character move, at no stamina cost, as part of the attack (see §10.3).

**Shop.** The cards Blacksmith Andre sells, laid face up beside the bonfire tile as tier one and tier two stock (see §11.4).

**Souls.** The currency, tracked in soul tokens on each dashboard, that characters earn from encounters and bosses and then spend (see §7.6).

**Spark.** A unit of the party's shared supply, counted on the spark dial, that is spent to rest at the bonfire (see §7.4).

**Spawn node.** A node marked with a spawn symbol, where an enemy or boss is placed at the start of an encounter (see §5.1).

**Spell.** An equipment or upgrade card with a scroll in its art: a pyromancy, a sorcery or a miracle (see §4.3).

**Stagger.** A condition that weakens a model's attack, block, resist and dodge until the end of the model's turn (see §8.5).

**Stamina.** The effort a character spends to run, dodge and attack, marked by black cubes on the endurance bar (see §3.4).

**Starting equipment.** The equipment cards bearing a character's icon, with which that character begins the game; they join neither treasure deck (see §6.2).

**Stat scaling.** The bonus to a weapon's attack value for a character whose stat far exceeds the weapon's highest requirement (see §10.5).

**Summon.** A friendly phantom that an embered character may call at the fog gate to fight a boss beside the party (see §12.1).

**Terrain node.** A node marked with a terrain symbol, where terrain is placed at the start of an encounter (see §5.1).

**Threat.** The value on a dashboard or data card that orders enemy turns and decides who first holds the aggro token (see §7.5).

**Tier one.** The lower of the two grades of treasure deck and shop, in use from the start of the game (see §6.2).

**Tier two.** The higher of the two grades of treasure deck and shop, opened in the standard game by the mini-boss's defeat (see §6.2).

**Titanite.** An upgrade card with Titanite in its name, such as a Titanite Shard, fused into the weapon that holds it (see §4.2).

**Transposed treasure.** A treasure card marked with a blue light and a white class emblem; every such card belongs to tier two (see §6.2).

**Transposition.** The campaign exchange, made during a rest, of a boss treasure card and souls for a legendary treasure card (see §18.8).

**Trap.** A token dealt face down to a node, blank or showing damage that strikes when a character enters the node (see §5.5).

**Two-handed.** Said of a weapon that needs both hands, or of a one-handed weapon held with the other weapon slot empty (see §10.4).

**Upgrade.** A card, marked with a sword or plate mail, that enhances the weapon or armour card it is fitted to (see §4.2).

**Walk.** The minor action that moves a character one node, to an adjacent node, at no cost in stamina (see §10.1).

**Weak arc.** An arc marked in red on the boss's latest behaviour card; an attack made from it gains a black die (see §15.6).
