# 19. Campaigns

Sixteen roads lead out from the first bonfire. One climbs through the Undead Burg to Anor Londo, another descends into the Abyss, and a third never leaves the arena. This chapter sets out all 16 campaigns, section by section.

Each campaign states the sets it requires and lists the tiles of each *section* in order outward from the *bonfire* tile; a section is laid out and completed as the campaign setup rules state (see §18.1).

## 19.15 Treasures of the Ice Cave

This campaign requires the core set, the Phantoms expansion, and the Vordt of the Boreal Valley expansion. The party may add the Explorers and Characters expansions.

The Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 encounter decks are built only from the *encounter cards* of the core set (see §6.5, Expansion Encounters). Both *Level 4 encounters* are fought on the Vordt of the Boreal Valley expansion's *mega-boss game board* (see §18.1).

The party does not place *invader tokens* during this campaign (see §12.2). Two *encounters* each list two *invaders*. They are fought under the invader rules (see §12.2), with the following changes.

- Both invaders are part of that encounter every time the characters enter it, until both have been killed during one visit. If either invader vanishes, or the encounter is *failed*, both return the next time the characters enter it. An encounter that is *cleared* before both of its invaders have been killed is fought again when the characters next enter it, the Level 4 encounter of Section Three included, and it is not skipped.
- The first invader is placed as the invader rules state, and the second on a node adjacent to it, chosen by the players. Each invader has its own *behaviour deck*, and each takes a turn after each character's turn, in the order the players choose. The health of the second invader is recorded on any *health dial* that is not in use.
- The *souls* and the treasure cards of both invaders are awarded when the second of them is killed, and not before.
- The section is completed only when that encounter is *cleared* and both of its invaders have been killed (see §18.1).

## 19.16 Demons and Dragons

This campaign requires the core set, the Asylum Demon expansion, the Guardian Dragon expansion, the Gaping Dragon expansion, and the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion. The party may add the Explorers and Characters expansions.

The Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 encounter decks are built only from the *encounter cards* of the core set (see §6.5, Expansion Encounters). The Level 4 encounter deck is built from the Level 4 encounter cards of the four mega-boss expansions together. Each *Level 4 encounter* is fought on the *mega-boss game board* of the expansion that supplies its card, and each *mega-boss* on its own game board (see §18.1). The Asylum Demon is fought on the mega-boss side of the Asylum Demon expansion's game board (see §17.3).

The following rules take the place of the soul rewards of campaign play (see §18.5).

- An *encounter* of Level 1, 2, 3, or 4 awards no *souls*, whether it is *cleared* for the first time or again. It awards its *treasure cards* as in any campaign (see §18.5). Bosses and *invaders* award souls as in the standard game (see §7.6).
- When a Level 4 encounter is cleared, the party gains one *spark*, up to the dial's maximum (see §18.3).
- A cleared Level 4 encounter and a defeated mega-boss are not fought again when the party rests (see §18.1).
- At the start of each *section*, before the characters leave the *bonfire* tile, each character may raise stats at no cost, as the following table shows (see §3.3).

| Section | *Stat tiers* granted to each character |
|---|---|
| One | Two stats from the base tier to stat tier one, then one stat from stat tier one to stat tier two |
| Two | Two stats from the base tier to stat tier one, then one stat from stat tier one to stat tier two |
| Three | One stat from stat tier one to stat tier two, then one stat from stat tier two to stat tier three |
| Four | One stat from stat tier one to stat tier two, then one stat from stat tier two to stat tier three |
| Five | Two stats from stat tier two to stat tier three |
