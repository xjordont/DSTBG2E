# 19. Campaigns

Twelve roads lead out from the first bonfire. One climbs through the Undead Burg to Anor Londo, another descends into the Abyss, and a third never leaves the arena. This chapter sets out all twelve campaigns, section by section.

Each campaign states the sets it requires and lists the tiles of each *section* in order outward from the *bonfire* tile; a section is laid out and completed as the campaign setup rules state (see §18.1).

## 19.1 The First Journey

This campaign requires the core set. The party may add the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion to play Section Four.

The encounter decks of Sections One to Three are built only from the *encounter cards* of the core set (see §6.5, Expansion Encounters).

### Section Four: Bathed in Flame

1. Bonfire tile
2. Level 3 encounter
3. Level 3 encounter
4. *Level 4 encounter*
5. Black Dragon Kalameet (*mega-boss*)

Section Four is an optional rule. It requires the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion. The party decides during setup whether Section Four is in play.

The *mega-boss game board* is placed with its Level 4 encounter side face up and with one of its *doorways* aligned with a doorway of the second Level 3 encounter tile, not with a doorway of the bonfire tile (see §18.1). When the Level 4 encounter is *cleared*, the party flips the game board to its mega-boss side (see §6.7, The Mega-Boss Encounter). A cleared Level 4 encounter does not *reset* when the characters rest at the bonfire (see §6.7, Level 4 Encounters).

The Level 3 encounter decks of Section Four are built from the encounter cards of the core set. If the party owns the Darkroot expansion, they are built from the encounter cards of that expansion instead.

## 19.9 The Undead Colosseum

This campaign requires the core set, the Darkroot expansion, the Explorers expansion, the Vordt of the Boreal Valley expansion, and the Executioner's Chariot expansion. The party may add the Characters expansion.

The Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 encounter decks are built from the *encounter cards* of the core set, the Darkroot expansion, and the Explorers expansion together. The *Level 4 encounter* deck is built from the Level 4 encounter cards of the Vordt of the Boreal Valley expansion and the Executioner's Chariot expansion together. The six named *encounters* are encounter cards of the Executioner's Chariot expansion.

When the characters rest at the *bonfire*, a *cleared* encounter does not *reset*, and its encounter card stays face up. A cleared encounter is not fought again, and the party passes its tile without paying the cost of skipping it (see §7.5, Encounter Start). A *failed* encounter resets (see §11.5). When an encounter is cleared, every character gains the campaign soul reward for its level and two further *souls* (see §18.5).
