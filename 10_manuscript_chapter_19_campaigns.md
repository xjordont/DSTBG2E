# 19. Campaigns

A name in brackets after a level is the *encounter card* placed on that tile, or the *invader* that joins it where the campaign says so. For the Twin Gargoyles, see §17.1.

## 19.9 The Undead Colosseum

Needs the core set and the Darkroot, Explorers, Vordt of the Boreal Valley and Executioner's Chariot expansions.

- The Level 1 to 3 encounter decks combine the core set, Darkroot and Explorers. The Level 4 deck combines the Level 4 cards of both mega-boss expansions.
- The six named encounters are Executioner's Chariot *encounter cards*.
- A *cleared* encounter does not *reset*: it is not fought again, and the party passes its tile at no cost.
- Each cleared encounter pays every character two *souls* more than the reward for its level.

- When Section One ends, each character gains 16 *souls* on top of the boss reward.

## 19.15 Treasures of the Ice Cave

Needs the core set and the Phantoms and Vordt of the Boreal Valley expansions. The Level 1 to 3 encounter decks use the core set alone. No invader tokens are placed. Two encounters each have two *invaders*.

- Both invaders join the encounter every time the party enters it, until both are killed in one visit.
- An encounter *cleared* before both are killed is fought again, the Level 4 encounter included, and cannot be skipped.
- The second invader is placed on a node adjacent to the first, chosen by the players. Each has its own behaviour deck and takes a turn after each character's turn, in the order the players choose. A spare health dial records the second's health.
- The *souls* and treasure cards of both are given when the second is killed.
- The section ends when the encounter is cleared with both invaders killed.

| Section | Encounters | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| One: Glacial Entrance | 1, 1, 2, 2 (Melinda the Butcher and Oliver the Collector) | None |
| Two: Arctic Pass | 2, 2, 2, 3 | Boreal Outrider Knight |
| Three: Frozen Corridor | 2, 3, 3, 4 (Guarded Path, with Marvelous Chester and Paladin Leeroy) | None |
| Four: The Boreal Sanctuary | 3, 3, 4 (The Dog's Domain) | Vordt of the Boreal Valley |

## 19.16 Demons and Dragons

Needs the core set and the Asylum Demon, Guardian Dragon, Gaping Dragon and Black Dragon Kalameet expansions. The Level 1 to 3 encounter decks use the core set alone. The Level 4 deck combines the Level 4 cards of the four expansions. Each Level 4 encounter is fought on the game board its card belongs to, and each *mega-boss* on its own.

- Encounters pay no *souls*. Bosses and *invaders* pay as in any campaign (see §18.5).
- Clearing a Level 4 encounter gives one *spark*, never above the maximum.
- A *cleared* Level 4 encounter and a defeated mega-boss are not fought again.
- Section Five is fought in the order Level 4 encounter, Stray Demon, Level 4 encounter, Black Dragon Kalameet. The Stray Demon's defeat does not end the section.
- At the start of each section, before leaving the *bonfire* tile, each character raises stats at no cost:
  - Sections One and Two: two stats from the base tier to stat tier one, then one from stat tier one to two.
  - Sections Three and Four: one stat from stat tier one to two, then one from stat tier two to three.
  - Section Five: two stats from stat tier two to three.

| Section | Encounters | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| One: The Dungeon's Gates | 1, 1, 1, 2 | Asylum Demon |
| Two: The Bloody Road | 2, 2, 2, 2 | Titanite Demon |
| Three: The Peak of Peril | 2, 3, 3, 4 | Guardian Dragon |
| Four: The Maw of Death | 3, 3, 4 | Gaping Dragon |
| Five: The Final Conflict | 4, then 4 | Stray Demon, then Black Dragon Kalameet |
