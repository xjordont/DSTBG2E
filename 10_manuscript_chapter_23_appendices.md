# Appendix A. Weapon Stat Revisions

The *stat requirements* in this appendix replace those printed on the *weapon* cards listed. A weapon not listed keeps its printed requirements. The revised requirements decide the treasure tier of a listed weapon: a requirement of 25 or more in any stat makes the card *tier two treasure*, and a card whose requirements are all below 25 is *tier one treasure* unless its treasure type places it elsewhere (see §6.2). A listed weapon is *scaled* against the highest requirement given here, and the stat of that requirement is its *relevant stat* (see §10.5).

Every listed weapon has 0 INT and 0 FTH. A weapon shown with 0 STR or 0 DEX has no requirement in that stat, and every character meets it.

*Example.* The Brigand Axe's revised requirements are 19 STR and 16 DEX, so strength is its *relevant stat*. The Knight has 24 STR and 20 DEX, meets both requirements, and equips the Brigand Axe. The Knight's strength exceeds 19 by five, so the Brigand Axe is *scaled*, and each of its *attack options* adds one to its *attack value*.

## Treasure Type

The Tier column follows from the revised *stat requirements* alone. A listed card that is *transposed treasure* or *legendary treasure* is *tier two treasure* whatever its requirements (see §6.2). A listed card that is *boss treasure* enters neither treasure deck and is placed with its boss (see §6.2).

### Titanite Replacement

A Titanite Chunk may be attached only to a weapon that has a Titanite Shard attached. A Titanite Slab may be attached only to a weapon that has a Titanite Chunk attached. Blacksmith Andre makes the replacement while the characters rest at the bonfire (see §11.4).

The Titanite Chunk or Titanite Slab occupies the *upgrade slot* of the card it replaces. A replaced *Titanite* card does not remain fused to the weapon (see §4.2, Titanite). A replaced Titanite Shard is returned to the bottom of the tier one treasure deck, and a replaced Titanite Chunk is returned to the bottom of the tier two treasure deck.

A character may equip a weapon with a Titanite Chunk or a Titanite Slab attached only if the character has at least 25 STR and at least 25 DEX and meets the stat requirements of the weapon (see §4.2).
