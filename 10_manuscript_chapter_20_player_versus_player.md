# 20. Player versus Player

After each boss is defeated (*mini-boss*, *main-boss* and *mega-boss*), a player versus player (PvP) encounter is fought on the boss tile if the party has two or more characters (see §16.5).

## 20.1 Setup

- Remove all enemy models and *trap* tokens from the boss tile.
- A character who died in the boss encounter is left out of PvP.
- *Summons* and *invaders* are left out of PvP.
- Restore each character as a rest at the *bonfire* restores them (see §11.2).
- Place each character's model on a corner *node* of the boss tile, one character to a corner.
- The *aggro* token is not used.

## 20.2 The Fight

- Characters take turns in descending *threat* order. A die roll settles each tie before the first turn.
- A character's turn is the same as in any encounter. Attacks are made against other characters.
- Where a card or a *heroic action* names an enemy, it means another character.
- The attacker's roll is the attack value. The defender *blocks*, *resists*, *dodges* or parries as against an enemy's attack (see §9).
- A *push* or a *condition* on the attack applies to each character it hits.
- The *dodge difficulty* depends on the *stamina* cost of the attack option used:

| Stamina cost | Dodge difficulty |
|---|---|
| 0 | 4 |
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 or 4 | 1 |
| 5 or more | 0 |

- After each character's turn, that character's player places one *trap* token face down on an outermost free *node* of the tile.
- A free node holds no model, token or terrain. The outermost nodes are those along the edges of the tile; once the edges are full, the next ring inwards is used.
- A character who moves or is pushed onto a trap token flips it and must dodge the trap (see §5.5).
- A character who fails to dodge a trap dies at once. The damage on the token is not used.
- A dead character's model is removed from the tile.

## 20.3 Victory

- Dying in PvP carries no penalty:
  - *Souls* are not dropped.
  - The *ember* token is not removed.
  - *Hollow* cubes are not placed.
- The last character alive wins.
- A win after any boss but the final boss gives the winner +1 to the attack value of every attack in later PvP encounters, and nowhere else.
- The winner after the final boss is the champion of the game.
- When the PvP encounter ends, return the *trap* tokens to the supply. The steps that follow the boss's defeat then carry on.
