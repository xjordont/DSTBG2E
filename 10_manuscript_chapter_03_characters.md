# 3. Characters

## 3.1 Character Dashboards

Each character is represented by a model, with their information tracked on a dashboard of the same name.

The dashboard shows everything specific to the character's class:

1. Name
2. Illustration
3. *Heroic action*
4. Equipment slots
5. *Endurance bar*
6. Stat progression track
7. *Threat* {{icon:ast-0059-taunt}}, printed as the taunt level
8. Heroic action token slot
9. *Luck* token slot
10. *Estus flask* token slot
11. *Ember* token slot

## 3.2 Equipment Slots

Each dashboard has four equipment slots: an armour slot, a left weapon slot, a right weapon slot and a *back-up slot*.

- **Armour slot.** One armour card, marked with the armour icon {{icon:ast-0047-armour}}.
- **Weapon slots.** One weapon card each, marked with the one-handed weapon icon {{icon:ast-0043-one-handed-weapon}} or the *two-handed* weapon icon {{icon:ast-0045-two-handed-weapon}}. While a two-handed weapon is in one weapon slot, the other must stay empty unless a card's text says otherwise. While a one-handed weapon is in one weapon slot, the other may hold another one-handed weapon.
- **Back-up slot.** Up to two treasure cards of any type. These cards are at hand during an encounter but are not *equipped*.

Only the cards in the armour slot and the weapon slots are equipped.

A character may also hold any number of treasure cards in their *inventory*, a stack of face-down cards beside the dashboard. Outside an encounter, a character may move cards freely between the inventory and the dashboard. Once an encounter has started, the inventory is closed until the encounter is over.

Outside an encounter, two characters may trade cards between their inventories, one card for one card, if both players agree. Characters cannot exchange cards in any way during an encounter.

## 3.3 Stat Progression Track

The stat progression track shows the character's strength (STR), dexterity (DEX), intelligence (INT) and faith (FTH). Each character has their own array of values, which sets out how their skills grow.

Each stat has a row of four spaces: a base space, then one space each for stat tiers one, two and three. Each space is printed with the value of the stat at that tier.

The value of a stat is the number printed in the space that holds its white stat cube. Each time a stat is raised, its cube moves one space to the right (see §11.3).

## 3.4 Endurance Bar

The *endurance bar* tracks a character's *stamina* and damage. It has ten spaces. The bar begins empty and fills with stamina cubes (black) and damage cubes (red) during play.

- When a character spends stamina, they place that number of stamina cubes on the bar, starting from the leftmost free space.
- When a character suffers damage, they place that number of damage cubes on the bar, starting from the rightmost free space.
- When a character recovers stamina, they remove that number of stamina cubes, starting with the one furthest to the right.
- When a character is healed, they remove that number of damage cubes, starting with the one furthest to the left.

A *hollow* character has two hollow cubes on the two rightmost spaces of the bar (see §7.2). The hollow cubes are white cubes, or any marker that is neither red nor black. They leave a hollow character eight spaces for stamina and damage.

If a character must place a stamina cube and there is no free space, the cube is not placed and the character is *staggered* {{icon:ast-0066-stagger}} instead (see §8.5). A character may never choose to spend more stamina than the bar has room for.

If a character must place a damage cube and there is no free space, the character dies (see §7.2). A character whose bar is exactly full is alive.

## 3.5 Character Tokens

Each character has four tokens: *heroic action*, *luck*, *estus flask* and *ember*.

The heroic action, luck and estus flask tokens each have a ready side and a used side. The ready side has the brighter colours. A token's effect is available only while its ready side shows. The token is flipped to its used side when the character uses the effect.

### Heroic Action

A character may flip their heroic action token to the used side to resolve the heroic action printed on their dashboard. It is a *minor action* unless its text gives another moment (see §10.1). The wording of the heroic action overrides any other rule.

### Luck

A character may flip their luck token to the used side to reroll any or all dice of one attack or defence roll. The dice to be rerolled are chosen at once and rolled together. The new results stand.

### Estus Flask

A character flips their estus flask token to the used side to *drink estus*, the *major action* that heals them (see §10.1).

### Ember

The ember token marks an *embered* character. It is placed on the dashboard when the character becomes embered and returns beside the dashboard when the character stops being embered (see §7.3).
