# 13. Mimics

With the Explorers expansion, any *chest* {{icon:ast-0009-treasure-chest}} may hide a *mimic*. One *ambush card* lies face down beside each encounter tile (see §6.5).

When the last enemy falls in an encounter that holds an unopened chest, the characters stay on their *nodes* and do not yet recover. The encounter is not yet *cleared*, and none of its *souls* or treasure is collected. The party decides whether to open the chest or to leave it for fear of a mimic. If it cannot agree, the character holding the *aggro* token decides.

- **Leave it closed.** The encounter is cleared and the characters recover (see §7.5). The chest stays closed, with its treasure card under it.
- **Open it.** Reveal the tile's ambush card. A chest on a tile with no ambush card holds treasure.

If the ambush card shows treasure, flip the chest to its open side. The party takes the treasure card under it, the encounter is cleared and the characters recover.

If the ambush card shows a mimic, the encounter goes on and the mimic is fought at once as a *mini-boss*.

1. Take the Hungry Mimic's *data card* and *behaviour cards* if the party's chosen mini-boss has not yet been defeated, and the Voracious Mimic's if it has.
2. Shuffle the behaviour cards and deal as many as the deck size {{icon:ast-0027-behaviour-deck-size}} on the data card to form the mimic's behaviour deck. Set the other cards aside.
3. Set the mimic's health dial to its starting health {{icon:ast-0029-starting-health}}.
4. Put the mimic's model in place of the chest.
5. Leave every character on the node they occupied when the last enemy fell, with the damage, *stamina* and *conditions* they carry. A character who died in the encounter stays out of the fight.
6. The mimic takes the first turn.

A mimic follows the rules for a mini-boss (see §15, §16), except that it has no *heat-up* cards: when it heats up, one of its unused behaviour cards, taken at random, is shuffled in instead.

If the party kills the mimic:

- The party takes two treasure cards in place of one: the card under the chest and the top card of the treasure deck of the current tier.
- The encounter is cleared and the characters recover. Killing a mimic is not the defeat of a boss, and nothing that follows a boss's defeat applies (see §16.5).
- The encounter may be fought again like any other, and the mimic does not return.

If the encounter is *failed* against the mimic, the mimic's model is removed, the chest stays closed, and the ambush card stays face up.

A chest left closed, or one whose mimic was not killed, may be opened each time the party later clears that encounter. A mimic met again is set up afresh.
