# BOSS TURNS

## Boss Turn Overview

During a boss encounter, the boss always takes their turn first. Draw the top card from the boss behaviour deck at the beginning of every boss turn. The boss’s actions are then determined by resolving each symbol on the behaviour card from left to right. When the right-most symbol is resolved, the boss’s turn ends. After each boss turn, one character may take their turn, then the boss takes another turn, a second character, and so-on. characters must take an equal number of turns and cannot take a turn until every other character has taken an equal number of turns.

If there are no cards remaining in the behaviour deck at the start of a boss’s turn, flip the discard pile face-down without shuffling the cards to renew the boss behaviour deck. Then, draw the top card of the boss behaviour deck as usual. Players can gain an advantage over a boss by memorising the order in which the boss behaviour cards will appear each time the deck is renewed.

## Boss Movement & Symbols

Bosses abide by the same enemy movement and attack rules previously described with the following exceptions.

When a boss moves towards a target, it first rotates to align the centre of its front arc with the adjacent node closest to the target character. Once facing is resolved, the boss moves forward into the adjacent node it has just centred in its front arc. The boss repeats this for each node of movement instructed by the current behaviour card.

When a boss moves away from a target, it first rotates to align the centre of its back arc with the adjacent node furthest from the target character. Once facing is resolved, the boss moves backwards into the adjacent node it has just centred in its back arc. The boss repeats this for each node of movement instructed by the current behaviour card.

If a boss behaviour card instructs movement towards a target already sharing the same node, the boss remains in the same node but rotates to bring the target model into the front-arc of the boss’s base. The target character model should remain static while the boss model rotates underneath to change facing towards the target.

If a boss moves into a node occupied by a character, the character is placed on the boss’s base in the arc the character was in at the beginning of the boss’s movement. If the character was on a node split between two arcs the player may choose either.

If a boss move behaviour does not indicate a target it moves the number of nodes indicated in the direction shown without rotating to change facing.

If a boss *leaps* into a node occupied by a character, the boss maintains its original facing and the character(s) are pushed into any adjacent node of their choosing unrestricted by the boss arc.

The *turn* symbol indicates the boss turns 90 degrees to the left or right as indicated by the direction of the quarter-turn arrow.

The *rotate* symbol indicates the boss turns 180 degrees to face their rear.

## Boss Attacks

Boss attacks follow the same rules as enemy attacks but can contain additional symbols with more powerful effects. When resolving a boss behaviour attack note the four boss arcs coded as either normal (black), attack (green), or weak (red).

The *area* symbol indicates that the boss is attacking entire arcs instead of an individual character. Bosses do not change facing when they attack and instead deal damage to characters within range that occupy nodes in their attack arcs. If a node is split and only half in an attack arc it is still targeted by that attack. characters targeted by an *area* attack either block*, resist,* or dodge as they normally would.

The *repeat* symbol is similar to the character’s equipment symbol and allows a boss to repeat its entire behaviour card multiple times before ending its turn.

## Boss Aggro

Boss behaviours may indicate their target as the character with the *aggro* token on their dashboard. The *aggro* token is assigned to the character with the highest *threat* value at the beginning of the boss encounter. Similar to basic encounters, the *aggro* token moves to the character who dealt the most damage before the boss's next turn. Because the boss takes a turn after each individual character takes their turn, one can assume any damage dealt by a character will be the most damage prior to the boss’s activation. Therefore, simply move the *aggro* token to a character’s dashboard if they deal any damage to the boss during their turn. If no damage is dealt, the *aggro* token remains stationary for that turn.

## Boss Death

When the boss’s health dial is reduced to zero, the boss is slain and the encounter ends. If this was the final boss (main or mega), the characters are victorious and the players win. If not, determine boss treasures and collect soul rewards as previously described. Then, increase the spark dial by one and give each character an *ember* token to mark them as *embered.* Finally, the characters return to The Bonfire to rest at no spark cost. They may visit the Firekeeper and Andre if they wish.

This is often a reasonable time to end a game session if time is limited. Either today or another day the players may continue on with post-boss setup rules as described earlier in these rules.
