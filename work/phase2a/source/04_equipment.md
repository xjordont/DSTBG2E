# EQUIPMENT

## Equipment Cards

Equipment cards include weapons, spells, shields, armour, and items which the character equips to improve their combat capabilities. Equipment cards with either a one or two-handed weapon icon are considered weapons unless the card depicts a spell as indicated by a picture of a scroll with an overlying spell icon in the centre of the card. Equipment cards are considered armour if they have the armour icon.

1.  Equipment name.

2.  Equipment type (common, class-specific, transposed, legendary, boss).

3.  Equipment slot (one-handed weapon, two-handed weapon, or armour).

4.  Icon to indicate what core or expansion set the card is from.

5.  Attack range.

6.  Minimum stat requirements to equip (STR, DEX, INT, FTH)

7.  *Attack options*

8.  *Block* value.

9.  *Resist* value.

10. *Dodge* value.

11. Upgrade slots available.

Each equipment card has four listed stat requirements which indicate what stat values on a character's dashboard are required to equip the item.

An equipment card’s actions represent an attack or special effect used during an encounter. Each line on the card represents a separate attack option that can be used as a character's main action. The specifics of using equipment cards offensively is explained in detail later on in the section title “**Character Attacks**”.

An equipment card provides defensive capabilities including block, resist, and dodge. The specifics of using equipment cards defensively is explained in detail later on in the section titled “**Enemy Attacks**”.

An equipment card may have a number of upgrade slots to permit improving the equipment with upgrade cards at Blacksmith Andre which is described later in more detail.

Some equipment cards have dice modifiers that add a fixed value to the die roll or subtract a fixed value from the die roll.

Players should include all equipment cards from expansions available in their games to increase variety.

## Upgrades

Upgrade cards are used to enhance equipment. There are two types of upgrade cards: those marked with a sword which may only be used to upgrade weapons, and those marked with play mail, which may only be used up grade armour. Upgrades can only be added to or removed from equipment by Blacksmith Andre while the characters are resting at The Bonfire. Upgrades are limited to the number of upgrades permitted by a piece of equipment’s upgrade limit. For example, a character could attach up to two weapon upgrade cards to a weapon card with an upgrade limit of two.

Upgrade cards with a picture of a magic scroll are considered spells and are not attached to equipment permanently by Andre. Instead, upgrade spells are attached to a character's equipment as a main action similar to casting an attack spell. Upgrade spells must be equipped to a character’s weapon slot or catalyst to be transferred to a weapon or armour card equipped to the same character. The upgrade spell card is attached to the target equipment for the rest of the encounter but returns to the character’s inventory after the encounter ends.

Aside from Titanite, all weapons and armour upgrade cards are returned to a character's inventory when the equipment card they are attached to is sold. Unlike the gems and relics of other upgrade cards, Titanite upgrade cards are fused permanently into the weapon to which they are attached. Therefore, any upgrade card, including the word Titanate (i.e., Titanite Shard) must be returned to the bottom of the tier one treasure deck along with the weapon it is attached to when sold.

## Spells

### Spells

- Spells are a type of equipment or upgrade card defined by the presence of a scroll as part of the equipment card’s art, usually with a coloured magic effect in the foreground.

- A character is always limited to one spell *attack option* per turn.

- Spells cannot be affected by scaling and do not receive scaling bonuses.

- Spells cannot be two-handed and do not receive two-handed bonuses.

- Spells are considered either Pyromancy, Sorcery, or Miracles.

  - Pyromancies display red or purple magic effects and have both INT and FTH requirements.

  - Sorceries display blue or black magic effects and have only INT requirements.

  - Miracles display yellow or pink magic effects and have only FTH requirements.

<!-- -->

- Spells stored within their respective *catalyst* may use their *attack options* as if equipped.

  - Pyromancer Flames may store three pyromancies

  - Staves and Catalysts may store three sorceries.

  - Talismans and Chimes may store three miracles.

  - Spell cards are considered stored in the *catalyst* card they are attached to.

  - Spells may be freely transferred between a characters’ catalyst and their inventory between encounters.

## Embers

*Ember* cards may only be purchased from the tier one shop and are in limited supply. Ember cards may be stored in a player’s inventory, but must be equipped to the backup slot to be available for use during an encounter. Discarding an *ember* card permits the character to place an *ember* token on their character dashboard with the aforementioned effects.
