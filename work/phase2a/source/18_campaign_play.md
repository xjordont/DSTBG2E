#  CAMPAIGN PLAY

## Setup

The setup for a campaign is largely the same as the basic game. However, each section of a scenario has varying numbers of exploration tiles, may contain multiple bosses, and may have encounters with special rules. Campaigns provide a more challenging and longer play experience suitable for players who have played the basic game at least a few times..

Players might expect to meet over multiple play sessions to complete a campaign. A single section of a campaign might serve as a convenient amount of content to cover in a session. While campaigns can vary in length, the included campaigns are all three sections long.

When starting a campaign, the players must first choose one of the available campaigns. This rule-set includes three campaigns which require only the components available in the core set. Many of the game’s expansions also add additional campaigns for selection.

## The Fog Gate

The fog gate does not provide a short-cut to the boss encounter in campaign play as it does in the basic game. The fog gate token is purely aesthetic in campaign play.

## Sparks

The characters start a campaign with the same number of sparks used in the basic game (six minus the number of characters). The starting number of sparks also serves as the maximum number of sparks.

Each time a mini-boss, main-boss, or mega-boss is defeated, the characters gain one *spark* as in the rules for the basic game. However, characters no longer rest at the *bonfire* for free after defeating a boss except for the final boss of each section. Sparks cannot be purchased or gained otherwise. The players lose the campaign if all characters are lost to the hollow without a *spark* to revive them.

## Adding & Dropping Players

During campaign play, sometimes a new player would like to join the characters part way through or an existing player is required to leave. New players can join a campaign that is in progress at the cost of one spark each. The new player begins the game with starting character stats and equipment. The new maximum number of sparks is based on the new number of characters. If a player character leaves the characters, the characters gain one spark, the character’s equipment is added to the inventory, and the maximum number of sparks is changed to reflect the new number of characters.

## Encounter Soul Rewards

- Encounter *soul* rewards are reduced by one compared to the basic game.

- Encounters that have been *cleared* may be either skipped for stamina cost or repeated for *souls* rewards but not treasure rewards, just as in the basic game.

## Encounter Treasure Rewards

- Encounters still reward a single treasure card to each character after the first time an encounter is *cleared*, as in the basic rules.

- All encounters and chests reward tier one treasures initially, even after a mini-boss is defeated.

- All encounters and chests reward tier two treasures after tier two treasure deck is unlocked by paying 16 *souls* to burn bonfire ascetic.

## The Bonfire Tile

The rules for returning to the *bonfire* are essentially unchanged from the basic rules. Characters may return to the *bonfire* to revive, rest, improve their stats at the Firekeeper, and visit Blacksmith Andre (attach upgrades, sell treasure cards, and/or purchase treasure from Andre's Shop). The cost to level up stats at the Firekeeper is increased in Campaign play to slow the characters’ progression to scale better across a longer journey. Characters may now also transpose boss items and burn bonfire ascetic.

### Blacksmith Andre's Shop

Andre's Shop functions similarly to the basic rules with some changes to how the shop is stocked to better suit the progression of the campaign. Use the basic rules for Andre's Shop with the following modifications.

- Tier one and two shops are only restocked after each section is completed. This means that if a section has multiple bosses, only defeat of the section's final encounter will restock the shop.

- Tier two treasures are only available after the characters pay 16 *souls collectively* to burn bonfire ascetic and unlock tier two treasures.

- Never add legendary or boss treasure to either tier of treasure deck.

- Boss treasure is only obtained by defeating bosses.

- Legendary Treasures are now only available through transposition.

### Bonfire Ascetic

In campaign play, characters do not gain access to tier two treasures freely after defeating a boss. They must decide at what point their need for better equipment outweighs the cost of increased difficulty. Characters may pay a communal one time fee to burn the bonfire ascetic to gain access to tier two treasures. A total of sixteen souls may be paid from any combination of character contributions while resting at the bonfire. Once the bonfire ascetic has been burnt, characters immediately populate the tier two shop. Thereafter, any treasure cards earned through encounters or opening chests come from the tier two treasure deck. The tier one shop does not restock when burning bonfire ascetic; the characters must defeat a boss to restock the shop(s).

However, burning the bonfire ascetic invites stronger enemies. After the bonfire ascetic has been burnt, all non-boss enemies increase their attack damage by one for the remainder of the campaign. This effect is cumulative with stat scaling for character count. This does not affect invaders, mini-bosses, main-bosses, or mega-bosses.

### Transposition

After the final boss of the first section is defeated, characters may elect to transpose boss treasure to obtain legendary treasure. Transposition is only available after the characters spend a *spark* to rest at the *bonfire*.

First, players gather all available legendary treasure cards and shuffle them to create the legendary treasure deck. Second, five legendary treasure cards are dealt face-up near the *bonfire* tile. Third, characters may now remove a boss treasure in their inventory from the campaign, pay four *souls*, and obtain any of the five available legendary treasure cards. Fourth, deal another legendary treasure card face-up by the *bonfire* tile to keep five transposition options available.
