# PLAYER VS PLAYER

- Decide during setup if PvP rules will be used or not.

- After each boss (mini, main, and mega) a PvP encounter will occur.

- Remove all enemy models and traps from the boss encounter board.

- Restore each character as if they rested at the *bonfire*.

- Place each character in a corner of the boss encounter board.

- Dead characters are excluded from PvP.

- Phantoms (Summons and Invaders) are excluded from PvP.

- *Aggro* token is not used.

- *First* *turn* token is given to the character with the highest threat value.

- Characters take turns in descending threat order.

- Character turn structure is unchanged from basic rules.

- Players place a trap token on an outermost available node after each turn.

- Characters that move onto a trap token may attempt to *dodge*.

- Characters that fail to *dodge* a trap immediately die.

- Dying in PvP does not cause any penalty.

  - Souls are not dropped.

  - Ember is not removed.

  - Hollow cubes are not placed.

- The last character alive is awarded victory.

- Victory on non-final bosses provides +1 attack damage in future PvP only.

- Victory on the final boss determines the ultimate champion.

- Dodge difficulty is based on an the attacker attack option*’s* stamina cost:

  - \[0\] -\> 4

  - \[1\] -\> 3

  - \[2\] -\> 2

  - \[3\] -\> 1

  - \[4\] -\> 1

  - \[5+\] -\> 0
