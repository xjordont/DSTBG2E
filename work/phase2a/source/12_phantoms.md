# SUMMONS

## Phantoms

*Embered* characters may summon a random friendly phantom at the fog gate prior to encountering a mini-boss or main-boss.

1.  *Embered* characters may elect to pay two *souls* to draw a phantom card.

2.  Draw a random phantom card of appropriate level.

    - Standard phantoms for mini-bosses

    - Advanced phantoms for main-bosses or mega-bosses

3.  Players must agree on only one of the drawn phantoms to summon.

4.  Shuffle all of the phantom’s behaviour cards to form the behaviour deck.

5.  Place the chosen phantom's model on the boss tile’s entrance node after the characters have placed their character models on their starting node.

6.  Phantoms take their turn after all character turns are resolved and before the first enemy turn begins.

7.  Each phantom turn, draw and resolve the top card of the phantom behaviour deck.i

8.  After each icon on the encounter card is resolved in left to right order, the phantom’s turn ends.

Phantoms are removed from play if their health dial is reduced to or the encounter ends.

## Invaders

### Invader Tokens

1.  *Embered* characters provoke an invader if no invasion has occurred yet.

2.  Randomly draw an invader token from the supply.

    1.  Standard invaders before the mini-boss.

    2.  Advanced invaders after the mini-boss.

3.  Shuffle enough blank invader tokens with the selected invader token so there are equal tokens to face-down encounter cards.

4.  Place an invader token face-down on each face-down encounter card.

5.  When the characters move their models onto an encounter tile with a face-down invader token, flip the invader token face-up before revealing the encounter card.

### Invader Encounter Setup

1.  If an invader token reveals an invader icon, an invader is added to the upcoming encounter.

2.  Shuffle the invader’s behaviour cards and deal a number equal to their starting decks deck size to create the invader behaviour deck. When the invader is reduced to its Heat Up value, add an unused invader behaviour card to the behaviour deck and shuffle.

3.  Set the invader heal dial to the invader’s starting health value. Invaders do not multiply their health by the number of characters like other enemy models.

4.  After the encounter card setup is complete, place the invader model on the encounter tile’s most central node. If the central node is blocked by train, then play the invader model in a node adjacent to the centre node of the players’ choosing.

### Invader Combat

1.  Characters enter combat with the encounter normally with the addition of an invader who takes a turn after each character’s individual turn.

2.  The invader takes a turn each time a character completes their turn, not in threat order with the other enemies. Thus, the invader will take many more turns than any other model in the encounter.

3.  On an invader’s turn, resolve the top card of the invader behaviour deck.

4.  After each icon on the encounter card is resolved in left to right order, the invader’s turn ends.

5.  If any character dies, the invader immediately vanishes without award.

6.  The invader dies if their health dial is reduced to zero. Immediately award *souls* and all invader treasures when the invader dies.

7.  Invaders do not reset or return once encountered regardless of outcome.
