**Weapons - Updated Stat Requirements**

The first edition of DStBG has many nonsensical weapon stat requirements which this edition aims to remedy. Specifically, many high strength requirement weapons from the video game series where given intelligence and/or Faith requirements. Weapons with intelligence and/or faith requirements that do not deal magical damage where assessed. If the assessed weapon had no intelligence and/or faith requirements in the source material, the stats were revised for this edition. Revisions typically involved removing inaccurate stat requirements and adding a strength or dexterity requirement if previously zero. If the weapon already had both strength and dexterity requirements, the weapons primary stat requirement was often increased to ensure overall balance remained roughly equal to the first edition. Affected weapons and their changes are summarised below.
