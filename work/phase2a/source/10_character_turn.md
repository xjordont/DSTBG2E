# CHARACTER TURN

## Character Turn Order

### Minor Actions:

> *Walk*: Move character model to an adjacent node.
>
> **AND**
>
> *Run*: Move one additional node per stamina spent.
>
> **AND**
>
> *Equip*: Swap equipment cards between the back-up slot, weapon slots, and armour slots on the character dashboard. Inventory is unavailable.
>
> **AND**
>
> *Heroic Action*: Perform ability per dashboard; flip *heroic action* face-down.

> *Attack*: Use an *attack option* from the left and/or right weapon slot.

> **OR**
>
> *Rest*: Restore all of the character's stamina, if no *run* action was taken this turn.

>
> **OR**
>

## Character Movement

Characters have four methods of moving from node to node during an encounter. Characters may first *walk* as a minor action which allows them to move one space to an adjacent node. The character may then elect to *run*, which allows additional movement at the cost of one stamina per node. The character may repeat the *run* minor action as many times as they are able to pay stamina. The third way a character can move is by successfully *dodging* an attack targeting their model which forces the character to move one space to an adjacent node. Finally, some weapon’s *attack options* are marked with the *shift* icon, which allows the character to move one node during an *attack* major action. *Shift* movement is either resolved immediately before or after the attack is resolved, depending on whether the *shift* icon is before or after that *attack option’s* dice icons.

All character movement (*walk*, *run*, *dodge,* and *shift*) may only be performed if the character ends their movement in a valid node. A valid node is any node that does not contain terrain that would block movement such as a gravestone or chest. Additionally, characters are not permitted to *walk, run,* or *shift* to a node containing a barrel. However, characters may be pushed or *dodge* into a node with a barrel token, which causes the barrel to be destroyed. Characters may move into a node already at the node model limit but must push a friendly model out of the node immediately. Characters may not leave the encounter tile(s) noted on the current encounter card during their movement.

## Character Attacks

During the character’s turn they may make up to one attack with each weapon or spell equipped to the weapon slots on their character dashboard. A character makes an attack by selecting an *attack option* on one of their equipped weapons, paying the stamina cost, and then nominating a valid enemy model as their target within the weapon’s range value. Then roll the number and colour of dice associated with the selected attack option, count the number of sword icons, and apply modifiers to determine the attack’s attack value. At this point the player may elect to issue any re-rolls available to them by their *luck* token or otherwise. Once the final attack value is determined that number is compared to the enemy’s relevant defence value to determine damage dealt.

All weapons do physical damage by default. Certain weapons and most spells will instead do magical damage if the attack option selected is labelled with the magical damage icon. Final damage dealt by physical attacks is calculated by subtracting the enemy’s block value from the character's attack value. Final damage dealt by magical attacks is calculated by subtracting the enemy’s resist value from the character’s attack value.

Players then place a number of damage tokens onto the target enemy model’s base equal to the final damage dealt. If the total number of wounds received by an enemy model ever equals (or exceeds) the enemy’s scaled health value, that enemy dies and is removed from play.

Though many weapons simply deal damage, some have special abilities explained in text on their cards or with icons. This includes the magical damage icon explained above, the conditions explained previously, and the following weapon icons.

### *Range*

Some *attack options* specify a range that is different from the weapon’s default range listed on its equipment card. An *attack option* specific range replaces the weapon’s range for that attack action.

### *Shift*

When a character makes an attack with the *shift* icon, they may move up to the number of nodes shown. Shift icons that appear before the dice icons allow a character to move before rolling the dice. Those that appear after the dice allow the character to move after rolling the dice. Movement from *Shift* icons costs 0 Stamina and does not replace the character’s options to *Walk* and *Run* during their activation.

### *Area-of-Effect (AoE)*

Attacks with the *AoE* icon can target all enemies on a single node within range instead of targeting just one enemy. Make the attack roll once, and compare it to the block or resist value of each enemy on the node to determine final damage dealt to each enemy model.

### *Shaft*

Bows, polearms, and other unwieldy weapons are difficult to use effectively at short range. Weapons with the *shaft* icon cannot target enemy models on the character’s node.

### *Repeat*

The *repeat* icon allows the character to use that entire weapon option the number of times indicated in the circle. This represents a rapid flurry of attacks such as from an agile sword or a repeating crossbow.

## Two-Handed Attacks

Characters equipped with a two-handed weapon are decidedly on the offensive. Attack actions with two-handed weapons may reroll any single die after rolling to attack. Additionally, if a character equips a one-handed weapon without equipment to their other hand, that weapon is considered to be wielded as two-handed and gains the same reroll option.

## Stat Scaling

Weapons may provide additional attack damage if they are equipped by a character who’s stats far exceed the weapon’s stat requirements. If the character's current stat value exceeds an equipment’s highest stat requirement by ten or more points, the character should mark the equipped item with a red damage cube to indicate that it is scaled. If two requirements are tied, use either. A scaled weapon benefits from a +1 modifier to the attack value of all of its’ attack option*s*. Weapons are not provided additional stat scaling beyond the +1 benefits for excess stats beyond ten. Spells are not affected by scaling.
