# CRYSTAL LIZARDS

Crystal lizards are strange and glimmering reptiles that offer character’s powerful rewards if the lizard is slain before it can escape. Players should include crystal lizards as an optional game component if they own the Iron Keep expansion set. The following rules are assuming the players are playing with the Iron Keep expansion set and the included crystal lizards.

Whenever the character reveals an encounter card with the crystal lizard symbol, a crystal lizard appears. During encounter set-up, place a crystal lizard on the terrain node indicated by the encounter card. Some encounters contain multiple crystal lizards. Crystal lizards take their turns like any other enemy model in descending *threat* order. However, their behaviours dictate that they simply run from the characters which makes killing them challenging. Crystal lizards do not scale their health depending on the number of characters. Whenever a character kills a crystal lizard, they immediately place one Titanite Scale card into their inventory. When the last enemy of an encounter (beside crystal lizards) dies, any remaining crystal lizards are immediately removed from play. The crystal lizards removed from play at the end of an encounter have escaped and do not award characters any treasure.

If any number of crystal lizards die during an encounter, no further crystal lizards will spawn on repeated attempts of that encounter. Mark encounter tiles with an upside-down crystal lizard model if any crystal lizard dies during an encounter. Otherwise, if no crystal lizard died in a previous attempt, the crystal lizards will respawn on repeated attempts until one dies. Note that an encounter is still considered *cleared* regardless of any surviving crystal lizards.
