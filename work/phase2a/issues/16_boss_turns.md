# Issues — chapter 16 (Boss Turns)

- Character order (§16.1): the chapter applies A-019 and A-022: the players choose which living character takes each character turn, and the descending threat order of §7.5 and §10.1 does not apply in a boss encounter. Both of those sections delegate boss encounters to §16.1. If the author prefers threat order in boss encounters too, one sentence of §16.1 and the amended glossary entry Turn change.
- Repeat (§16.1): the repeat icon on a behaviour card repeats the whole card (manuscript), while §9.3 has an enemy's repeat icon repeat one attack. The chapter adds that targets are determined again at each resolution. The author must confirm.
- Push-move against a wall (§16.2, Push-Moves): §8.4 leaves a character who cannot be pushed directly away where they stand. The chapter adds, for bosses only, the manuscript's arc rule with its wall fallback, which reproduces the fact source's worked example. §8.4 cites §16.2 for the differences of boss movement; confirm.
- Arc of a character on a node the boss enters (§16.2): drafted as the arc in which the node lay immediately before the boss entered it, in place of the manuscript's "at the beginning of the boss's movement", because the boss turns before each node.
- Leap (§16.2, Leap): three completions have no manuscript source: a leap whose target is on the boss's own node still pushes the characters there; a character with no valid adjacent node stays and is placed in an arc of the player's choice; a leap whose push icon carries a damage value attacks as a push-move does. The author must confirm. No icon slug exists for the leap icon or the skull icon; both are named in words.
- Icons (§16.2, §16.3): the push icon on an attack uses ast-0058 and the push icon with a damage value uses ast-0031, as §9.2 and §9.3 do; the chapter instructions named the satellite glyph for §16.3. Layout must confirm.
- Aggro (§16.4): a riposte's damage moves the token at the end of that boss turn (A-018), and the last riposting character takes it if several riposte; an attack reduced to no damage does not move it. §12.1's Distract icon (a summon treated as holding the token for one boss turn) is left to §12.1.
- Dead characters at a boss's defeat (§16.5): §20.1 bars a character who died in the boss encounter from the PvP encounter that follows, so the chapter keeps such a character out of play until the free rest and does not apply the recovery of §7.5. The character still receives the soul reward and an ember token. The author must confirm.
- Final boss (§16.5): following the manuscript, the defeat of the final boss wins the game and grants no rewards. §20.1 says the PvP encounter "follows the rewards for the boss's defeat"; after the final boss there are none.
- Unawarded boss treasure (§16.5 step 3): returned to the game box; the manuscript is silent.
- Spark and rest (§16.5): §7.4 says the party gains one spark "(see §16.5)", while §6.6 step 17 and §6.7 step 8 add it as setup steps. The chapter attributes the spark and the free rest to those setup steps and states that each is gained once. The lead editor may prefer to cite one place only.
- Cross-chapter dependencies satisfied here: §5.2 and §5.5 (boss movement and blocked nodes), §7.3, §7.4, §7.5, §7.6, §7.7, and §11.1 (rewards, embering, spark, free rest), §8.1 and §8.4 (area targets; push-move differences), §9.1, §9.3, and §9.5 (boss turns; dodge difficulty on the behaviour card), §10.1 and §12.1 (alternation; summon cadence, targeting, and aggro), §12.2 (no invasion at the moment of embering), §13 (mimic alternation and aggro), §17.1 (a boss moving onto a character's node; Boss Death resolved once), §20.1 and §20.9 (PvP encounter after the rewards and before the setup; one rest).
- Cross-chapter dependencies placed on others: §18.1 and §18.3 must state how the spark, the rest, and the setup after a boss differ in campaign play; §17 must define any icon found only on one boss's cards.
- Glossary: the ADD lines for Attack arc, Facing, and Leap repeat the chapter 15 delta verbatim, and those for Nearest character, Node attack, Push-attack, and Push-move repeat the chapter 08 and 09 deltas verbatim, so that integration merges them idempotently. The AMEND of Aggro token, Round, and Turn extends entries owned by chapters 07, 10, and 20.
