# Open issues — chapter 23 Appendices

- **Butcher Knife (B-020).** The approved finding offers two values: 24 0 0 0 (tier one) or 28 0 0 0 (tier two). The appendix uses 24 (tier one), the first-listed value. The author may prefer 28; only this row and its Tier cell change.
- **Treasure type of the listed weapons.** No supplied source gives the treasure type (common, class-specific, transposed, legendary, boss) of any card, so the Tier column of Appendix A is computed from the requirements alone and the section Treasure Type states the type rules in general terms. The audit suspects that Black Knight Halberd, Gravelord Sword, Lifehunt Scythe, Priscilla's Dagger, and Vordt's Great Hammer are boss treasure; if any listed card is transposed, legendary, or boss treasure, its Tier cell needs a check against the card.
- **Additional Treasure grouping.** CMP-0004 groups the 34 cards by type (weapons and shields; armour; items, upgrades and spells). The grouping is not kept, because the sources do not confirm the types (Soul Spear sits among the weapons, and Skull Lantern, Mask of the Child, and Divine Blessing are untyped). The source's second sub-list (Greatsword, Tower Shield, Steel Armour, Priscilla's Dagger, Lifehunt Scythe, Blue Titanite, Black Knight Halberd, Black Knight Shield, Gravelord Sword, Gravelord Sword Dance) appears to be the boss treasure of the four bosses in Appendix B; the author to confirm. CMP-0004 says 33 names; there are 34, all present.
- **Demon Titanite.** The manuscript lists the card with no note. The appendix lists it with no change of rule (CMP-0004). The author to confirm that no amendment was intended.
- **Demon Titanite and Blue Titanite as Titanite.** CMP-0004 labels both "Weapon upgrade (Titanite)", but §4.2 and the glossary name only four Titanite cards (Shard, Chunk, Slab, Scale). The appendix does not call the two cards Titanite. If they fuse permanently, §4.2 and the glossary entry need both names.
- **Mon Ring.** "One use per floor" is read as once between rests at the bonfire (CMP-0004). The author to confirm.
- **Chloranthy Ring.** The two notes are merged as CMP-0004 proposes: at the start of the wearer's turn, every character on the wearer's node recovers one stamina. The alternative reading (each character on the node recovers one more at the start of their own turn) is not adopted. The interplay with frostbite (§8.5) is not stated in any source.
- **Black Leather Armour and Eastern Armour.** CMP-0004 keeps the dice and requirements of one printing; those values are in no supplied source, so the appendix says only that the printing carrying the stated text is used.
- **Printed names (OQ-17).** "Four-Pronged Plough" follows the manuscript; the printed card may read "Plow", which `tools/style_check.py` would flag unless the name is whitelisted. "Sellsword Twinblades" and "Onikiri and Ubadachi" follow CMP-0004, not the source ("Sellsword Twinblade", "Onikiri And Ubadachi"). All three need a check against the cards.
- **Cross-chapter pointers.** §4.1 (Stat Requirements), §6.2, and §10.5 do not cite Appendix A, and §4.2 (Titanite) states that attached Titanite cannot be removed without citing the replacement rule of Appendix B. One cross-reference in each is recommended at merge. §17.7 and §17.10 call the cards "replacement" cards; Appendix B calls them "revised cards" that replace the printed cards. The sense agrees; the wording may be aligned.
- **Treasure totals.** §2.5 counts 534 treasure cards. Whether that total includes the 34 additional treasure cards is not stated in any source.
- **A-066.** The rule that equipment with a highest requirement of zero cannot be scaled is not in the committed §10.5. It does not affect Appendix A (no listed weapon has a highest requirement of zero) but remains open for §10.5.
