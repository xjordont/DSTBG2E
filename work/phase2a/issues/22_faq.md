# Issues — chapter 22 (F.A.Q.)

- New player in the standard game (§22.1): the approved draft allows a player to join a standard game on the campaign's terms. The rule is anchored in §18.4 (one sentence added there). The author must confirm that joining is wanted outside campaign play.
- Embered reduction and traps (§22.3): the approved ruling is that trap damage is not reduced. §5.5 has a trap dodged "as an attack"; §7.3 reduces only attacks. If the author wants traps reduced, §7.3 must say so.
- Ties (§22.4): the approved draft gave every unresolved choice to the aggro holder. The chapters give that tie-break only for treasure (§7.7) and the chest decision (§13.1); the answer is limited to what the chapters state.
- The answers on campaign sparks and transposition (§22.7) follow the chapter 18 draft and must be rechecked if §18.3 or §18.8 changes.
