# Issues — chapter 11 (Returning to the Bonfire)

- Sold ember card (§11.4, Sales): the manuscript sends every sold card to "the bottom of their respective treasure deck", but ember cards belong to no treasure deck (§6.2). The chapter returns a sold ember card face up to the tier one shop, by analogy with the surplus ember card of §7.3, and prices it as a tier one card (two souls to buy, one soul to sell). The author must confirm, or forbid the sale of ember cards.
- Sold boss treasure (§11.4, Sales): returned to the game box, as the chapter 06 issues list asked (A-077). A-077 also proposed the game box for sold legendary treasure; the chapter instead treats legendary and transposed treasure as tier two cards (one soul, bottom of the tier two treasure deck), because §6.2 shuffles the five legendary cards into that deck. The campaign table's legendary sell value of four souls is left to §18.6. The author must confirm.
- Titanite sale value (§11.4, Sales): the manuscript says attached Titanite is "returned" with a sold weapon and names no payment. The chapter states that the weapon's sell value is paid once and the Titanite adds nothing. The author must confirm.
- Upgrade service (§11.4, Upgrades): the manuscript names no cost for attaching an upgrade and does not mention removal; §4.2 (committed) says Andre attaches and removes upgrade cards. The chapter states that both are free of souls and unlimited during a rest, and keeps the manuscript's requirement that the weapon or armour card be equipped when the upgrade is attached. The author must confirm.
- Return without a rest (§11.1): the chapter states that returning to the bonfire tile is free, that the models are placed on the tile without passing back through the encounter tiles, and that nothing is restored and nobody is visited until the party rests. The manuscript implies this (the forced rest places every model on the tile) but never states it. The author must confirm.
- Free rest and PvP (§11.2): the manuscript grants a free rest "after defeating a boss or completing player-versus-player (PvP)", and the PvP chapter restores the characters "as if they rested" before the PvP encounter. The chapter says only that the free rest is taken after the PvP encounter (see §20.9). §20.1 must own the restoration before the PvP encounter and §20.9 the free rest after it; §6.6 and §6.7 (which place the free rest in the setup after a boss) and §16.5 must agree on the order boss, PvP encounter, setup, rest.
- Shop restock inside the rest (§11.2, step 7): the chapter instructions place a conditional restock in the rest procedure. §6.6 step 16 and §6.7 step 7 already restock during setup, before the free rest, so step 7 is worded to do nothing once that restock has happened ("if the shop has not been restocked since a boss was last defeated"). The step is placed before the visits to the Firekeeper and Andre so that the characters shop from the new stock. For campaign play the chapter redirects the condition to the completion of a section; §18.6 must match.
- Souls spent outside a rest (§11.3): A-100 makes the summon cost at the fog gate the one exception to "souls are spent only while resting". §7.6 (committed) states the general rule without the exception; §11.3 states the exception and cites §12.1. §7.6 and §12.1 must agree. In campaign play the spark buy-back (OQ-22), the bonfire ascetic, and transposition are assumed to be paid while resting; §18.3, §18.7, and §18.8 must say so.
- "Souls cannot be pooled" (§11.3) follows §7.6. The bonfire ascetic is a communal payment (§18.7); §18.7 must state it as the exception.
- Boss tile reset (§11.5): the reset procedure covers the encounter tiles only and cites §15.2 for a failed boss encounter. §15.2 must state how the boss model, health dial, and behaviour deck are reset when the party rests.
- Invaders and mimics at a reset (§11.5): the chapter cites §12.2 for the manuscript's rule that an invader does not return once encountered. Nothing in the manuscript says what happens to ambush cards or to an opened chest's mimic at a reset; §13 must state it.
- D-011 (pay one soul at the Firekeeper to ready the luck token) is not applied: the rest itself readies the token (§11.2, step 6), so the purchase has no use in this edition. The author must confirm the drop is intended.
- Glossary: the entry **Starting equipment** said "may not be sold"; it is amended to "cannot be sold" with a cross-reference. The entry **Fog gate** is left as it is; §15.1 owns it.
