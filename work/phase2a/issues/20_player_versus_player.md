# Issues — chapter 20 (Player versus Player)

- Stalemate rule (§20.8): A-035 (OPTIONAL, decision needed) is applied: after three consecutive rounds with no damage and no death, every character suffers one damage at the end of each further round. It adds a mechanic beyond CMP-0009; the author must confirm the rule and the three-round threshold. "And no character dies" is added so that a trap death does not count as a quiet round.
- Dodge-difficulty table (§20.4): the values 3 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 1 follow B-024 and replace the manuscript's 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 0, which CMP-0009 reproduced. The change is logged as a mechanical change (CH-20-018) outside the five signed off by number at Gate 1; it rests on the approve-all decision and the lead's recommendation.
- Characters who died in the boss encounter (§20.1): the chapter follows CMP-0009 (they take no part). A-034 argued the exclusion is unreachable because a cleared boss encounter revives the dead; §16.5 must not contradict the exclusion, and the author may prefer that every character takes part.
- Order of events after a boss (§20.1, §20.9): the chapter places the PvP encounter after the boss's rewards (§16.5) and before the setup of §6.6 or §6.7, whose final steps hold the spark and the free rest. §16.5 and §11.2 must describe that rest as following the PvP encounter when PvP is in play, and must not grant a second rest.
- Choices left to the players (§20.5): the standard game leaves equal push directions and the model displaced from a full node (§8.4, §5.3) to the players jointly. The chapter gives the choice to the player whose character makes the attack or movement. No finding or completion covers this; the author must confirm.
- Heroic actions and card text that name enemies: §20.4 applies an attack option's icons and text to a defender, but no source says which heroic actions and non-attack card effects work against characters. The author must rule.
- Luck (§20.4): the chapter follows §3.5 (a character rerolls only their own rolls). If luck may be spent on an opponent's roll, §3.5 and this chapter both change (see the chapter 03 issues list).
- Parry (§20.4): the chapter names no weapon slot and defers to §9.6 for the conditions of a parry and the riposte weapon, because the left-slot rule and A-068 disagree (see the chapter 03 issues list).
- Trap tokens (§20.6): only the trap tokens in the supply are used, so tokens lying on encounter tiles reduce the number available. CMP-0009 names only the entering character; the chapter follows §5.5 (each character on the node). The trap's printed damage value is unused. The author must confirm that a full endurance bar (no space for the dodge's stamina cube) kills, as CMP-0009 states, rather than staggering under §3.4.
- Corner node (§20.1): defined as the node nearest a corner of the tile among the nodes without terrain. The definition is new and untested on mega-boss game boards of irregular shape.
- Victory marker (§20.8): a wound token of value 1 marks each victory bonus (CMP-0009). The core set supplies six such tokens, which are also needed for enemies. §2.8 and the glossary entry Wound token (owner §8.2) do not mention this use; the entry is not amended here to avoid a clash with the chapter 08 delta. A dedicated marker would need a line in §2.8.
- Campaign play: no source says whether PvP encounters are played in a campaign or which boss is the final boss there. §18 is silent and the chapter does not address it.
- Threat ties (§20.2): "decided at random" follows CMP-0011; no method is named.
- Glossary entries owned elsewhere and not amended: Aggro token ("at the start of each encounter"), Encounter ("ends cleared or failed"), and Area-of-effect ("every enemy on the targeted node") do not mention the PvP exceptions; the new headword PvP encounter states them.
- Icons: `ast-0054-node-attack` is used for the AoE icon and `ast-0058-push-affected-enemies` for the push icon on an attack option; §10.3 and §8.4 must use the same slugs.
