# Issues — chapter 13 (Mimics)

- "The mimic's souls" (§13.3): the chapter instructions ask for the mimic's souls to be awarded with its two treasure cards, but no source gives a mimic a soul reward: the manuscript's soul table has no mimic row, CMP-0014 awards only "the rewards of the encounter that contained the chest", and the expansion awards none. The chapter states that a mimic has no soul reward of its own and that the deferred soul reward of the encounter is collected once the mimic is killed. If a mimic is meant to pay a reward of its own (for example the boss reward), the author must give the value and §7.6 must list it.
- B-035 (approved PROPOSED) removes all stamina cubes before the mimic's first turn. It is not applied (CH-13-022): it alters deferred recovery, which the preserve list protects, and contradicts CMP-0014. The author must confirm whether the easing is wanted.
- Failed mimic encounter (§13.4): A-060's proposed fix keeps the cleared marker when the mimic encounter is failed; CMP-0014 turns the encounter card face down. The chapter follows CMP-0014 and resolves the encounter as any failed encounter under §7.5, so a black cube from an earlier clear is removed with it. The author must confirm.
- Chest token (§13.3): a killed mimic takes its chest token off the tile. §5.5, Chests and §11.5 step 4 say that terrain stays on its nodes through a reset; both need the exception or a pointer to §13.3.
- Deferred resolution (§13.1, §13.3): §7.5, Encounter Resolution has the characters decide "before step 1". The chapter relies on §7.5 for every step and omits only the opening of the chest; §7.5 must accept that an encounter whose enemies have been killed is failed if the mimic encounter that follows is failed.
- Mimic models: the expansion supplies two models, one of them a closed mimic for a chest known to be a mimic. The chapter returns the chest token to the node after a failed mimic encounter and leaves the ambush card face up as the record. The author may prefer the closed model as the marker.
- Several chests on one tile: one ambush card is dealt per tile. No source says what happens when an encounter card places more than one chest; the chapter is written for one.
- Mimic values: the chapter does not restate the Hungry Mimic's or the Voracious Mimic's threat, health, deck size, or heat-up point; they are read from the data cards.
- Cross-chapter dependencies: §17.6 must describe the mimics as this chapter does (no arcs, one unused card at heat-up, Loot the Body as the chest's card and one drawn); §19.8 must state that the chest of the Gallery of the Hidden Warrior is a Voracious Mimic with no ambush card and that killing it completes the section; §16.1 and §16.4 must hold the turn alternation and aggro rules that §13.2 cites; §6.6 gathers the ambush cards, including any left face up.
- Glossary: **Chest** (owned by §5.5) says that with mimics in play "opening a chest requires revealing the tile's ambush card"; this agrees with §13.1 and is not amended.
