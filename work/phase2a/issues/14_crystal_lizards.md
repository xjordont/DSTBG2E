# Issues — chapter 14 (Crystal Lizards)

- Data card: the crystal lizard's data card was not supplied. The chapter states its behaviour from the manuscript ("they simply run from the characters") and from the description of the expansion (it does not attack): the behaviour icons move it away from the characters and it makes no attack. The author must confirm against the card, including its threat, health, and movement value.
- Marker for a tile where a crystal lizard has been killed: the manuscript marks the tile with an upside-down crystal lizard model, but the expansion supplies only two models, so two marked tiles would leave no model to place. The chapter requires the tile to be marked and gives the upside-down model as an example. The author may name a different marker.
- Supply of Titanite Scale cards: CMP-0015 caps the reward at the three cards supplied. With a card for each kill, an encounter with two crystal lizards awards two cards; a kill made after the third card is taken awards nothing. The author must confirm that no other card is substituted.
- Killer of a crystal lizard: the card goes to "the character who kills" it. No source says who receives it if a crystal lizard is killed by a condition token or by other damage that is not an attack; the chapter is silent.
- Cross-chapter dependencies: §18.7 must exempt crystal lizards (and invaders) from the bonfire ascetic, as §8.3 exempts them from enemy scaling (A-028). §7.5, Encounter Start step 8 places the crystal lizards and §7.5, Encounter Resolution excludes them from clearing, both citing this chapter; §7.7 awards the Titanite Scale "at once", as here.
