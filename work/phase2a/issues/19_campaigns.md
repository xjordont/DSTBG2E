# Issues — chapter 19 (Campaigns)

- Unverified campaigns (A-111): Demon Hunting (§19.6, Asylum Demon booklet), Call of the Abyss (§19.7, The Four Kings booklet), Into the Depths (§19.10, Gaping Dragon booklet), and Big Game Hunting (§19.12, Manus booklet) come from booklets that were not supplied. Their section names, lists, and named encounters (Outskirts of Blighttown, Darkened Chamber, The Depths) are kept exactly as the manuscript gives them. The Last Giant (§19.12) and the Vordt of the Boreal Valley content (§19.9) are likewise unverified.
- The Gauntlet invader rule (§19.4): the manuscript repeats the Ambush Lies Ahead preamble word for word (each invader appears the first time only, once each). The supplied Phantoms text gives The Gauntlet a different rule: a listed invader is part of its encounter every time the party enters it until the invader is defeated. The chapter keeps the manuscript's rule because no finding or Gate 1 answer covers it; the author must say whether the copy was deliberate. Under the manuscript's rule a section's final encounter can be cleared without its invader after one failed attempt.
- Call of the Abyss, Section Two (§19.7): the list is Level 1, Level 2, Level 2, Level 3, Great Grey Wolf Sif. This is the same sequence as the rejected duplicate of Facing the Abyss, whose Section Two was a transcription error (the Darkroot text has Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, Level 3). It cannot be checked without The Four Kings booklet and is kept as given.
- Demon Hunting (§19.6): Sections Three and Four are both named "Sen's Fortress" in the manuscript; kept, unverified. The Section One rest rule joins A-104 (spend a spark, restore, restart Section One, no Firekeeper or Andre) to the manuscript's parenthetical (the characters revive on Section Two's bonfire tile). CMP-0008 asks the author to confirm that the missing bonfire tile is deliberate. The chapter applies the rule to any rest in Section One, chosen or required; A-104 spoke only of a required rest.
- Section Four of The First Journey (§19.1): the heading is "Section Four: Bathed in Flame", as instructed. The supplied Kalameet text and CMP-0021 name the section's location "Royal Wood" and use "Bathed in Flame" as the scenario title. The location name does not appear in the chapter; swap the heading if the location form is preferred.
- The Undead Colosseum requirement (§19.9): C-076 (REQUIRED) makes Explorers a required expansion because the Old Dragonslayer and the Pursuer are Explorers bosses; the CMP-0022 draft left Explorers optional. The chapter follows C-076. The soul reward ("the campaign soul reward for its level and two further souls") depends on the campaign table in §18.5. The shared Level 4 encounter deck departs from §6.7 step 4 (the mega-boss's own four cards) for this campaign only.
- Dependencies on §18.1 (drafted in parallel):
  - The Gauntlet's sections end in an encounter that lists an invader, not in a "named encounter". §18.1's completion rule must cover a section that ends in any encounter, and must say what a campaign without bosses grants in sparks and treasure tiers. A Hollow Pursuit's Section Two is completed when the mimic is killed, which §19.8 states as an exception.
  - Level 4 encounters inside sections (A-094): Call of the Abyss Section Four lists two; Into the Depths Section Four lists one before a main-boss and Section Five lists two named Level 4 encounters; Demon Hunting Section Four and Big Game Hunting Sections Two A to Two C list one before each mega-boss. §19 cites §18.1 for the board placement and states no general rule.
  - Demon Hunting places a mega-boss (Stray Demon) in Section Four of five, and Big Game Hunting has three mega-bosses. §18.1 must not end a campaign at the first mega-boss (A-049 is moot under OQ-14, but the "final boss" wording still matters).
  - Big Game Hunting: the manuscript does not say which mega-boss game board is used for the Asylum Demon mini-boss encounter in Section One, nor how the three Section Two bonfire tiles and boards are laid out when the order is free. §19.12 states only what the manuscript states.
  - The supplied texts add the transposed and legendary treasure after Section One of each campaign (after Section Two in The Undead Colosseum). §19 leaves the treasure tiers to §18.1.
- Glossary: the AMEND for **Section** widens the current definition ("ending in one or more bosses"; "named encounter") to match §19.4, §19.6, and §19.8. Chapter 18 owns the term; if its delta differs, chapter 18's governs and the two need reconciling.
- §13.4 returns a mimic to its chest when a mimic encounter is failed. In A Hollow Pursuit the chest has no ambush card, so §19.8's sentence ("as if an ambush card showing Mimic had been revealed") must apply each time the chest is opened; no text in §13 contradicts this, but §13.1 mentions the campaign in one line only.
- Ambush Lies Ahead and The Gauntlet list advanced invaders (Paladin Leeroy, Armorer Dennis, and others) in fixed encounters. §12.2's limits (one invasion per leg, standard invaders before the mini-boss) are read as not applying to listed invaders, which §12.2 already allows ("Some campaigns place named invaders in listed encounters and use no invader tokens").
