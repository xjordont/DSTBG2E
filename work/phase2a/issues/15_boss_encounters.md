# Issues — chapter 15 (Boss Encounters)

- Starting facing (§15.2 step 6): the chapter instructions asked for the boss "facing as the data card shows", but no fact source shows a facing on a data card. The chapter applies A-122: the front arc faces the adjacent node closest to the entry nodes, the players settling ties, and a boss's special rules may replace the step (see §17; Black Dragon Kalameet faces the centre of its game board). The author must confirm.
- Fog gate shortcut (§15.1): the chapter states that encounters bypassed by the shortcut are not skipped encounters, so no stamina cube is placed for them, and that the shortcut is open whatever the state of the encounters on the route. The manuscript says only "travel directly … without having to fight any encounters". The author must confirm that the skip cost (OQ-10) is not charged.
- Heat-up shuffle (§15.5, Heat-Up): the heat-up card is shuffled into the cards that remain in the behaviour deck only, and the discard pile is left in order (A-042). The other reading of "shuffle it into the boss behaviour deck" reshuffles deck and discard pile together. The author must confirm.
- Deck reset (§15.5, Deck Reset): no source says what happens to a boss's deck between attempts once the deck is built at setup (A-043). The chapter rebuilds the deck from all of the boss's behaviour cards at each reset, so every attempt meets a newly built deck, which matches §17.1 (Twin Gargoyles). Consequences to confirm: undealt standard cards are set aside rather than returned to the game box (CH-15-021), and a card revealed by a scry is lost when the deck is rebuilt.
- Weak arcs on a card with several arc diagrams (§15.6, Arc Diagrams): the chapter uses the diagram beneath the last attack icon (fact source). The arc state "neutral" follows the icon set; the manuscript wrote "normal". The colours black, green, and red are the manuscript's.
- Parry of boss attacks (§15.6, Defence): §9.6 forbids parrying a boss "except where §15.6 allows". The chapter allows the parry of any boss attack that targets one character and is not an area attack, a node attack, or a push-attack, which is the manuscript's exclusion list. The shield slot remains as §9.6 states it.
- Push off a boss's node (§15.6, Characters and Arcs): the same-arc limit is applied to every push off the boss's node, including the displacement caused by the node model limit, for which §8.4 says "any valid node chosen by the players". §8.4 cites §15.6 for the limit; if the node model limit is meant to be exempt, one clause of §15.6 must go.
- Dodge on a boss's node (§15.6, Defence): "any arc on the base" is drafted as "any other arc", because the dodge movement is mandatory (§9.5). A character who dodges onto the boss's node is placed in an arc of the player's choice; the manuscript does not cover that case.
- Boss tiles (§15.3): the manuscript does not say along which edges the two boss tiles are joined; the chapter says "along one edge to form a single rectangular tile". The statement that a boss tile has no terrain follows from the absence of an encounter card; the author must confirm that neither boss tile is printed with terrain nodes.
- Examples: no verified boss statistics are available, so the worked examples use an unnamed boss with illustrative values (starting health 24, heat-up point 12, block 2, resist 1). Layout may substitute a named boss once its card is checked.
- Intro: the sentence "Bosses follow the enemy rules of §8 and §9 except as stated in this chapter and §16." uses in-line section references, as the approved draft (F-027) gives it, and stands as its own paragraph after the intro.
- Glossary: the ADD lines for Node attack, Push-attack, and Push-move repeat the chapter 08 and 09 deltas verbatim, and the ADD line for Leap is repeated verbatim in the chapter 16 delta, so that integration merges them idempotently. The reconciled entries Parry and Riposte (owner §9.6) are not amended here.
- Cross-chapter dependencies satisfied here: §5.1 (joined boss tiles are one tile), §6.5 to §6.7 and §7.5 (deck built at setup, top card may be scried), §6.7 (mega-boss encounter set up and failed as any boss encounter), §8.4 (arc limit on pushes off the boss's node), §9.6 (parry allowance), §10.3 and §12.1 (weak-arc die), §11.5 (fog gate shortcut; reset of a failed boss encounter), §12.2 and §13 (heat-up owned by §15.5; no arcs), §17.1 (starting facing; new deck).
- Cross-chapter dependencies placed on others: §18.2 must state that the fog gate token is aesthetic only in campaign play and is not moved to the bonfire tile as a shortcut; §17 must state any special placement, facing, deck construction, or heat-up for a boss, since §15.2 and §15.5 defer to it.
