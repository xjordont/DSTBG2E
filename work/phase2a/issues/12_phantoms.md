# Issues — chapter 12 (Phantoms)

- Invader and clearing (§12.2, End of an Invasion): the manuscript does not say whether an invader must be killed for its encounter to be cleared, and §7.5 leaves the point to this section. The chapter states that an invader does not count towards clearing and that an invader still on the tile when the encounter is cleared is removed without award. The author must confirm; the alternative (the invader must be killed) makes the encounter harder to leave. §7.5, Encounter Resolution ("crystal lizards do not count") and the glossary entry **Cleared** must then name invaders beside crystal lizards.
- Invasion trigger (§12.2, Invasions): the manuscript's "Embered characters provoke an invader if no invasion has occurred yet" is drafted as two moments (A-045): the dealing of encounter cards while a character is embered, and the discarding of an ember card while a face-down encounter card lies on a tile. A character who becomes embered while every encounter card is face up provokes no invasion until the next setup. The embering at a boss's defeat is deferred to the next setup so that tokens are never placed on cards that §6.6 is about to remove. The author must confirm.
- First setup (§6.5, Encounter Cards, step 5): "place the invader tokens" places nothing in the standard game, because no character is embered when the first encounter cards are dealt. The step is correct as a pointer but reads as unconditional; §12.2 states the condition.
- Souls spent away from the bonfire (§12.1, Summoning): §7.6 states without exception that souls "are spent only while resting at the bonfire". The summoning payment is an exception (A-100); §12.1 says so, and §7.6 needs the same exception or a pointer to §12.1.
- Blank invader tokens (§12.2, Invader Tokens): the expansion supplies three, enough for four encounter cards. A campaign section with more than four face-down encounter cards has too few; no source says which cards go without a token.
- Summon defence (§12.1, Summons in Combat): CMP-0018 gives a summon block, resist, and dodge dice "exactly as for a character". A character who fails a dodge rolls armour dice only; a summon has no armour card, so the chapter states that a failed dodge deals full damage. The chapter also states that a hit summon is pushed and receives conditions as a character does, which no source states in terms. The author must confirm both.
- Summon attacks (§12.1, Summon Turns): the summon behaviour cards were not supplied. The chapter resolves their attacks as character attacks (dice shown on the card against the boss's block or resist), following the description of the expansion. The author must confirm against the cards.
- Choice of summon (§12.1, Summoning): the manuscript requires the players to agree on one drawn card and gives no tie-break; none is added, because the aggro token is not yet assigned at the fog gate.
- Invader treasure (§12.2, Invader Rewards): awarded at once, so a disagreement is settled by the aggro holder "at that moment"; §7.7 words the general tie-break as the holder "when the encounter ends". The two differ only for a card awarded during an encounter.
- Invader as enemy (§12.2): "the rules for enemy models apply to an invader" except as stated, so characters may push an invader and place conditions on it. If invaders are meant to resist pushes as bosses do, the author must say so.
- Cross-chapter dependencies: §15.2 must place the characters on the entry nodes of the boss tile before the summon is placed; §16.1 must admit the summon's turn into the alternation of boss and character turns; §16.4 must state that damage dealt by a summon does not move the aggro token and must allow for the Distract icon; §15.6 must give the weak-arc die to summons as well as characters; §8.3 and §18.7 must exempt invaders (and crystal lizards) from enemy scaling and from the bonfire ascetic (A-028); §19.3 and §19.4 must state that those campaigns place no invader tokens; §11.5 must leave unrevealed invader tokens in place at a reset.
- Glossary entries owned elsewhere and not amended here: **Cleared** (invaders do not count); **Weak arc** ("a character attacking a boss": summons also gain the die); **Behaviour deck** and **Behaviour card** ("invader or phantom": an invader is a phantom; "summon" is meant); **Shift** ("phantom behaviour icon": only summon cards carry it); **Turn** (the summon's and invader's turns).
