# Issues — chapter 17 (Boss Special Rules)

## Dependencies placed on §17 by committed chapters (all satisfied)

- §2.3 (Ornstein & Smough: one main-boss, two models, one health dial, one behaviour deck, each card names the acting model): stated in §17.1, Two Boss Models in One Encounter, and applied in §17.2.
- §5.3 (a boss model that moves onto a node holding another boss model pushes it to a valid node chosen by the players): stated in §17.1 with the consequences for facing and for characters on either node.
- §5.5, Pillars (placement, destruction, and restoration of pillars): stated in §17.3. §5.5 places pillars "at the start of a mega-boss encounter fought on that expansion's mega-boss game board"; §17.3 says "every boss encounter fought on the mega-boss side of that game board", which includes it.
- §6.7, The Mega-Boss Encounter, step 5 ("Apply the mega-boss's special rules (see §17)"): §17.3, §17.8, and §17.9 give them; §17.10 delegates the rest.
- §2.9 (Kalameet's 8x strafe cards and 4x calamity tokens; the Chariot's two models, 4x track behaviour cards, and mega-boss setup card; 4x pillar tokens): each is listed under Components and ruled on in §17.8, §17.9, and §17.3. The mega-boss setup card is defined in §17.9 and added to the glossary.
- §20.1 (a PvP encounter follows each boss): §17.1 states that for the Twin Gargoyles the PvP encounter follows the second Gargoyle only.

## Facts only the author can confirm

- Ornstein & Smough (§17.1, §17.2): the data card and behaviour cards were not supplied. Three statements are inferences from the Gate 1 answer (OQ-25) and need confirmation: (a) the two models start on the main-boss spawn node and the mini-boss spawn node of the joined boss tiles, one on each as the data card directs, with the players choosing if the card is silent; (b) a condition token gained by either model is placed beside the shared health dial and affects the boss; (c) the boss heats up once at the heat-up point and neither model leaves play before the boss is defeated. If the printed data card gives each model its own health or makes one model's death the heat-up, OQ-25's shared-dial reading overrides the card, and the Foreword's rule that the book governs over card text must cover it.
- Twin Gargoyles (§17.1): four details have no source and were settled so that the rule is complete: the single Gargoyle model is returned to the mini-boss spawn node for the second part; a character standing on that node is placed in an arc of the player's choice; the second Gargoyle takes its first turn after the turn of the character who defeated the first; a summon in play stays in play. The official footnote covers only a defeat by the second Gargoyle; §17.1 applies the same result (both fought again) to a defeat by the first.
- Asylum Demon (§17.3): the expansion's rules were not supplied. §2.3 lists the Asylum Demon as a mini-boss fought on the boss tiles, while the manuscript's Pillar Smasher text places pillars on "the mega-boss game board" for both demons. §17.3 states that pillar tokens are used in every boss encounter fought on the mega-boss side of that board and in no other, without saying where the Asylum Demon mini-boss is fought. The author must confirm the tile used for the Asylum Demon, and whether "Pillar Smasher" is the special ability printed on both data cards.
- Pillar damage (§17.3): the manuscript says characters "immediately suffer one damage". The chapter states that this damage is not an attack and cannot be blocked, resisted, or dodged, and that only characters (not summons or enemies) are affected. Confirm.
- Wind Up (§17.3): the manuscript's "considered a Run for purposes of rules interaction" is drafted as "every rule that applies to a Run applies to it". The movement is made during the boss's turn; whether it stops the character from taking the Rest major action in their own next turn (§10.1) is not stated and is left to the general rule, under which it does not. Confirm.
- Great Grey Wolf Sif (§17.4): the data card was not supplied. The entry names the cool-down card Limping Strike and says it is used only as the data card directs; the trigger, and whether the card is set aside when the deck is built, are unknown.
- Artorias (§17.4): "remove two random behaviour cards" is drafted as two cards removed from the behaviour deck and its discard pile taken together, then all three heat-up cards added and the whole shuffled. Confirm that the discard pile is included.
- Old Dragonslayer (§17.6): the approved CMP-0005 draft added "This is in addition to the ordinary heat-up at his heat-up point." The sentence is omitted because the data card could not be read: with eight behaviour cards, a deck size of four, and three heat-up cards placed by Corrupted Dragonslayer, the card may show no heat-up point. If it shows one, the general rule in §15.5 applies without further text.
- Black Dragon Kalameet (§17.8): Kalameet keeps its previous facing when placed on the landing node (CMP-0005 completion; no source states the facing). The official step that reveals behaviour cards for gravestones in the Level 4 encounter is dropped, as CMP-0005 directs, because the scry rule replaces it.
- Executioner's Chariot (§17.9): (a) the two models are the skeletal horse and the chariot, as the expansion defines them; the chapter instruction's "chariot and rider" was read in that sense. (b) Before the heat-up, every enemy model takes a turn after each character's turn (CMP-0005's recasting of the alternating activation); this is a severe difficulty rule and wants a playtest. (c) The aggro token before the heat-up is not ruled on: under §16.4 it moves only when the boss is damaged, which cannot happen before the heat-up, so it stays with its holder unless the holder dies. The author may prefer that damage to a Black Hollow Mage or Falchion Skeleton moves it. (d) The Mages and Skeletons are stated to scale with the number of characters under §8.3. (e) The models' facing at setup is left to §15.2. (f) "The Chariot does not heat up at a heat-up point" and "the skeletal horse does not heat up again" follow CMP-0005; the heat-up side of the data card was not legible. (g) Crush is stated not to apply to a dodged attack.
- Raise Skeleton (§17.9, Unique Icons): the rule is owned here although the icon also appears in Level 1 to 3 encounters that contain a Black Hollow Mage. "Killed during the encounter" limits the choice to skeletons of the current encounter.

## Cross-chapter dependencies created by §17

- §8.4 must list the displacement of one boss model by another as a cause of a push, and must state that no character's movement or attack pushes a boss model (§17.1 cites §8.4 for both; the chapter 05 issues list raises the same point).
- §8.5 must own the effect and duration of calamity, corrosion, frostbite, and stagger; §17.3, §17.8, and §17.10 only cite it.
- §9.4 and §9.5 must cover a defence against an attack that targets nodes (strafe, track behaviour cards) no differently from any other attack; §9.5 must state the move after a successful dodge, which §17.1 relies on for a dodge difficulty of 0.
- §13 owns every mimic rule (no arcs, heat-up with an unused standard card, two treasure cards); §17.6 only cites it.
- §15.2 must state how a boss model faces when placed (cited for the second Gargoyle) and how a failed boss encounter is resolved. §15.3 must state that the joined boss tiles carry both the mini-boss and the main-boss spawn node. §15.5 must allow that some bosses build their decks or heat up differently "(see §17)". §15.6 must own the weak-arc bonus.
- §16.1 must allow the behaviour deck to be renewed whenever a card is due and the deck is empty, not only at the start of a boss turn (the Pursuer's second card, §17.6). §16.2 must state where a character is placed when a boss moves onto their node. §16.3 must define the area of an area attack (range and attack arcs), which §17.3 uses to find the pillars destroyed. §16.5 must not award anything when the first of the Twin Gargoyles is defeated; a sentence citing §17.1, Twin Gargoyles is enough.
- §19: each section that lists the Gargoyle twice (The First Journey, Ambush Lies Ahead, Demon Hunting) must mark the pair as the Twin Gargoyles and cite §17.1. §17.8 cites §19.1 for the campaign section that ends with Kalameet, as the chapter instructions direct; the manuscript's The First Journey has no such section, so §19.1 must add it (bonfire tile, two Level 3 encounters, a Level 4 encounter, Black Dragon Kalameet) or the reference in §17.8 must change. §17.9 cites §19.9 (The Undead Colosseum) for the Chariot.
- Appendix B must list the replacement data cards and behaviour cards for the Black Knight, Gravelord Nito, Crossbreed Priscilla, and the Heavy Knight, which §17.7 and §17.10 cite.

## Glossary entries owned by other chapters

- **Heat-up** ends "Some bosses heat up differently; see Boss Special Rules." Its owner (§15.5) must change this to "(see §17)"; it is not amended here to avoid two chapters replacing the same entry.
- **Boss** calls a boss "a single powerful enemy"; Ornstein & Smough is one boss with two models (§17.1). The owner (§15) may wish to add "fought with one model or, for some bosses, more than one".
- **Calamity** (owner §8.5) is unchanged and agrees with §17.8.
