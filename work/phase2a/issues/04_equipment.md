# Open issues — chapter 04 Equipment

- Shield definition (B-022, applied in §4.1, Card Types) assumes no printed shield card carries an attack option. If any shield card or Appendix B amendment (for example a shield with a push effect) gives a shield an attack line, the author must confirm and the test "has no attack option" must be replaced.
- Destination of a consumed ember card is not stated anywhere in the manuscript. §4.4 says the character "discards" it; the author must confirm whether it is removed from the game or returned to the tier one treasure deck or shop. §7.3 (Embering) owns the consequence and must match.
- Cross-chapter dependency: §6.3 Shop Setup must hold A-046 in full (ember cards added at setup only, one per character; unsold ember cards stay in the shop; no ember card is added at restock). §4.4 cites §6.3 for this.
- Cross-chapter dependency: §9.4 Block and Resist must state D-033 and A-120 (a fixed defence modifier is applied after the two highest dice are summed); §10.3 states the attack modifier. §4.1 says only that modifiers exist and cites both.
- Cross-chapter dependency: §11.4 Blacksmith Andre must cite §4.2 for upgrade stat requirements, the one-card-per-slot limit, Titanite permanence, and the return of upgrades when equipment is sold, and §4.4 for ember purchase, rather than restating them.
- §4.3 states that a character who does not meet a stored spell's stat requirements cannot use it. This is inferred from "as if equipped" (CMP-0016); the author must confirm.
- §4.3 states that spells are never two-handed, including a spell equipped with the other weapon slot empty (preserve list). If any spell card bears the two-handed icon, the author must confirm it still occupies one slot.
- Glossary: "Attack (major action)" is added here because the chapter italicises *Attack*; the §10 drafter is likely to add the same headword and the two must be reconciled.
