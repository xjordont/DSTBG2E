# Issues — chapter 08 (Combat Basics)

- Frostbite duration (§8.5, Frostbite): Gate 1 OQ-16 names stagger only, but the approved fix of A-078 (REQUIRED) covers "a stagger or frostbite token". The chapter removes both at the end of the affected model's next complete turn. The outcome differs from the manuscript only when a frostbite token is placed during the model's own turn (a riposte with a frostbite weapon). The author must confirm the frostbite half.
- Conditions on bosses (§8.5; B-038): the chapter states that any model, including a boss and a phantom, may hold condition tokens, so a second bleed token deals three damage to a boss. B-038 offered the opposite ruling (bosses immune to bleed) as an alternative. The author must confirm.
- Staggered enemies (§8.5, Stagger; A-079): an enemy, boss, or phantom reduces its attack values, its block and resist values, and its dodge difficulty by one, and no value falls below zero. The floor of zero is a clarification with no manuscript source.
- Bleed and poison damage "is not an attack" (§8.5): this wording keeps the embered reduction of §7.3 (three or more damage "from a single attack") from applying to the three bleed damage. If the author intends an embered character to suffer two, §7.3 must say so. Whether condition damage counts towards moving the aggro token (§7.5, Encounter Turn Order) is likewise unstated.
- Push direction (§8.4): the manuscript's "most directly opposite" node is kept, with A-085's fallback. A model is pushed only to a valid node farther from the attacker than its own node, measured by range, so a model on a corner node (and any model with only sideways nodes free) is not pushed. The official text allows any farther node of the players' choice; the author must confirm the stricter rule.
- Push-move damage (§8.4): per the chapter instructions §8.4 owns the damage of a push-move (the satellite glyph), which A-023 had placed under Enemy Attacks. §9.2 and §9.3 must cite §8.4 and not restate it, and §9.3 must own the rest of A-023 and D-008: the skull, nearest-character, and node icons, the tie-breaks, and the out-of-range miss. §8.1 states only that an out-of-range target is not hit.
- Repeat on enemy data cards (§8.2): the glossary entry Repeat covers attack options and behaviour cards only. §8.2 says the repeat icon "shows the number of times the behaviour is resolved (see §9.3)"; §9.3 must state how a printed repeat icon on an enemy data card is resolved, and the owner of the glossary entry must widen it.
- Dodge and push (§8.4; §9.5): §8.4 states that a model is pushed from the node it occupies when the push is resolved, and that a character who dodges the attack of a push-move is not pushed. §9.5 must state whether the dodge movement happens before or after the roll; if a failed dodge still moves the character, the push is resolved from the new node.
- Summons and push-moves (§8.4; §12.1): the push-move procedure pushes "each character" on the enemy's node and on the node it enters. Whether a summon on those nodes is pushed and attacked as a character is left to §12.1, as is the targeting of phantoms (§8.1 cites §12).
- Scaling exemptions (§8.3; A-028): bosses, mimics, phantoms, and crystal lizards are stated never to be scaled. The mimic is inferred from its health dial and its mini-boss encounter (§13 must agree). §18.7 must state that the bonfire ascetic raises the health only of enemies that are subject to enemy scaling, so that invaders, mimics, and crystal lizards are unaffected, and §18.4 must state from when a joining or leaving character counts (A-027).
- Cross-chapter dependencies: §10.1 must own stamina overflow (stagger; a second stagger token is not placed, A-009) and must cite §8.5 for the frostbitten character who recovers no stamina and cannot Rest (A-010). §9.6 must own the stagger placed by a parry. §10.3 must own the damage calculation and cite §8.2 for wound tokens and the kill. §15.4 must describe the behaviour deck size, heat-up point, and health dial. §15.6 and §16.2 must own the arc limit on a push off a boss's node and boss push-moves. §17.1 must own the push between two boss models. §17.8 and §17.10 must name the attacks that place calamity and corrosion. §20.5 must own pushes and conditions between characters (A-032).
- Wound token values (§8.2): the tokens have values of 1, 3, and 5, so the chapter counts their total value. §7.5, Encounter Turn Order breaks an enemy tie by "the most wound tokens" and counts "wound tokens placed" for the aggro token; both read better as the total value of wound tokens.
- Glossary: Attack value says an enemy's attack value is "the fixed number printed in the attack icon"; §8.4 also treats the damage value of a push-move as an attack value, and the owner of that entry (§9.3) must add it. The entries Hit, Target, Data card, Wound token, Push, Condition, and the six conditions are amended in this chapter's delta.
- Example values: the examples take the Hollow Soldier's base health of 1, movement value of 1, attack value of 4, and block of 1 from the audit evidence and the voice guide. The author must confirm them against the printed card. The examples in §8.1 and §8.4 use an unnamed enemy because no supplied text gives the push icons of a named enemy.
- Icons: the push icon is tagged with `ast-0058-push-affected-enemies` in §8.4 (the character-attack glyph) and left in words on the enemy data card in §8.2; the skull icon, the node icon, and the calamity and corrosion icons have no slug in the extracted set. Layout must confirm.
- A-081 (OPTIONAL) was not applied: the chapter sets no supply limit on poison tokens. The author may prefer the limit of the seven tokens supplied.
