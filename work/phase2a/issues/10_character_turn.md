# Issues — chapter 10 (Character Turn)

- Scaling requirement (§10.5; OQ-19): the Gate 1 answer speaks of "the attack option's highest requirement", but stat requirements are printed on the card, not on each attack option. The chapter compares the character's stat with the highest of the four requirements printed on the weapon card. The requirements of attached upgrade cards are not counted. The author must confirm both points.
- Weapons with no requirement (§10.5; A-066, OPTIONAL, not applied): as the rule stands, a weapon whose highest printed requirement is zero is scaled for any character whose stat is five or more. A-066 would exclude such weapons, which alters the preserved scaling mechanic outside the changes signed off at Gate 1. The author must decide.
- Tied requirements (§10.5): the manuscript says "use either". The chapter lets the character choose the relevant stat among the tied stats, so a weapon is scaled if either tied stat exceeds the requirement by five.
- Scaling marker (§10.5): the red cube is taken from the red (damage) cubes, the same component §3.3 uses to mark stat tier four. §2.8 lists no separate marker; the author must confirm.
- Rerolls on one roll (§10.3): the manuscript allows "any re-rolls available". The chapter reads this as allowing both the two-handed reroll and the luck token on the same attack roll, in either order, and does not forbid rerolling one die twice. The author must confirm.
- Actions resolved in full (§10.1): OQ-26 allows the minor actions and the major action in any order. The chapter treats each action as a unit, so a character cannot walk or run between the two attacks of one Attack action. This is an inference; if a minor action is allowed between the two attacks, one sentence in §10.1 is deleted.
- Repeat (§10.3, Repeat): "that entire weapon option" is read as every step after payment, so each resolution has its own shift, target, roll, and two-handed reroll, and the stamina cost is paid once. No source states the per-resolution target. §15.6 must state that the weak arc die is added to each roll of a repeated attack (A-037); the chapter 20 draft already treats each use as a separate attack.
- Attack with no target (§10.3, Range): the chapter keeps the manuscript's order (pay the stamina, then choose the target) and adds that an attack with no permitted target within range cannot be made. No source states this.
- Fixed modifiers and the floor (§10.3): a fixed modifier is applied once to the total after rerolls, and an attack value below zero counts as zero. The floor has no manuscript source; it matches the chapter 09 draft's treatment of the defence total.
- Targets (§10.3): the chapter lists enemies, bosses, invaders, mimics, and crystal lizards as the targets of a character's attacks and calls them all "enemy" within the section. The chapter 08 draft (§8.1) lists enemies, bosses, and invaders; §13 and §14 must confirm that mimics and crystal lizards are attacked under §10.3.
- Dependencies met by the drafts now on disk: §8.5 (frostbite stops the start-of-turn recovery and the Rest major action; stagger subtracts one from the attack roll total and is placed by stamina overflow), §9.6 (shield in the left weapon slot, riposte with the weapon in the right weapon slot, riposte not counted against the turn's attacks), and §20.3 to §20.4 (PvP attacks use the §10.3 attack value). If any of these change, step 1 of §10.1, step 8 and the riposte paragraph of §10.3, and the parry sentence of §10.4 must follow.
- Dependencies still open: §8.5 must state every effect that step 3 of the turn procedure resolves at the end of a character's turn (poison damage, removal of stagger and frostbite). §12.1 must state the summon's turn after each character's turn in a boss encounter (OQ-32). §15.4 and §15.6 must own the health dial, the boss arcs, and the weak arc black die, which §10.3 only cites.
- Wound tokens (§10.3): step 10 and the examples speak of the total value of wound tokens, because §2.8 supplies tokens of value 1, 3, and 5 and the chapter 08 draft (§8.2) does the same. The glossary entry Wound token is left to chapter 08's delta.
- Glossary, Attack value: this chapter and the chapter 09 delta both amend the entry. This chapter's text replaces "sword icons" with the values rolled (as §2.7 describes the dice) and keeps chapter 09's sentence for enemies; the lead editor must keep one merged entry.
- Glossary, other entries: Frostbite and Stagger are left to chapter 08's delta. "Attack (major action)" stands as chapter 04 added it. New headwords are Drink Estus (major action) and Relevant stat. Turn is amended to remove the word "activation".
- Italics: "major action" and "minor action" are left plain where they follow an italicised action name ("the *Attack* major action"), as chapters 04 and 07 do.
- Example values: the block and resist values given to the Silver Knight Swordsman and the Hollow Soldier, and the stat requirements given to the great axe, rapier, and sword, are illustrative. They must be checked against the data cards and Appendix A at proof.
- Catalysts and two-handed wielding (§10.4): the chapter denies the two-handed reroll to spell attack options, including those of a spell stored in a catalyst. Whether a catalyst card that prints attack options of its own gains the reroll when it is the only card in the weapon slots is not addressed by any source.
- Forced stamina (§10.1): no rule in this chapter obliges a character to place a stamina cube against their will. The stagger-on-overflow rule is kept for card effects and for other chapters, as the preserve list requires.
