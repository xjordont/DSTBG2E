# Issues — chapter 07 (Gameplay)

- Encounter scry (§7.5, Encounter Resolution): the manuscript does not say where a replaced encounter card goes; the chapter places it at the bottom of the same-level encounter deck by analogy with the treasure and boss-deck scries. The author must confirm, and §6.5 must keep the undealt encounter cards of each level as face-down decks beside the bonfire for this to work.
- Boss-behaviour scry (§7.5, Encounter Resolution): the manuscript allows the next boss's behaviour deck to be scried after any gravestone encounter, but §15.5 builds the deck at the start of the boss encounter. §15.5 must allow the deck to be built (or its top card read) before the encounter, or the author must drop this scry option.
- Enemy turn-order tie-breaks (nearest, most wound tokens, players' choice) are owned here (§7.5, Encounter Turn Order); §9.1 must cite §7.5 rather than restate them (cut/simplify list).
- The scry procedure is stated under §7.5, Encounter Resolution, per the chapter instructions; §5.5, Gravestones must keep only the trigger and "(see §7.5, Encounter Resolution)". The glossary entry Scry is amended accordingly.
- A-007 (an ember card held by a character who becomes embered) is stated in §7.3; §4.4 must cite it. Where a discarded ember card goes (game box or shop) is stated nowhere in the manuscript and is left to §4.4.
- Assignment of the pre-dealt per-character treasure cards (§7.7): the chapter has the players agree and, on disagreement, the aggro holder decide the assignment. This extends OQ-29 to a case the manuscript does not cover; the author may prefer a random deal.
- Whether a killed invader is required for an encounter to be cleared, and which character receives invader treasure, are left to §12.2.
- Level 4 encounters reset like any other encounter (A-123 records this as a departure); §6.7 and §11.5 must not contradict it.
- The revived character's placement ("upright on the tile beside the surviving characters") leaves the node to the players; the author may wish to require a node within the node model limit (§5.3).
- Hollow cubes (§7.2): the component's colour and source remain unconfirmed (see the chapter 02 issues list).

Resolved at integration (lead editor): the scry procedure now lives only in §7.5 (§5.5 trimmed, CH-05-033); a cleared Level 4 encounter does not reset (CMP-0023; CH-07-049), and §11.5 must state the same exception.
