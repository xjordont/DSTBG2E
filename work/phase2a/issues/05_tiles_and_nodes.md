# Issues — chapter 05 (Tiles and Nodes)

- Traps and enemies (§5.5, Traps): the two approved findings disagree. A-083's proposed fix has enemies on the node suffer the trap's damage without rolling; D-009's proposed fix (the restored rule) has enemies neither trigger nor suffer traps, which A-083 also lists as its alternative. The chapter follows D-009. The author must confirm.
- Summons and traps (§5.5, Traps): no source states whether a summon triggers or suffers a trap. The chapter states that only characters trigger and are affected by traps, so summons and invaders are excluded. The author must confirm.
- Scry count (§5.5, Gravestones): the manuscript grants one scry for "an encounter containing a gravestone". The chapter states one scry per cleared encounter however many gravestones the tile holds. The author must confirm that two gravestones on one tile do not grant two scries.
- Scry of an encounter card (§5.5, Gravestones): the manuscript does not say where a replaced encounter card goes; the chapter sends it face down to the bottom of the encounter deck of its level. The author must confirm.
- Boss behaviour scry (§5.5, Gravestones; A-043): the chapter states that each boss's behaviour deck is built at setup and kept face down on its boss tile (see §6.5; §15.5). §6.5 and §15.5 must build the deck at that point, or the boss behaviour scry has no deck to reveal.
- Trap dealing exclusion (§5.5, Traps): the manuscript excludes basic nodes "along a tile edge adjacent to another tile"; the official component text excludes nodes along a wall that has a doorway. The chapter keeps the manuscript's exclusion. The two differ only for a doorway that leads nowhere.
- Trap token faces (§5.5, Traps): the chapter states that a trap token is blank or shows a dodge difficulty and a damage value (A-033 evidence). §2.8 must describe the token the same way.
- Entry nodes (§5.1): the chapter defines entry nodes as the basic nodes adjacent to the doorway through which the characters entered (glossary wording). The manuscript's Encounter Start step 3 says "the basic nodes along the wall adjacent to the previous encounter tile"; §7.5 must use the §5.1 definition.
- Main-boss tile adjacency (§5.1): the chapter states that the two boss tiles joined for a main-boss encounter form one tile, so nodes across the join are adjacent. §15.3 must state the join the same way.
- Tile boundary (§5.2): the rule that no model leaves the encounter's tile or passes through a doorway during an encounter is owned here; §10.2 and §9.2 must cite §5.2 rather than restate it. Phantom arrival (§12) must state where an invader or summon is placed, since models do not enter through doorways during an encounter.
- Overcrowding push (§5.3): the displacement caused by the node model limit is a push resolved under §8.4 to a valid node chosen by the players. The glossary entry **Push** (owned by §8.4) lists only push-attacks and push-moves as causes; §8.4 must add the node model limit (and the two-boss displacement of §17.1) as a cause, or the glossary entry must be amended by its owner.
- Enemy pathing around terrain (§5.5): the chapter states that an enemy or boss moves around blocking terrain "choosing its path as §9.2 or §16.2 describes". §9.2 and §16.2 must state what an enemy or boss does when the node its icon points to is not a valid node.
- Chest opening (§5.5, Chests): the chapter states only that a chest is opened and awarded when its encounter is cleared, as §7.7 describes, and under §13 with mimics; §7.7 must state the opening step, the aggro-holder tie-break (OQ-29), and that the pre-dealt card is the reward (OQ-11).
- Node icons (§5.1): the icon set distinguishes primary and secondary spawn nodes and terrain nodes (from the encounter card legend); the chapter attaches the primary icons to "spawn node" and "terrain node" and does not describe a primary/secondary distinction, which no supplied rule uses. Layout must confirm the icon choice.
