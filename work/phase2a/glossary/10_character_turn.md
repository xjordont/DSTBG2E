# Glossary deltas — chapter 10 (Character Turn)

ADD **Drink Estus (major action).** The major action in which a character flips the *estus flask* token to its used side and removes every damage cube from their endurance bar. It may be taken only while the token shows its ready side (see §3.5, Estus Flask; §10.1).
ADD **Relevant stat.** The stat whose requirement is the highest of the four stat requirements printed on a weapon card; if two or more requirements are tied for the highest, the character chooses one of the tied stats. A weapon is *scaled* by comparing the character's relevant stat with that requirement (see §10.5).
AMEND **Area-of-effect (AoE).** A character attack option marked with the area-of-effect icon. It targets one node within range: one attack roll is compared with the block or resist of every enemy on that node, and every enemy there is hit (see §10.3, Area-of-Effect).
AMEND **Attack value.** The total of the values rolled on an attack's dice after rerolls, with any fixed modifier applied once to the total, plus one for a *scaled* weapon and minus one for a *staggered* attacker, to a minimum of zero (see §10.3). For enemies the attack value is the fixed number printed in the attack icon, scaled for the number of characters (see §9.3).
AMEND **Equip.** A minor action, once per turn, in which a character exchanges any number of cards among the back-up slot, the two weapon slots, and the armour slot. It is the only way to change equipped cards during an encounter; the inventory cannot be accessed (see §10.1).
AMEND **Major action.** The one principal action of a character's turn: *Attack*, *Rest*, *Drink Estus*, or *Ember*. A character may take one major action per turn (see §10.1).
AMEND **Minor action.** An action a character may take during their turn before the major action, after it, or both: *Walk*, *Equip*, and *Heroic Action* once each per turn, and *Run* any number of times (see §10.1).
AMEND **Repeat.** An icon that causes an entire attack option or behaviour card to be resolved the number of times shown. For a character's attack option the stamina cost is paid once and each resolution is a separate attack roll with its own target (see §10.3, Repeat).
AMEND **Rest (major action).** The character removes every stamina cube from their endurance bar; damage cubes, hollow cubes, and condition tokens stay. A character cannot *Rest* in a turn in which they have *run*, cannot *run* after resting in the same turn, and cannot *Rest* while holding a *frostbite* token (see §10.1).
AMEND **Run.** A minor action: the character spends one stamina and moves one node. It may be taken any number of times per turn. A character who has *run* cannot *Rest* in that turn (see §10.2).
AMEND **Scaled (weapon).** A weapon equipped by a character whose *relevant stat* exceeds the weapon's highest printed stat requirement by five or more. It is marked with a red cube and each of its attack options adds one to its attack value. Spells are never scaled (see §10.5).
AMEND **Shaft.** A weapon icon. An attack option with the shaft icon cannot target a model on the attacker's own node (see §10.3, Shaft).
AMEND **Shift.** A weapon or phantom behaviour icon. A character whose attack option bears it may move up to the number of nodes shown, before the target is chosen if the icon precedes the dice and after the attack's damage and effects if it follows them. A *shift* is a *move*, costs no stamina, and does not use the *Walk* or *Run* minor action (see §10.3, Shift).
AMEND **Turn.** One model's opportunity to act. In each round the enemies take their turns, then the characters take theirs in descending threat order; in boss encounters the boss and the characters alternate turns (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §10.1).
AMEND **Two-handed.** A weapon bearing the two-handed icon, or a one-handed weapon equipped while the other weapon slot holds no card; a shield in the other weapon slot is equipment in the other hand. A weapon wielded two-handed may reroll one die of each attack roll. Spells are never two-handed (see §10.4).
AMEND **Walk.** A minor action, once per turn: the character moves one node without spending stamina (see §10.2).
