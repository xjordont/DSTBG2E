AMEND **Threat.** The value that fixes turn order, printed on the character dashboard as its Taunt Level and on enemy, boss, and phantom data cards. Enemies act in descending threat before the characters, who then act in descending threat. The character with the highest threat takes the aggro token at the start of each encounter; the players settle ties (see §3.1; §7.5, Encounter Turn Order).
AMEND **Invader.** A hostile phantom brought into one encounter by an *invasion*. An invader acts after each character's turn using its own behaviour deck, is not scaled, counts towards clearing the encounter while it is on the tile, vanishes without award if a character dies, and awards *souls* and its treasure card when killed (see §12.2).
AMEND **Behaviour card.** A card drawn from the behaviour deck of a boss, mimic, invader, or summon at the start of that model's turn. Its icons are resolved from left to right. A boss's current behaviour card sets the range and the dodge difficulty of its attacks and shows its weak arcs until the next card is drawn (see §15.5; §16.1).
AMEND **Shift.** An icon found on attack options and on summon behaviour cards. A character whose attack option bears it may move up to the number of nodes shown, before the target is chosen if the icon precedes the dice and after the attack's damage and effects if it follows them. A *shift* is a *move*, costs no stamina, and does not use the *Walk* or *Run* minor action (see §10.3, Shift; §12.1).
AMEND **Encounter.** A battle fought on one or more tiles against the enemies set out by an encounter card, or against a boss. An encounter ends *cleared* or *failed* (see §7.5); a PvP encounter ends in neither way (see §20.7).
AMEND **Boss.** A single powerful enemy whose turns are driven by a behaviour deck and whose base is divided into four arcs. There are three ranks: *mini-boss*, *main-boss*, and *mega-boss*. A boss is not scaled for the number of characters and cannot be pushed by characters (see §15; §16).
AMEND **Embered.** The state of a character with an *ember token*. An embered character is not *hollow*, reduces by one the damage of any attack that deals three or more, may call a *summon* before a boss encounter, and provokes *invasions*. The state ends when the character dies (see §7.3).
AMEND **Treasure card.** Any card from a treasure deck or from Blacksmith Andre's shop: *starting equipment*, *common treasure*, *class-specific treasure*, *legendary treasure*, *boss treasure*, or an *ember card*. A treasure card that may be equipped is an *equipment card* (see §2.5; §4.1).
AMEND **Main-boss.** The second boss of the standard game, fought on both boss tiles joined as one after the mini-boss is defeated. Its defeat wins the standard game unless a mega-boss is in play (see §6.6; §15.3).
AMEND **Mini-boss.** The first boss of the standard game, fought on the mini-boss tile (see §6.5; §15.3).
AMEND **Hollow.** The state of a character revived after dying. Two *hollow cubes* block the two rightmost spaces of the endurance bar, leaving eight. Hollow is removed only by resting at the *bonfire* or by becoming *embered*. A hollow character who dies is *lost*, and the encounter is *failed* (see §7.2).
AMEND **Dropped souls.** *Souls* placed on an encounter tile by a character who died there or, when an encounter was *failed*, by a living character. Only the character who dropped them may reclaim them, by surviving to clear that encounter; any still on the tiles when a boss is defeated are returned to the characters who dropped them. They are returned to the supply if that character dies first (see §7.6; §6.6).
AMEND **Shop.** Blacksmith Andre's stock of face-up treasure cards, in a tier one shop and a tier two shop. A tier one card costs two *souls* and a tier two card four; a tier one or tier two treasure card sells for one soul and a boss treasure card for six, and starting equipment cannot be sold (see §11.4). The tier one shop is stocked at setup and the tier two shop when the mini-boss is defeated; both are restocked only after a boss is defeated (in campaign play, after a section is completed), unsold cards going to the bottom of their deck first (see §6.3).
AMEND **Tier two treasure.** The treasure deck that holds common and class-specific treasure with a stat requirement of 25 or more, all transposed treasure, and the five legendary cards. Encounter rewards and chests draw from tier two once the mini-boss is defeated (in campaign play, once the *bonfire ascetic* is burnt) (see §6.2).
AMEND **Twin Gargoyles.** The boss encounter set by a campaign *section* that lists the Gargoyle as fought twice: one mini-boss encounter in two parts, fought against one Gargoyle and then at once against a second, with no rest or reward between them. The rewards of a defeated boss follow only when the second Gargoyle is defeated, and a failure against either Gargoyle means both are fought again (see §17.1, Twin Gargoyles).
AMEND **Hollow cube.** A white cube, taken from the white cubes that are not in use as stat cubes, placed on an endurance bar. Two hollow cubes block the two rightmost spaces of a *hollow* character's endurance bar (see §2.8, §7.2).
AMEND **Ember card.** A tier one shop card. Discarding an *ember* card from the back-up slot (the *Ember* major action, or outside an encounter) makes the character *embered*, and the card is returned to the game box (see §7.3). A character may carry only one *ember* card and cannot carry one while *embered*; a card held when the character becomes *embered* passes to another character (see §7.3).
AMEND **Titanite.** A weapon upgrade card whose name includes the word Titanite (Titanite Shard, Titanite Chunk, Titanite Slab, Titanite Scale, Demon Titanite, and Blue Titanite), fused permanently to the weapon it is attached to. A Titanite Chunk is attached only in place of an attached Titanite Shard, and a Titanite Slab only in place of an attached Titanite Chunk; the replaced card returns to the bottom of the treasure deck of its tier (see Appendix B). When that weapon is sold, the Titanite is sold with it for no additional *souls*, and each card goes where a sold card of its kind goes (see §4.2; §11.4).
AMEND **Bonfire.** The characters' safe haven, represented by the *bonfire* tile, which has no nodes. The Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre wait there and are visited only while the party rests. The party returns to the bonfire tile only to rest, and a rest costs one *spark* unless it follows the defeat of a boss (see §11.1, §11.2).
AMEND **Gravestone.** A terrain token or model. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into a node containing a gravestone. When an encounter is *cleared*, the players may *scry* one card for each gravestone on its tile (see §5.5, Gravestones; §7.5, Encounter Resolution).
AMEND **Entry node.** A basic node along the tile edge that holds the doorway through which the characters entered the tile. Characters are placed on entry nodes at the start of an encounter, no more than three to a node (see §5.1; §7.5, Encounter Start). In a Level 4 encounter the four basic nodes along that edge are the entry nodes (see §6.7).
AMEND **Basic node.** The most common node type, printed in yellow. Trap tokens are dealt to basic nodes, and the basic nodes along the edge through which the characters entered a tile are its entry nodes (see §5.1).
AMEND **Doorway.** A gap in a tile's wall through which the characters pass from one tile to the next. No model passes through a doorway during an encounter, and the basic nodes along the edge of the doorway by which the characters entered are the entry nodes (see §5.1; §5.2).
AMEND **Repeat.** An icon that causes an entire attack option, or the whole row of behaviour icons on an enemy data card or a behaviour card, to be resolved more than once. A character may resolve the attack option up to the number of times shown: the stamina cost is paid once, and each resolution is a separate attack roll with its own target (see §10.3, Repeat). An enemy or a boss resolves its row of icons the number of times shown (see §9.1; §16.1).
AMEND **Push.** Forced movement of a model from its node to an adjacent valid node. A push is caused by a push-attack, by a push-move, by the node model limit, by a boss model moving onto the node of another boss model, or by a rule of a boss such as a leap. A push is not a move and costs no stamina; a gravestone, a chest, and a pillar token block it, and a push with no valid node does not happen (see §8.4).
AMEND **Condition.** A lasting effect marked by a condition token on a model: *bleed*, *poison*, *frostbite*, *stagger*, *calamity*, or *corrosion*. A condition token is placed on each model hit by an attack that bears the matching icon, after the attack's damage and any push have been resolved. A model holds at most one token of each condition, except poison (see §8.5).
AMEND **Parry.** A defence available to a character with a shield equipped in either weapon slot when the attacker is on the character's node; an attack that pushes, a node attack, and a boss attack outside §15.6 cannot be parried. The character pays one stamina and rolls dodge dice against the attack's dodge difficulty. Success makes the attack miss, staggers the attacker, and allows a *riposte*; failure deals the full attack value with no block or resist roll and staggers the character (see §9.6).
AMEND **Riposte.** The attack a character may make at once against the attacker after a successful *parry*, using one attack option of the weapon in the other weapon slot, never a spell, and paying its stamina cost. A riposte cannot be parried, and it does not count against the attacks of the character's own turn (see §9.6).
AMEND **Scaled (weapon).** A weapon equipped by a character whose *relevant stat* exceeds the weapon's highest printed stat requirement by five or more. It is marked with a red cube and each of its attack options adds one to its attack value. A weapon whose stat requirements are all zero is never scaled, and spells are never scaled (see §10.5).
AMEND **Attack value.** The total of the values rolled on an attack's dice after rerolls, with any fixed modifier applied once to the total, plus one for a *scaled* weapon and minus one for a *staggered* attacker, to a minimum of zero (see §10.3). For enemies the attack value is the fixed number printed in the attack icon, or the damage value of a push-move, scaled for the number of characters and reduced by one while the enemy is *staggered* (see §8.3; §8.4; §9.3).
AMEND **Magical damage.** Damage from an attack that bears the magical damage icon. Magical damage is reduced by resist (see §9.3; §10.3).
AMEND **Physical damage.** Damage from an enemy attack marked with the crossed swords icon, or from a character's attack option that does not bear the magical damage icon. Physical damage is reduced by block (see §9.3; §10.3).
AMEND **Bonfire ascetic.** In campaign play, a one-time communal payment of 16 *souls* made while resting at the *bonfire*. With the cost of a bought *spark*, it is one of the two exceptions to the rule that souls cannot be pooled. It opens tier two treasure and the tier two shop and increases by two the base health of every enemy subject to enemy scaling for the rest of the campaign, cumulative with the health scaling for the number of characters. Enemy attack values are not changed (see §18.7).
AMEND **Transposition.** In campaign play, exchanging one boss treasure card and four *souls* for one of the five face-up legendary treasure cards while resting at the *bonfire*. It is available once the first section is completed, and the boss treasure card is returned to the game box (see §18.8).
AMEND **Fog gate.** The token that marks the doorway of a boss encounter. A boss encounter begins when the characters pass through it. The first time they pass through, the token is moved to the *bonfire* tile, where it is a shortcut to that boss until the boss is defeated (see §15.1). In campaign play it only marks the doorway of the boss encounter: it is not moved and gives no shortcut (see §18.2).
AMEND **Campaign.** A game played through a fixed list of *sections*, taken in order, in which the characters keep what they have gained from one section to the next. The book supplies 16 campaigns; campaign play follows the standard game except as its own chapter states (see §18.1, §19).
AMEND **Invader token.** A token from the Phantoms expansion that shows an invader or is blank. When an *invasion* is provoked, one token is placed face down on each face-down encounter card that carries no black cube, one of them showing an invader; a token is revealed when the characters fight that encounter (see §12.2).
AMEND **Invasion.** The placing of invader tokens that brings one invader into the game. An invasion is provoked when the encounter cards are dealt while a character is *embered*, or when a character becomes embered by discarding an *ember card* while a face-down encounter card that carries no black cube lies on a tile; no more than one is provoked before the mini-boss is defeated and no more than one after it (see §12.2).
AMEND **Weak arc.** A boss arc shown in red on the arc diagram of the boss's current behaviour card, the card it drew most recently. A character or a *summon* that attacks a boss from a weak arc adds one black die to each attack roll, however many weak arcs the attacker is in (see §15.6).
AMEND **Turn.** One model's opportunity to act. In each round the enemies take their turns, then the characters take theirs in descending threat order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §10.1). In a boss encounter the boss takes a turn before each character's turn, and the players choose the order of the characters (see §16.1). An *invader* takes a turn after each character's turn, and so does a *summon* in a boss encounter (see §12).
AMEND **Crystal lizard.** An optional enemy from the Iron Keep expansion, placed on a terrain node that the encounter card marks with the crystal lizard symbol. It flees rather than fights, is not scaled, does not count towards clearing the encounter, and escapes without award when the encounter is *cleared*. Killing one awards the party a Titanite Scale card, and once one has been killed in an encounter none is placed there again (see §14).
AMEND **Mimic encounter.** The encounter fought against a *mimic* on the tile of a *cleared* encounter whose chest the party has opened. It is fought as a mini-boss encounter without arcs, with the characters on the nodes they occupy. The characters remove their stamina cubes before the mimic's first turn; the recovery and the rewards of the cleared encounter wait until the mimic is killed (see §13.2).
AMEND **Victory bonus.** The reward for winning a *PvP encounter* that follows the defeat of a boss other than the final boss, marked by a spare token placed beside the winner's dashboard. It adds one to the attack value of each of the character's attacks in every subsequent PvP encounter. A character holds no more than one victory bonus, and it is kept until the game ends (see §20.8).
AMEND **Boss.** A powerful enemy whose turns are driven by a behaviour deck and whose base is divided into four arcs. Most bosses are fought with one model; Ornstein & Smough and the Four Kings are fought with several boss models, each with its own health dial (see §17.1). There are three ranks: *mini-boss*, *main-boss*, and *mega-boss*. A boss is not scaled for the number of characters and cannot be pushed by characters (see §15; §16).
AMEND **Heat-up.** The event in which a boss, mimic, or invader becomes more dangerous. When a boss's health is reduced to or below its heat-up point, its behaviour deck and discard pile are gathered at the start of its next turn and shuffled together with one heat-up card taken at random to form a new behaviour deck (see §15.5); a mimic or an invader adds one of its unused behaviour cards instead. Some bosses heat up differently (see §17).
AMEND **Signature card.** A behaviour card that is included in every behaviour deck built for its boss: Black Dragon Kalameet's Mark of Calamity and Hellfire Blast, the Gaping Dragon's two Stomach Slam cards, and the Old Iron King's three Fire Beam cards (see §17.8; §17.10).
AMEND **Wind Up.** A behaviour icon printed on behaviour cards of the Asylum Demon, the Stray Demon, and the Last Giant. When it is resolved, each character may spend one stamina to move to an adjacent node before the next icon is resolved; the movement counts as a *Run* (see §17.3).
AMEND **Shaft.** A weapon icon. An attack option with the shaft icon cannot target a model on the attacker's own node (see §10.3, Shaft). On a behaviour card of Manus, Father of the Abyss, an attack with the shaft icon does not target a character on Manus's own node (see §17.10, Manus, Father of the Abyss).
AMEND **Aggro token.** The single token that marks the character with aggro. It is placed on the character with the highest threat at the start of each encounter and, after each round of character turns, moves to the character who inflicted the most damage on enemy models during that round (on a tie, or if no damage was inflicted, the holder keeps it). If the holder dies it moves to the living character with the highest threat. In boss encounters it moves to any character who damages the boss, or another enemy model in the encounter, during their turn (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §16.4).
AMEND **Mini-boss.** The first boss of the standard game, fought on the mini-boss tile or, for the Asylum Demon, on the mega-boss game board of its expansion (see §6.5; §15.3; §17.3).
AMEND **Beam card.** A card from the Old Iron King expansion revealed when a Beam icon is resolved. It shows the Iron King node on which the Old Iron King's model is placed and the nodes whose characters the Beam attack targets (see §17.10, Old Iron King).
AMEND **Behaviour card.** A card drawn from the behaviour deck of a boss, mimic, invader, or summon at the start of that model's turn. Its icons are resolved from left to right. A boss's current behaviour card sets the range and the dodge difficulty of its attacks and shows its weak arcs until the next card is drawn (see §15.5; §16.1).
AMEND **Cage node.** One of the four nodes at the corners of the mega-boss side of the Guardian Dragon's *mega-boss game board*. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into a cage node; the Guardian Dragon's model is placed on one only when it resolves an Inferno icon (see §17.10, Guardian Dragon).
AMEND **Calamity.** A condition from the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion. A model holds at most one calamity token, which reduces the total of each of its block, resist, and dodge rolls by one. A calamity token is removed only when the encounter ends (see §8.5, Calamity).
AMEND **Fiery Breath card.** A card from the Guardian Dragon expansion revealed when an Inferno icon is resolved. It shows the cage node on which the Guardian Dragon's model is placed, the nodes whose characters the Inferno attack targets, and the attack value and dodge difficulty of that attack (see §17.10, Guardian Dragon).
AMEND **Final boss.** The last boss of the game: the main-boss, or the mega-boss if the players chose to add one. Defeating the final boss wins the game (see §7.1). In campaign play the final boss is the last boss listed in the campaign's last section (see §18.1).
AMEND **Iron King node.** One of the three nodes, printed on the mega-boss side of the Old Iron King's *mega-boss game board*, on which the Old Iron King's model is placed. The Old Iron King never stands on any other node (see §17.10, Old Iron King).
AMEND **Leap.** A boss movement marked with the leap icon. The boss is removed from its node and placed on the node of its target, at any distance, without changing its facing. Every character on that node is pushed to a valid adjacent node chosen by that character's player, and the push is not limited by arcs (see §16.2).
AMEND **Stagger.** A condition. A staggered character or *summon* subtracts one from the total of each attack, block, resist, and dodge roll; any other staggered model reduces its attack values, its block and resist values, and its dodge difficulty by one. The token is removed at the end of the model's next complete turn. Stagger is placed by attacks that bear the stagger icon, by stamina overflow, and by a parry (see §8.5, Stagger; §9.6; §10.1).
