AMEND **Crystal lizard.** An optional enemy from the Iron Keep expansion, placed on a terrain node that the encounter card marks with the crystal lizard symbol. It flees rather than fights, is not scaled, does not count towards clearing the encounter, and escapes without award when the encounter is *cleared*. The character who kills one takes a Titanite Scale card, and once one has been killed in an encounter none is placed there again (see §14).
