ADD **PvP encounter.** An optional contest fought between the characters alone, without enemies, on the tile on which a boss has been defeated, after each boss's defeat and before the rest that follows it. It has no enemy turns and no aggro token, is neither *cleared* nor *failed*, awards no *souls* and no treasure, and death in it costs nothing (see §20.1).
ADD **Victory bonus.** The reward for winning a *PvP encounter* that follows the defeat of a boss other than the final boss, marked by one wound token of value 1 placed beside the winner's dashboard. Each victory bonus adds one to the attack value of each of the character's attacks in every subsequent PvP encounter; victory bonuses are cumulative and are kept until the game ends (see §20.8).
ADD **Ultimate victor.** The character who wins the *PvP encounter* that follows the defeat of the final boss (see §20.8).
AMEND **Trap token.** A token, blank or showing a dodge difficulty and a damage value, dealt face down to the basic nodes of a trapped encounter. It is turned face up when a character enters its node by a move, a dodge, or a push, and each character there must suffer the damage or *dodge* it; enemies neither trigger traps nor suffer them (see §5.5, Traps). In a *PvP encounter* one trap token is placed at the end of each character's turn, and a character who fails to dodge it dies (see §20.6).
AMEND **Round.** One pass of the encounter turn order: every enemy model takes a turn in descending threat order, then every character takes a turn in descending threat order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order). In a *PvP encounter* a round is one turn by each character still in play (see §20.2).
