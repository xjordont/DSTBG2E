ADD **Mimic encounter.** The encounter fought against a *mimic* on the tile of a *cleared* encounter whose chest the party has opened. It is fought as a mini-boss encounter without arcs, with the characters on the nodes they occupy and before any recovery or reward (see §13.2).
AMEND **Mimic.** An optional enemy from the Explorers expansion that masquerades as a chest. When the party opens a chest whose *ambush card* shows Mimic, the Hungry Mimic (before the mini-boss is defeated) or the Voracious Mimic (afterwards) is fought in a *mimic encounter*. Killing it awards two treasure cards in place of the chest's one (see §13).
AMEND **Ambush card.** A card from the Explorers expansion that shows Treasure or Mimic. When mimics are in play, one is dealt face down beside each encounter tile and is turned face up only when the party chooses to open that tile's chest (see §13.1).
