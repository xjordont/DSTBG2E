ADD **Character dashboard.** The board that records a character's class: the heroic action, the four equipment slots, the stat progression track, the endurance bar, the threat value, and the four token slots (see §3.1).
ADD **Armour slot.** The dashboard slot that holds one equipped *armour* card (see §3.2).
ADD **Weapon slot.** One of the two dashboard slots, the left weapon slot and the right weapon slot, that each hold one equipped weapon card. A two-handed weapon requires the other weapon slot to be empty (see §3.2).
ADD **Equipped.** The state of a card in the armour slot or a weapon slot of the dashboard. Cards in the back-up slot and the inventory are not equipped (see §3.2).
AMEND **Back-up slot.** The dashboard slot that holds up to two treasure cards of any kind, carried but not equipped (see §3.2). During an encounter, cards in the back-up slot may be exchanged with the weapon and armour slots only by the *Equip* minor action; an *ember card* must be in the back-up slot to be used.
AMEND **Inventory.** A character's unlimited stack of unequipped treasure cards, kept face down beside the dashboard. Outside an encounter it may be rearranged freely and cards may be traded one for one between inventories; during an encounter it cannot be accessed (see §3.2).
AMEND **Stat tier.** A character's position on each stat progression track: the base tier, or stat tier one, two, three, or four. It is marked with a *stat cube* or, at stat tier four, with a red cube in the stat tier three space. A stat at stat tier four has the value 40 or the stat tier three value plus five, whichever is higher. Tiers are bought in sequence from the Firekeeper (see §3.3, §11.3).
AMEND **Threat.** The value that fixes turn order, printed on the character dashboard as Taunt Level and on enemy and phantom data cards. Enemies act in descending threat before the characters, who then act in descending threat. The character with the highest threat takes the aggro token at the start of each encounter (see §3.1, §7.5).
AMEND **Luck.** A character token. Flipping it allows the character to reroll any or all dice of one of their own attack or defence rolls; the dice are chosen together and the new results stand. It is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Luck).
AMEND **Estus flask.** A character token. Flipping it as the Drink Estus major action removes all damage cubes from the endurance bar; stamina cubes, hollow cubes, and condition tokens are not removed. It is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Estus Flask).
AMEND **Heroic action.** The once-per-rest ability printed on the dashboard, resolved as a minor action by flipping the *heroic action* token to its used side. Its printed wording overrides other rules. The token is readied only by resting at the *bonfire* (see §3.5, Heroic Action).
AMEND **Ember token.** The token that marks an *embered* character. It begins the game beside the dashboard, is placed in the dashboard's ember slot when the character becomes embered, and is removed when the character dies (see §3.5, Ember; §7.3).
