ADD **Level 4 encounter.** The single encounter fought on the Level 4 encounter side of a *mega-boss game board* after the main-boss is defeated and before the mega-boss encounter. All four nodes along the wall beside the doorway are entry nodes; once *cleared* it is not fought again and does not reset (see §6.7).
ADD **Mega-boss game board.** The double-sided board supplied with a mega-boss expansion. Its Level 4 encounter side is the tile of the Level 4 encounter and its mega-boss side is the tile of the mega-boss encounter (see §6.7, §15.3).
ADD **Boss treasure.** A treasure card supplied with a boss. One of a boss's treasure cards is awarded at random when the boss is defeated (see §16.5).
ADD **Treasure card.** Any card from a treasure deck: *starting equipment*, *class-specific treasure*, *common treasure*, *legendary treasure*, or *boss treasure* (see §2.5). A treasure card that may be equipped to a dashboard slot is an *equipment card*.
ADD **Stat cube.** A white cube that marks a character's *stat tier* on a stat progression track (see §3.3).
ADD **Hollow cube.** One of eight cubes of a colour distinct from the damage, stamina, and stat cubes. Two hollow cubes block the two rightmost spaces of a *hollow* character's endurance bar (see §7.2).
ADD **Health dial.** The dial that records the health of a boss, summon, invader, or mimic. Enemies record damage with wound tokens instead (see §15.4).
ADD **Invader token.** A token from the Phantoms expansion that shows an invader icon or is blank. When invaders are in play, one is placed face down on each face-down encounter card and is revealed when the characters enter that tile (see §12.2).
AMEND **Souls.** The currency of the game, tracked by soul tokens on each character's dashboard. *Souls* are gained by *clearing* encounters and killing invaders, dropped on death, and spent at the *bonfire*. *Souls* may not be transferred between characters. A character adventuring alone begins with six *souls* (see §6.1).
AMEND **Shop.** Blacksmith Andre's stock of face-up treasure cards, in tier one and tier two piles. The tier one shop is stocked at setup and the tier two shop when the mini-boss is defeated; both are restocked only after a boss is defeated (in campaign play, after a section is completed), unsold cards going to the bottom of their deck first (see §6.3).
AMEND **Entry node.** A basic node beside the doorway through which the characters entered the tile. Characters are placed on entry nodes at the start of an encounter, no more than three to a node. In a Level 4 encounter all four nodes along the wall beside the doorway are entry nodes (see §6.7).
AMEND **Encounter level.** The difficulty of an encounter, from 1 to 4. The levels of the encounters leading to a boss are printed on that boss's data card and increase from the tile adjacent to the bonfire tile towards the boss tile (see §6.5). Level 4 encounters precede mega-bosses only (see §6.7).
AMEND **Legendary treasure.** A rare treasure card marked with an orange light under a chest. It cannot be purchased; five are shuffled into the tier two deck when the tier two shop is first stocked, and one drawn while stocking a shop is shuffled back (see §6.2, §6.3). In campaign play legendary treasure is obtained only by *transposition*.
