ADD **Attack arc.** A *boss arc* shown in green on the arc diagram beneath an *area attack*. The area attack targets every character within range who is in that arc (see §15.6; §16.3).
ADD **Boss spawn node.** The spawn node of a boss's type: the mini-boss spawn node, the main-boss spawn node, or the mega-boss spawn node. The boss model is placed on it at the start of every boss encounter (see §15.3).
ADD **Facing.** The direction in which the centre of a boss's front arc points. A boss faces directly towards an adjacent node and changes its facing only when a movement icon or a turn icon turns it (see §15.6).
ADD **Heat-up point.** The health value, printed on a boss's data card, at or below which the boss heats up (see §15.5).
ADD **Node attack.** An enemy attack that bears the node icon. It targets every character on the node of the character named by its skull icon or nearest-character icon; each character defends separately, and a node attack cannot be parried (see §9.3).
ADD **Push-attack.** An attack that bears the push icon. A model it hits is pushed directly away from the attacker, whether or not the attack dealt damage (see §8.4).
ADD **Push-move.** An enemy or boss movement that bears the push icon. Characters on the enemy's node are pushed aside before it moves, and characters on each node it enters are pushed directly away from the node it left; a push icon that carries a damage value also makes each entry a physical attack (see §8.4).
AMEND **Behaviour deck.** The face-down deck of behaviour cards from which a boss, mimic, invader, or summon draws one card at the start of each of its turns. A boss's behaviour deck is built during setup and built again each time its failed encounter is reset (see §15.5). When the deck is empty at the start of a turn, the discard pile is turned face down without shuffling to form the new deck.
AMEND **Boss arc.** One of the four sectors (front, left, right, back) into which a boss's base is divided. The arcs extend across the tile: each node lies in at least one arc, and a node on the line between two arcs lies in both (see §15.6).
AMEND **Boss tile.** One of the two tiles on which mini-boss and main-boss encounters are fought. A mini-boss encounter is fought on the mini-boss tile alone; a main-boss encounter is fought on both boss tiles joined as one tile, the main-boss tile. A mega-boss is fought on the mega-boss game board supplied with it (see §15.3).
AMEND **Fog gate.** The token that marks the doorway of a boss encounter. A boss encounter begins when the characters pass through it. The first time they pass through, the token is moved to the *bonfire* tile, where it is a shortcut to that boss until the boss is defeated (see §15.1). In campaign play it is aesthetic only (see §18.2).
AMEND **Heat-up.** The event in which a boss, mimic, or invader becomes more dangerous. When a boss's health is reduced to or below its heat-up point, one heat-up card taken at random is shuffled into the cards that remain in its behaviour deck (see §15.5); a mimic or an invader adds one of its unused behaviour cards instead. Some bosses heat up differently (see §17).
AMEND **Heat-up card.** A behaviour card marked with the heat-up symbol. Heat-up cards are set aside when the behaviour deck is built, and one of them joins the deck when the boss heats up (see §15.5).
AMEND **Weak arc.** A boss arc shown in red on the arc diagram of the boss's current behaviour card, the card it drew most recently. A character who attacks a boss from a weak arc adds one black die to each attack roll, however many weak arcs the character is in (see §15.6).
ADD **Leap.** A boss movement marked with the leap icon. The boss is removed from its node and placed on the node of its target, at any distance, without changing its facing. Every character on that node is pushed to a valid adjacent node chosen by that character's player, and the push is not limited by arcs (see §16.2).
