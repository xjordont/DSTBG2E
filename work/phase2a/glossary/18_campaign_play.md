ADD **Campaign.** A game played through a fixed list of *sections*, taken in order, in which the characters keep what they have gained from one section to the next. The book supplies 12 campaigns; campaign play follows the standard game except as its own chapter states (see §18.1, §19).
AMEND **Section.** In campaign play, one list of tiles: a *bonfire* tile, a number of encounter tiles with their encounter levels, and the bosses to be faced. A section is completed when the last boss it lists is defeated or, for a section whose list ends in an encounter, when that encounter is *cleared*. The shop is restocked and a rest that costs no *spark* is granted only when a section is completed (see §18.1).
AMEND **Final boss.** The last boss of the game: the main-boss, or the mega-boss if the players chose to add one. Defeating the final boss wins the game (see §7.1). In campaign play the final boss is the last boss listed in the campaign's last section (see §18.1).
AMEND **Bonfire ascetic.** In campaign play, a one-time communal payment of 16 *souls* made while resting at the *bonfire*, the one exception to the rule that souls cannot be pooled. It opens tier two treasure and the tier two shop and increases by two the base health of every enemy subject to enemy scaling for the rest of the campaign, cumulative with the health scaling for the number of characters. Enemy attack values are not changed (see §18.7).
AMEND **Transposition.** In campaign play, exchanging one boss treasure card and four *souls* for one of the five face-up legendary treasure cards while resting at the *bonfire*. It is available after the last boss of the first section is defeated, and the boss treasure card is returned to the game box (see §18.8).
AMEND **Legendary treasure.** A rare treasure card marked with an orange light under a chest. It cannot be purchased; five are shuffled into the tier two deck when the tier two shop is first stocked, and one drawn while stocking a shop is shuffled back (see §6.2, §6.3). In campaign play legendary treasure enters neither treasure deck, is obtained only by *transposition*, and sells for four *souls* (see §18.6, §18.8).
AMEND **Level 4 encounter.** The single encounter fought on the Level 4 encounter side of a *mega-boss game board* after the main-boss is defeated and before the mega-boss encounter. All four nodes along the wall beside the doorway are entry nodes; once *cleared* it is not fought again and does not reset (see §6.7). In campaign play a Level 4 encounter is fought where its section lists it, on a mega-boss game board placed in the section's route (see §18.1).
AMEND **Encounter level.** The difficulty of an encounter, from 1 to 4. The levels of the encounters leading to a boss are printed on that boss's data card and increase from the tile adjacent to the bonfire tile towards the boss tile (see §6.5). In the standard game Level 4 encounters precede mega-bosses only (see §6.7). In campaign play each section lists the levels of its encounters (see §18.1).
AMEND **Mega-boss.** An optional final boss from a mega-boss expansion, fought on its own game board after a Level 4 encounter. Death and hollowing in a mega-boss encounter follow the usual rules (see §7.2). In campaign play a mega-boss is faced only where the campaign lists one, and a mega-boss that is not the campaign's final boss is fought under the same rules as any other boss (see §18.1).
AMEND **Resting at the bonfire.** The party action, costing one *spark* unless it follows the defeat of a boss (in campaign play, unless it follows the completion of a *section*; see §18.3), that revives dead and *lost* characters, removes every damage cube, stamina cube, hollow cube, and condition token, readies the heroic action, luck, and estus flask tokens, allows visits to the Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre, and resets every encounter. It neither places nor removes an *ember token* (see §11.2).
