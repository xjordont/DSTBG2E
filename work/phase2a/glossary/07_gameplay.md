ADD **Round.** One pass of the encounter turn order: every enemy model takes a turn in descending threat order, then every character takes a turn in descending threat order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order).
ADD **Reset.** The state of an encounter after the characters have rested at the *bonfire*: its encounter card is face down, its enemy models have been removed, and its terrain tokens remain in place. A *cleared* encounter keeps its black cube through a reset; a *failed* encounter has none (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution; §11.5).
ADD **Reduced reward.** Half the full soul reward, rounded down, gained by every character when an encounter already marked *cleared* is cleared again (see §7.6).
AMEND **Aggro token.** The single token that marks the character with aggro. It is placed on the character with the highest threat at the start of each encounter and, after each round of character turns, moves to the character who inflicted the most damage on enemy models during that round (on a tie, or if no damage was inflicted, the holder keeps it). If the holder dies it moves to the living character with the highest threat. In boss encounters it moves to any character who damages the boss during their turn (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §16.4).
AMEND **Chest.** A terrain token or model that blocks movement, dodging, and pushes. A chest holds one face-down treasure card of the current tier, revealed when the encounter is *cleared* and awarded to the party (see §7.7). With mimics in play, opening a chest requires revealing the tile's *ambush card* (see §13).
AMEND **Cleared.** The result of an encounter in which every enemy model is killed and at least one character survives. A *cleared* encounter card is marked with a black cube, rewards *souls* and (the first time only) treasure, keeps its black cube through a *reset*, and may thereafter be skipped (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution).
AMEND **Dropped souls.** *Souls* placed on an encounter tile by a character who died there or, when an encounter was *failed*, by a living character. Only the character who dropped them may reclaim them, by surviving to clear that encounter. They are returned to the supply if that character dies first (see §7.6).
AMEND **Ember card.** A tier one shop card. Discarding an *ember* card from the back-up slot (the *Ember* major action, or outside an encounter) makes the character *embered* (see §7.3). A character may carry only one *ember* card and cannot carry one while *embered*; a card held when the character becomes *embered* passes to another character (see §7.3).
AMEND **Failed.** The result of an encounter in which every character dies, or in which any *hollow* character dies. A *failed* encounter awards nothing; every living character drops their *souls* on the tile, its card is turned face down and its black cube removed, and the characters must rest at the *bonfire* (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution).
AMEND **Lost.** The state of a *hollow* character who has died (see §7.2). A *lost* character is removed from play, the encounter is *failed* at once, and the character is revived only when the characters rest at the *bonfire*.
AMEND **Scry.** To reveal one hidden card (the top card of a treasure deck, a face-down encounter card, or the top card of the next boss's behaviour deck) and either return it or send it away (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution).
AMEND **Spark.** The *bonfire*'s charges, tracked on the spark dial. The party begins with six minus the number of characters, gains one for each boss defeated, and spends one each time the characters rest at the *bonfire* (see §7.4). If the characters are required to rest while the dial shows zero, the players lose the game (see §7.1). In campaign play the dial's maximum is one more than its starting value and a *spark* may be bought back for *souls* (see §18.3).
AMEND **Threat.** The value that fixes turn order. Enemies act in descending threat before the characters, who then act in descending threat. The character with the highest threat takes the aggro token at the start of each encounter; the players settle ties (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order).
AMEND **Souls.** The currency of the game, tracked by soul tokens on each character's dashboard. *Souls* are gained by *clearing* encounters, defeating bosses, and killing invaders, and are dropped on death. They are spent while resting at the *bonfire* and, as the one exception, to call a *summon* at the fog gate (see §7.6, §12.1). *Souls* cannot be transferred between characters. A character adventuring alone begins with six *souls* (see §6.1).
