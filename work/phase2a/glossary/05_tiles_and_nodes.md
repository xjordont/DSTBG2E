ADD **Move.** A change of node made by a model through the *Walk* or *Run* minor action, a *shift* icon, or an enemy or boss movement icon. The movement after a successful *dodge* and the movement of a *push* are not moves (see §5.2).
ADD **Valid node.** A node that a model may enter by a given form of movement: an adjacent node not separated from the model's node by a wall and holding no terrain that blocks that form of entry. A node at its node model limit is a valid node (see §5.2).
ADD **Terrain node.** A node on which a terrain token or model is placed at the start of an encounter, as the encounter card directs. A terrain node that holds no terrain is used as a basic node (see §5.1).
AMEND **Adjacent node.** A node directly next to another node horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Nodes separated by a wall, including a wall printed across a tile or a mega-boss game board, are not adjacent, and nodes on different tiles are not adjacent (see §5.1).
AMEND **Barrel.** A terrain token or model with an intact side and a destroyed side. No model may *move* into a node containing an intact barrel; a model that *dodges* or is pushed into the node destroys the barrel. Barrels are restored when the characters rest at the *bonfire* (see §5.5, Barrels).
AMEND **Chest.** A terrain token or model with a closed side and an open side; no model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into its node. A chest holds one face-down treasure card of the current tier, revealed and awarded when the encounter is *cleared* (see §5.5, Chests; §7.7). With mimics in play, a chest is opened only as §13 describes.
AMEND **Gravestone.** A terrain token or model. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into a node containing a gravestone. When an encounter that contains a gravestone is *cleared*, the players may *scry* one card (see §5.5, Gravestones).
AMEND **Node model limit.** At most three non-boss models and one boss model may occupy a node. A model entering a full node forces one occupant to be pushed to a valid node chosen by the players (see §5.3).
AMEND **Pillar token.** A terrain token placed on each of the four pillar nodes of the Asylum Demon mega-boss game board. No model may *move*, *dodge*, or be pushed into its node until the pillar is destroyed (see §5.5, Pillars; §17.3).
AMEND **Range.** The maximum distance between an attacker and its target, counted in nodes along the shortest chain of adjacent nodes. Range 0 is the same node; range 1 is an adjacent node; range ∞ is unlimited. Range is never blocked by terrain or models (see §5.4).
AMEND **Terrain.** Gravestones, barrels, chests, and pillars, placed on terrain nodes, and trap tokens, dealt to basic nodes. Terrain stays on its nodes when encounters are reset (see §5.5).
AMEND **Trap token.** A token, blank or showing a dodge difficulty and a damage value, dealt face down to the basic nodes of a trapped encounter. It is turned face up when a character moves into or is pushed onto its node, and each character there must suffer the damage or *dodge* it; enemies neither trigger traps nor suffer them (see §5.5, Traps).
