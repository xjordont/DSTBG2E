ADD **Attack arc.** A *boss arc* shown in green on the arc diagram beneath an *area attack*. The area attack targets every character within range who is in that arc (see §15.6; §16.3).
ADD **Facing.** The direction in which the centre of a boss's front arc points. A boss faces directly towards an adjacent node and changes its facing only when a movement icon or a turn icon turns it (see §15.6).
ADD **Leap.** A boss movement marked with the leap icon. The boss is removed from its node and placed on the node of its target, at any distance, without changing its facing. Every character on that node is pushed to a valid adjacent node chosen by that character's player, and the push is not limited by arcs (see §16.2).
ADD **Nearest character.** The living character at the shortest range from an enemy. If two or more characters are tied, the nearest character is the one who holds the aggro token, otherwise the one with the highest threat, and otherwise the one the players choose (see §9.2).
ADD **Node attack.** An enemy attack that bears the node icon. It targets every character on the node of the character named by its skull icon or nearest-character icon; each character defends separately, and a node attack cannot be parried (see §9.3).
ADD **Push-attack.** An attack that bears the push icon. A model it hits is pushed directly away from the attacker, whether or not the attack dealt damage (see §8.4).
ADD **Push-move.** An enemy or boss movement that bears the push icon. Characters on the enemy's node are pushed aside before it moves, and characters on each node it enters are pushed directly away from the node it left; a push icon that carries a damage value also makes each entry a physical attack (see §8.4).
AMEND **Area attack.** A boss attack whose icon bears the area icon. It is made into the attack arcs of the arc diagram beneath it and targets every character within the card's range who is in one of those arcs. Each targeted character defends separately, and an area attack cannot be parried (see §16.3; §15.6).
AMEND **Round.** One pass of the encounter turn order: every enemy model takes a turn in descending threat order, then every character takes a turn in descending threat order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order). In a boss encounter a round is one turn by each living character, each preceded by a turn of the boss (see §16.1). In a *PvP encounter* a round is one turn by each character still in play (see §20.2).
AMEND **Turn.** One model's opportunity to act. In each round the enemies take their turns, then the characters take theirs in descending threat order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §10.1). In a boss encounter the boss takes a turn before each character's turn, and the players choose the order of the characters (see §16.1).
AMEND **Aggro token.** The single token that marks the character with aggro. It is placed on the character with the highest threat at the start of each encounter and, after each round of character turns, moves to the character who inflicted the most damage on enemy models during that round (on a tie, or if no damage was inflicted, the holder keeps it). If the holder dies it moves to the living character with the highest threat. In a boss encounter it moves, at the end of a character's turn, to that character if they damaged the boss during the turn, and a damaging riposte moves it at the end of the boss's turn (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order; §16.4).
