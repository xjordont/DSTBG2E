# Glossary deltas — chapter 04 Equipment

ADD **Attack (major action).** The major action in which a character makes up to one attack with each weapon or spell equipped in a weapon slot, each attack using one attack option and paying its stamina cost (see §10.3).
ADD **Equipment card.** A treasure card that a character equips in a weapon or armour slot, stores in a catalyst, or attaches to an equipped card: weapon, shield, catalyst, spell, armour, and upgrade cards (see §4.1).
ADD **Shield.** A one-handed weapon card that shows block or resist dice and has no attack option. A shield is a weapon for equipment slots and for two-handed attacks, and a shield for stocking the shop and for parrying (see §4.1).
ADD **Spell.** An equipment card or upgrade card whose art shows a scroll: a pyromancy, a sorcery, or a miracle. A character may use at most one spell attack option per turn, and spells are never scaled or two-handed (see §4.3).
ADD **Stat requirement.** One of the four values (STR, DEX, INT, FTH) printed on an equipment card or upgrade card. A character may equip a card only if each of their stats equals or exceeds the matching requirement of the card and of every upgrade attached to it (see §4.1).
ADD **Treasure card.** Any card drawn from a treasure deck or bought from Blacksmith Andre's shop: an equipment card or an ember card (see §2.5, §4.1).
ADD **Upgrade slot.** A space shown by the upgrade slots icon on a weapon or armour card; a card has zero, one, or two. Each attached upgrade card other than an upgrade spell occupies one slot (see §4.2).
ADD **Upgrade spell.** An upgrade card whose art shows a scroll. It is attached to a weapon or armour card equipped to the same character as the *Attack* major action during an encounter, occupies no upgrade slot, and returns to the inventory when the encounter ends (see §4.2).
ADD **Weapon.** An equipment card that bears the one-handed or two-handed weapon icon and whose art does not show a scroll. Shields, catalysts, bows, crossbows, and throwable weapons are weapons (see §4.1).
AMEND **Catalyst.** A weapon card that stores up to three spells of a matching school: a pyromancer flame stores pyromancies, a staff or catalyst stores sorceries, and a talisman or chime stores miracles. A stored spell occupies no equipment slot; its attack options are used as if it were equipped, and its block, resist, and dodge dice are ignored (see §4.3).
AMEND **Titanite.** A weapon upgrade (Titanite Shard, Titanite Chunk, Titanite Slab, or Titanite Scale) that is fused permanently to the weapon it is attached to. When that weapon is sold, the Titanite is returned with it to the bottom of the treasure deck of the weapon's tier (see §4.2).
AMEND **Upgrade card.** A card that enhances a weapon or armour card. Blacksmith Andre attaches it beneath the card, up to the card's upgrade slots, and the character must meet its stat requirements to equip the card. Upgrade spells are instead attached as the *Attack* major action during an encounter and return to the inventory afterwards (see §4.2).
