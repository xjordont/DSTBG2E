ADD **Blacksmith Andre.** The smith at the *bonfire* who keeps the *shop*, buying and selling treasure cards for *souls*, and who attaches and removes *upgrade cards* at no cost. He is visited only while the party rests at the bonfire (see §11.4).
AMEND **Bonfire.** The characters' safe haven, represented by the *bonfire* tile, which has no nodes. The Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre wait there and are visited only while the party rests. Returning to the bonfire tile costs nothing; resting there costs one *spark* (see §11.1, §11.2).
AMEND **Boss treasure.** A treasure card supplied with a boss. One of a boss's treasure cards is awarded at random when the boss is defeated (see §16.5). Boss treasure cannot be bought; it sells for six *souls* and is returned to the game box when sold (see §11.4; for campaign play, see §18.6).
AMEND **Firekeeper.** The figure at the *bonfire* who raises a character's stats, one *stat tier* per purchase, in exchange for that character's *souls*. Stat tiers one to four cost 2, 4, 8, and 10 souls (see §11.3; for campaign play, see §18.6).
AMEND **Resting at the bonfire.** The party action, costing one *spark* unless it follows the defeat of a boss, that revives dead and *lost* characters, removes every damage cube, stamina cube, hollow cube, and condition token, readies the heroic action, luck, and estus flask tokens, allows visits to the Firekeeper and Blacksmith Andre, and resets every encounter. It neither places nor removes an *ember token* (see §11.2).
AMEND **Shop.** Blacksmith Andre's stock of face-up treasure cards, in a tier one shop and a tier two shop. A tier one card costs two *souls* and a tier two card four; a treasure card other than starting equipment sells for one soul (see §11.4). The tier one shop is stocked at setup and the tier two shop when the mini-boss is defeated; both are restocked only after a boss is defeated (in campaign play, after a section is completed), unsold cards going to the bottom of their deck first (see §6.3).
AMEND **Starting equipment.** The three or four equipment cards a character begins with, marked with that character's class icon (see §6.2). Starting equipment cannot be sold (see §11.4).
