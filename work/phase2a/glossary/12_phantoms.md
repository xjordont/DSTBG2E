ADD **Distract.** An icon found only on summon behaviour cards. During the boss's next turn the summon is treated as holding the aggro token; the token itself is not moved (see §12.1).
ADD **Invasion.** The placing of invader tokens that brings one invader into the game. An invasion is provoked when the encounter cards are dealt while a character is *embered*, or when a character becomes embered by discarding an *ember card* while a face-down encounter card lies on a tile; no more than one is provoked before the mini-boss is defeated and no more than one after it (see §12.2).
AMEND **Phantom.** A *summon* or an *invader*: a model of the Phantoms expansion with a data card and behaviour cards. A summon is friendly and an invader hostile; a phantom is neither a character nor a boss (see §12).
AMEND **Summon.** A friendly phantom called before a boss encounter. Each *embered* character may pay two *souls* to draw one summon data card at random, and the players choose one of the drawn summons. The summon acts after each character's turn, defends with the dice on its data card, and is removed when its health dial is reduced to zero or the encounter ends (see §12.1).
AMEND **Invader.** A hostile phantom brought into one encounter by an *invasion*. An invader acts after each character's turn using its own behaviour deck, is not scaled, does not count towards clearing the encounter, vanishes without award if a character dies, and awards *souls* and its treasure card when killed (see §12.2).
AMEND **Invader token.** A token from the Phantoms expansion that shows an invader or is blank. When an *invasion* is provoked, one token is placed face down on each face-down encounter card, one of them showing an invader; a token is revealed when the characters fight that encounter (see §12.2).
