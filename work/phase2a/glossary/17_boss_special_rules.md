ADD **Twin Gargoyles.** The boss encounter set by a campaign *section* that lists the Gargoyle twice in succession: one mini-boss encounter in two parts, fought against one Gargoyle and then at once against a second, with no rest or reward between them. The rewards of a defeated boss follow only when the second Gargoyle is defeated, and a failure against either Gargoyle means both are fought again (see §17.1, Twin Gargoyles).
ADD **Pillar node.** One of the four terrain nodes printed on the mega-boss side of the Asylum Demon expansion's *mega-boss game board*, on each of which a *pillar token* is placed at the start of a boss encounter fought there (see §17.3).
ADD **Signature card.** One of Black Dragon Kalameet's two behaviour cards, Mark of Calamity and Hellfire Blast, that are included in every behaviour deck built for Kalameet (see §17.8).
ADD **Mega-boss setup card.** The card of the Executioner's Chariot expansion that shows the enemy spawn nodes on which the Black Hollow Mages and the Falchion Skeletons are placed at the start of the mega-boss encounter (see §17.9).
AMEND **Wind Up.** A behaviour icon printed on behaviour cards of the Asylum Demon and the Stray Demon. When it is resolved, each character may spend one stamina to move to an adjacent node before the next icon is resolved; the movement counts as a *Run* (see §17.3).
AMEND **Strafe card.** A card from the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion revealed when a strafe icon is resolved. It shows the nodes whose characters the strafe attack targets and the landing node on which Kalameet's model is then placed; a character on the landing node is pushed to an adjacent node (see §17.8).
AMEND **Track behaviour card.** One of the four behaviour cards of the Executioner's Chariot expansion, named Death Race and numbered 1 to 4, that direct the Chariot's turns in fixed order until it heats up. Each shows the node to which the Chariot moves and the nodes that its attack targets (see §17.9).
