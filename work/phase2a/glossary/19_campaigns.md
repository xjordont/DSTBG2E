AMEND **Section.** In campaign play, one list of tiles that the party lays out and plays through in order, ending in a boss or in an encounter. A section is completed when its final boss is defeated or, for a section that ends in an encounter, when that encounter is *cleared*, unless the campaign states another condition. Completing a section restocks the *shop* and grants a rest without spending a *spark* (see §18.1; §19).
