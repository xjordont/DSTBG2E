ADD **Base health.** The health printed on an enemy's data card, before enemy scaling (see §8.2).
ADD **Scaled health.** An enemy's base health plus the increase that enemy scaling gives for the number of characters. An enemy is killed when the total value of its wound tokens equals or exceeds its scaled health (see §8.2).
ADD **Enemy scaling.** The adjustment of every enemy to the number of characters fixed at setup: more health with two or more characters, movement values of 1 resolved as 2 with two or more characters, and +1 attack value with four characters. Bosses, mimics, phantoms, and crystal lizards are never scaled (see §8.3).
ADD **Push-attack.** An attack that bears the push icon. A model it hits is pushed directly away from the attacker, whether or not the attack dealt damage (see §8.4).
ADD **Push-move.** An enemy or boss movement that bears the push icon. Characters on the enemy's node are pushed aside before it moves, and characters on each node it enters are pushed directly away from the node it left; a push icon that carries a damage value also makes each entry a physical attack (see §8.4).
AMEND **Hit.** A target is hit by an attack when it is within the attack's range and does not avoid the attack by a successful *dodge* or *parry*, even if the attack deals no damage. Pushes and conditions carried by an attack apply only to models that the attack hits (see §8.1).
AMEND **Target.** The model at which an attack is directed. An enemy never targets another enemy; a character targets another character only in player versus player (see §8.1).
AMEND **Data card.** The card that defines an enemy, boss, mimic, or phantom: name, threat, range, dodge difficulty, block and resist, behaviour icons or behaviour deck size, health, and special ability (see §8.2 for enemies; §15.4 for bosses).
AMEND **Wound token.** A token of value 1, 3, or 5 that records the damage suffered by an enemy. Wound tokens with a total value equal to the damage are placed on the enemy model's base, and an enemy whose wound tokens equal or exceed its scaled health is killed and removed from the tile (see §8.2). Bosses, phantoms, and mimics use a health dial instead.
AMEND **Push.** Forced movement of a model from its node to an adjacent valid node. A push is caused by a push-attack, by a push-move, by the node model limit, or by a boss model moving onto the node of another boss model. A push is not a move and costs no stamina; a gravestone, a chest, and a pillar token block it, and a push with no valid node does not happen (see §8.4).
AMEND **Condition.** A lasting effect marked by a condition token on a model: *bleed*, *poison*, *frostbite*, *stagger*, *calamity*, or *corrosion*. A condition token is placed on each model hit by an attack that bears the matching icon, after the attack is resolved. A model holds at most one token of each condition, except poison (see §8.5).
AMEND **Bleed.** A condition. A model holds at most one bleed token. When a bleed token is to be placed on a model that already holds one, the model instead suffers three damage that block and resist do not reduce, and its bleed token is removed. A bleed token is otherwise removed only when the encounter ends (see §8.5, Bleed).
AMEND **Poison.** A condition. A model may hold any number of poison tokens. At the end of each of its turns a model suffers one damage for each poison token it holds, which block and resist do not reduce, and then removes all of its poison tokens (see §8.5, Poison).
AMEND **Frostbite.** A condition. A character with a frostbite token cannot recover stamina and cannot take the *Rest* major action; any other model with a frostbite token reduces each of its movement values by one. The token is removed at the end of the model's next complete turn (see §8.5, Frostbite).
AMEND **Stagger.** A condition. A staggered character subtracts one from the total of each attack, block, resist, and dodge roll; any other staggered model reduces its attack values, its block and resist values, and its dodge difficulty by one. The token is removed at the end of the model's next complete turn. Stagger is placed by attacks that bear the stagger icon, by stamina overflow, and by a parry (see §8.5, Stagger; §9.6; §10.1).
AMEND **Calamity.** A condition from the Black Dragon Kalameet expansion. A model holds at most one calamity token, which reduces the total of each of its block, resist, and dodge rolls by one. A calamity token is removed only when the encounter ends (see §8.5, Calamity).
AMEND **Corrosion.** A condition from the Gaping Dragon expansion. A model holds at most one corrosion token, which reduces the total of each of its block rolls by one. The token is removed when the affected model next suffers damage (see §8.5, Corrosion).
