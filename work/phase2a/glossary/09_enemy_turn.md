ADD **Nearest character.** The living character at the shortest range from an enemy. If two or more characters are tied, the nearest character is the one who holds the aggro token, otherwise the one with the highest threat, and otherwise the one the players choose (see §9.2).
ADD **Node attack.** An enemy attack that bears the node icon. It targets every character on the node of the character named by its skull icon or nearest-character icon; each character defends separately, and a node attack cannot be parried (see §9.3).
ADD **Push-attack.** An attack that bears the push icon. A model it hits is pushed directly away from the attacker, whether or not the attack dealt damage (see §8.4).
ADD **Push-move.** An enemy or boss movement that bears the push icon. Characters on the enemy's node are pushed aside before it moves, and characters on each node it enters are pushed directly away from the node it left; a push icon that carries a damage value also makes each entry a physical attack (see §8.4).
AMEND **Aggro.** The state of being the enemies' preferred target. Enemy movement icons and attack icons marked with the skull icon target the character who holds the aggro token (see §9.2, §9.3).
AMEND **Armour.** An equipment card bearing the armour icon. One armour card may be equipped in the armour slot. After a failed dodge, only the block or resist dice of the armour card and of the upgrade cards attached to it are rolled (see §9.5).
AMEND **Behaviour icon.** A movement or attack icon on an enemy data card or a behaviour card. Icons are resolved left to right; an icon that has no valid target, or that cannot be resolved, is skipped (see §9.1).
AMEND **Block.** The defence rolled against an attack that deals physical damage. The defence total is the sum of the two highest block dice rolled from all equipped cards, with the fixed modifiers of those cards then applied (see §9.4). For enemies, block is the fixed value subtracted from a physical attack value.
AMEND **Defence total.** The sum of the two highest block or resist dice rolled, to which the fixed modifiers of the equipped cards are then applied; a single die counts alone, no dice count as zero, and a total of less than zero is zero. Damage suffered equals the attack value minus the defence total, to a minimum of zero (see §9.4).
AMEND **Dodge.** A defence against an attack that targets the character. The character pays one stamina, may pay two further stamina for one extra green die to a maximum of four dice, and succeeds if the dodge icons rolled equal or exceed the attack's dodge difficulty. A successful dodge avoids the hit and the character must move to an adjacent valid node; after a failed dodge only the armour card's block or resist dice are rolled (see §9.5).
AMEND **Dodge difficulty.** The number of dodge icons a dodge or parry roll must equal or exceed. It is printed on an enemy data card, a boss behaviour card, or a trap token (see §9.5). In player versus player it is derived from the stamina cost of the attacker's attack option.
AMEND **Parry.** A defence available to a character with a shield equipped in the left weapon slot when the attacker is on the character's node; an attack that pushes, a node attack, and a boss attack outside §15.6 cannot be parried. The character pays one stamina and rolls dodge dice against the attack's dodge difficulty. Success makes the attack miss, staggers the attacker, and allows a *riposte*; failure deals the full attack value with no block or resist roll and staggers the character (see §9.6).
AMEND **Resist.** The defence rolled against an attack that deals magical damage, resolved as block is with resist dice (see §9.4). For enemies, resist is the fixed value subtracted from a magical attack value.
AMEND **Riposte.** The attack a character may make at once against the attacker after a successful *parry*, using one attack option of the weapon in the right weapon slot and paying its stamina cost. A riposte cannot be parried, and it does not count against the attacks of the character's own turn (see §9.6).
