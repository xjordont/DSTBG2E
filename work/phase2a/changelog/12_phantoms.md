# Change log — chapter 12 (Phantoms)

### CH-12-001 · §12 Phantoms
- Type: restructure
- Before: "# SUMMONS" / "## Phantoms" / "## Invaders"
- After: "# 12. Phantoms" / "## 12.1 Summons" / "## 12.2 Invaders"; "**Phantom.** A *summon* or an *invader*."
- Rationale: The manuscript used "phantom" both for the superset and for its friendly subset, and filed invaders under a chapter called "Summons". Phantom is now the umbrella term, a summon is friendly, and an invader hostile; the chapter and its sections are renamed to match.
- Source: C-064, C-103, C-104; Gate 1 — OQ-31

### CH-12-002 · §12 Phantoms

- Source: C-124; brief §2

### CH-12-003 · §12.1 Summons, Summoning
- Type: clarification
- Before: "*Embered* characters may elect to pay two *souls* to draw a phantom card. ... Draw a random phantom card of appropriate level. ... Players must agree on only one of the drawn phantoms to summon."
- After: "Each embered character may pay two *souls*. A character pays no more than once. ... Choose one of the drawn data cards. The summon on that card joins the encounter. ... Return every other drawn data card to the supply."
