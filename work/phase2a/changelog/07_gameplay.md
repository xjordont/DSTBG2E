# Change log — chapter 07 (Gameplay)

### CH-07-001 · §7 Gameplay
- Type: restructure
- Before: "- Characters die whenever they cannot add a damage cube to their endurance tracker because it is already full. Upon death, a character is temporarily removed from play."
- After: "A character dies when they must place a *damage cube* on their *endurance bar* and no space is free (see §3.4). A dead character is out of play for the rest of the *encounter* and takes no *turns*."
- Rationale: The chapter's bullet fragments are rewritten as standing rules and numbered procedures in lawbook voice; the empty page-break heading is deleted and "&" is written "and" in the two section titles.
- Source: C-119, F-085, C-098, C-104, C-112, C-035

### CH-07-002 · §7.1 Objective and Loss Conditions
- Type: completion
- Before: "The players lose collectively if at any point, all characters are dead and there are no remaining sparks to revive them."
- After: "The players lose the game when the characters are required to rest at the *bonfire* and the *spark* dial shows zero."
- Rationale: A hollow death fails the encounter while other characters live and forces a rest that a zero dial cannot pay; tying the loss to the required rest also covers a total wipe.
- Source: A-002, B-012, CMP-0020, CMP-0006

### CH-07-003 · §7.1 Objective and Loss Conditions

- After: "If the party chose player versus player (PvP) during setup, a PvP *encounter* is fought after each boss is defeated (see §20)."
- Rationale: PvP is fought after every boss, as §20 states; "intimate victor" and the advisory "Players should agree" are removed.

### CH-07-016 · §7.5 Encounter Start
- Type: mechanical change
- Before: "Characters may skip previously cleared encounters at the cost of two stamina cubes placed in the endurance bar of each character."
- After: "Each character places one *stamina cube* on their *endurance bar* for each encounter skipped since the party last fought an encounter or rested at the bonfire, to a maximum of three."
- Rationale: Two stamina per skip left a hollow character with no free space after three skips; one per skip, capped at three, keeps the toll survivable. The party may skip only if every character has the free spaces.
- Source: Gate 1 — OQ-10; B-014, A-008
