# Change log — chapter 14 (Crystal Lizards)

### CH-14-001 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: style
- Before: "# CRYSTAL LIZARDS" / "Crystal lizards are strange and glimmering reptiles that offer character's powerful rewards if the lizard is slain before it can escape." / an empty heading used as a page break
- After: "# 14. Crystal Lizards" / "Crystal lizards are glimmering reptiles whose hides are studded with the rarest titanite."
- Rationale: The first sentence is kept as a chapter intro that holds no rule, with its apostrophe error and "slain" removed. The empty heading is deleted. The chapter is short enough to need no numbered sections.
- Source: F-025, C-015, C-061, C-098, C-104, C-121

### CH-14-002 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: cut
- Before: "Players should include crystal lizards as an optional game component if they own the Iron Keep expansion set. The following rules are assuming the players are playing with the Iron Keep expansion set and the included crystal lizards."
- After: "*Crystal lizards* are an optional rule. They require the Iron Keep expansion. The party decides during setup whether crystal lizards are in play."
- Rationale: The advice and the scope disclaimer are replaced by the fixed optional-rule formula, in the same form as the mimics' opener.
- Source: F-025, C-018, C-124

### CH-14-003 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: clarification
- Before: "Whenever the character reveals an encounter card with the crystal lizard symbol, a crystal lizard appears. During encounter set-up, place a crystal lizard on the terrain node indicated by the encounter card. Some encounters contain multiple crystal lizards."
- After: "When an *encounter card* shows the crystal lizard symbol, one crystal lizard model is placed on each *terrain node* ... that the card marks with that symbol. The models are placed during Encounter Start (see §7.5, Encounter Start)."
- Rationale: Three sentences said one thing. The placing is tied to the step that §7.5 gives it, "set-up" becomes the noun setup elsewhere, and the crystal lizard is stated to be an enemy model under the enemy rules except as the chapter says.
- Source: C-059, C-120

### CH-14-004 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: clarification
- Before: "Crystal lizards take their turns like any other enemy model in descending *threat* order. However, their behaviours dictate that they simply run from the characters which makes killing them challenging."
- After: "On its turn it resolves the *behaviour icons* on its data card, which move it away from the characters. A crystal lizard makes no attack."
- Rationale: The comment on difficulty is cut and the behaviour is stated as a rule: the crystal lizard acts in threat order with the enemies, moves away, and does not attack.
- Source: C-121, C-120

### CH-14-005 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: correction
- Before: "Crystal lizards do not scale their health depending on the number of characters."
- After: "A crystal lizard is not scaled. Neither enemy scaling (see §8.3) nor the *bonfire ascetic* (see §18.7) changes its health or its movement."
- Rationale: The exemption covered health only, so a crystal lizard would still have gained from the other effects of scaling and from the bonfire ascetic. §8.3 lists the crystal lizard among the models that are not scaled and cites this chapter.
- Source: A-028

### CH-14-006 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: clarification
- Before: "When the last enemy of an encounter (beside crystal lizards) dies, any remaining crystal lizards are immediately removed from play. ... Note that an encounter is still considered *cleared* regardless of any surviving crystal lizards."
- After: "Crystal lizards do not count towards clearing an *encounter*. The encounter is *cleared* when every other enemy model has been killed (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution)."
- Rationale: The rule is stated once, without the address to the reader, and in the verbs of the style sheet: enemies are killed, encounters are cleared. The escape without award is kept.
- Source: F-026, C-061, C-122

### CH-14-007 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: correction
- Before: "Whenever a character kills a crystal lizard, they immediately place one Titanite Scale card into their inventory."
- After: "When a character kills a crystal lizard, that character takes one Titanite Scale card from the supply at once and places it in their *inventory* (see §4.2)."
- Rationale: The Treasure Rewards section of the manuscript awarded a Titanite Chunk for the same kill. Titanite Scale is the card the Iron Keep expansion supplies for the purpose and the name this chapter already used; §7.7 now cites this chapter.
- Source: A-096, C-056, D-028, B-032, CMP-0015

### CH-14-008 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: completion
- Before: new text
- After: "The Iron Keep expansion supplies three Titanite Scale cards. When none remains, a killed crystal lizard awards nothing, and no crystal lizard is placed in any later encounter."
- Rationale: With a card awarded for each kill, the manuscript let crystal lizards keep appearing after the three cards were gone, which left the reward undefined.
- Source: CMP-0015, A-097, D-028

### CH-14-009 · §14 Crystal Lizards
- Type: clarification
- Before: "If any number of crystal lizards die during an encounter, no further crystal lizards will spawn on repeated attempts of that encounter. Mark encounter tiles with an upside-down crystal lizard model if any crystal lizard dies during an encounter."
- After: "Once a crystal lizard has been killed in an encounter, no crystal lizard is placed in that encounter again, however many times the encounter is *reset* and fought (see §11.5)."
- Rationale: Present tense replaces the future and the mechanic is unchanged: crystal lizards return until one is killed and never afterwards. The upside-down model is kept as an example of a marker, because only two models are supplied.
- Source: C-122, F-088; preserve list

### CH-14-012 · §14
- Type: ruling
- Before: "When a character kills a crystal lizard, that character takes one Titanite Scale card from the supply at once and places it in their *inventory* (see §4.2)."
- After: "When a crystal lizard is killed, the party is awarded one Titanite Scale card from the supply at once (see §4.2). The players decide which character receives it, as for any card awarded to the party (see §7.7)."
- Rationale: [MECHANICAL CHANGE] An award to the party needs no rule for a crystal lizard killed by a condition or by an attack that hits several models, and it treats this card as every other card won from a kill is treated.
- Source: final rulings, phantom-E24 (`06_final_rulings.md`); Official Iron Keep booklet: the card is added 'to the inventory', which is shared (lines 125-127); §7.7 of this book.

### CH-14-013 · §14
- Type: ruling
- Before: "Once a crystal lizard has been killed in an encounter, no crystal lizard is placed in that encounter again, however many times the encounter is *reset* and fought (see §11.5). The party marks the tile to record the kill, for example by laying the killed model upside down beside the tile's encounter card. Until a crystal lizard has been killed there, the crystal lizards are placed again each time the encounter is set up."
- After: "Once a crystal lizard has been killed in an encounter, no crystal lizard is placed in that encounter again, however many times the encounter is *reset* and fought (see §11.5). To record the kill, one *damage cube* is placed on the encounter card of that encounter, where it stays until the card is returned to the game box. Until a crystal lizard has been killed there, the crystal lizards are placed again each time the encounter is set up."
- Rationale: A definite marker in the book's own idiom (a cube on the encounter card, as for a cleared encounter) replaces an example that fails on the second marked tile, because only two crystal lizard models are supplied.
- Source: final rulings, phantom-E25 (`06_final_rulings.md`); §2.2 of this book (2x Crystal Lizard); official Iron Keep booklet suggests 'an opened chest token beside the tile' (lines 128-131); §7.5 (black cube marks a cleared encounter).
