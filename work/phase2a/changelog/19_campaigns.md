# Change log — chapter 19 (Campaigns)

### CH-19-001 · §19 Campaigns
- Type: restructure
- Before: "# CAMPAIGNS" followed by thirteen H3 campaign headings, nested bullet lists, and two empty headings used as page breaks
- After: "# 19. Campaigns" with twelve numbered H2 campaigns (19.1 The First Journey to 19.12 Big Game Hunting); each section is an H3 followed by a numbered list of tiles
- Rationale: The chapter jumped from H1 to H3, used bullets for ordered tile sequences, and carried empty headings. Every campaign now has the same shape: requirement sentence, special rules, sections.

The party does not place *invader tokens* during this campaign (see §12.2). An *encounter* that lists an *invader* includes that invader every time the characters enter that encounter, until the invader is killed. A listed invader that has been killed does not appear again. - Source: final rulings, campaign-E35 (`06_final_rulings.md`); DS-Phantoms-Rules-lores.txt 636-640: 'Unlike the standard invader rules or other campaign encounters, when an encounter lists an invader, that invader is part of the encounter every time the party enters that encounter until the invader is defeated.'

### CH-19-048 · §19.15
- Type: ruling
- Before: "Both invaders are part of that encounter every time the characters enter it, until both have been killed during one visit. If either invader vanishes, or the encounter is *failed*, both return the next time the characters enter it."
- After: "Both invaders are part of that encounter every time the characters enter it, until both have been killed during one visit. If either invader vanishes, or the encounter is *failed*, both return the next time the characters enter it. - Rationale: [MECHANICAL CHANGE] Without this the campaign could dead-end: the invaders vanish at a character's death, the survivors clear the encounter, and a cleared Level 4 encounter is otherwise never fought again.
