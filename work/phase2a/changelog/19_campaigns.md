# Change log — chapter 19 (Campaigns)

### CH-19-001 · §19 Campaigns
- Type: restructure
- Before: "# CAMPAIGNS" followed by thirteen H3 campaign headings, nested bullet lists, and two empty headings used as page breaks
- After: "# 19. Campaigns" with twelve numbered H2 campaigns (19.1 The First Journey to 19.12 Big Game Hunting); each section is an H3 followed by a numbered list of tiles
- Rationale: The chapter jumped from H1 to H3, used bullets for ordered tile sequences, and carried empty headings. Every campaign now has the same shape: requirement sentence, special rules, sections.
- Source: C-104, C-098, C-101, C-105, CMP-0008

### CH-19-002 · §19 Campaigns
- Type: completion
- Before: new text
- After: "Each campaign states the sets it requires and lists the tiles of each *section* in order outward from the *bonfire* tile; a section is laid out and completed as the campaign setup rules state (see §18.1)."
- Rationale: The chapter had no introduction. A 39-word introduction and one pointer sentence are added; the count is twelve after the duplicate campaign is removed, and layout and completion are delegated to their owning section.
