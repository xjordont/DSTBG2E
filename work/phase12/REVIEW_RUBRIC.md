# Review rubric for the final consolidation pass

Assembled on 2026-11-24 from two community Claude skills for rulebook work, read as reference and not installed: the "rulebook writing" skill of the fil512/upship project (three rules) and the "rulebook editor" skill listed on agensi.io (seven failure modes). The house rules on voice and form are in section 2 of `REWRITE_BRIEF.md` and come first.

## Three rules

1. **Clarity over brevity.** A longer sentence that cannot be misread beats a shorter one that can. (The brief asks for terse text; where the two pull apart, the reader's first game decides.)
2. **Sequential disclosure.** A concept is introduced where the player first meets it, or the sentence points to where it is.
3. **One term for one thing.** The same word every time; the glossary headword is the word.

## Seven failure modes to look for

1. **Terminology drift.** One thing with two names (token and marker; rest and return to the bonfire).
2. **Forward reference without foundation.** A rule that assumes a later rule and gives no pointer.
3. **Phantom condition.** "If X happens, do Y" where nothing says what makes X happen.
4. **Permission gap.** The book never says whether a thing a player will want to do is allowed.
5. **Buried exception.** An exception in the middle of a paragraph, or far from the rule it limits.
6. **Setup or teardown gap.** A stranger could not set up, reset or pack away from the steps alone.
7. **Written against meant.** The words say something other than what the decision record says was decided.

## Redundancy

Each rule has one owner (section 4 of the brief). A second full statement of a rule elsewhere is a defect: it is cut, or cut down to a pointer "(see §N.M)". Two statements that differ are a contradiction and come first.
