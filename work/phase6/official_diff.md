# Dark Souls: The Board Game, 2017 core rules against 2022 core rules (Tomb of Giants)

Sources read in full: the 2017 core rulebook ("2017"), the 2022 Tomb of Giants rulebook ("2022") and the 2017 FAQ (last updated 11 April 2017). Page numbers are the numbers printed in each book ("p." for 2017 and "TG p." for 2022). Where a rule is not in a book the cell says "not found in the text". Statuses: SAME, CHANGED, NEW/REMOVED (NEW = only in 2022, REMOVED = only in 2017). Notes marked "(inferred)" are my reading of how two texts interact, not a quoted rule. Text from a PDF extraction was read with the columns interleaved; nothing below depends on pictures only.

## 1. Setup

| Rule | 2017 | 2022 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sparks by number of players | 1 player 5, 2 players 4, 3 players 3, 4 players 2; spark dial sits on the Bonfire tile (p. 8). | Identical table (1 = 5, 2 = 4, 3 = 3, 4 = 2); dial sits in the spark dial slot of the campaign dashboard (TG p. 14). | SAME |
| What a spark does | Dial goes down one when the party is defeated or chooses to rest. At 0 the party can no longer rest and the next character death loses the game (p. 8). | Dial goes down one every time the party returns to the bonfire, whether defeated or by choice. If the dial is 0 and the party returns to the bonfire, the game is lost (TG p. 15, 26, 27). | CHANGED |
| Starting souls | Soul cache starts with 0; in a one-player game it starts with 16 souls (p. 13). | Starting souls not found in the text (no soul figure is given in setup, solo or otherwise). | CHANGED (2022 figure and the solo allowance not found in the text) |
| Starting equipment and tokens | Each player takes a character board and starting equipment (armour in the armour slot, others in hand or backup slots); one Estus, one Heroic Action and one Luck token, all ready; four Level Up cubes at Base; characters begin on the Bonfire tile (p. 9). | Each player takes a dashboard and the starting equipment cards bearing the character's symbol; Estus, heroic action and luck tokens all ready; stat squares stay in the dashboard and are punched out and flipped on upgrade (TG p. 13, 14). | SAME in substance; mechanism for tracking upgrades differs |
| Treasure deck construction | Shuffle all 60 common treasure cards with each chosen character's five class treasure cards; place face down on the Bonfire tile. Transposed, legendary and boss treasure cards are held back face up (TG p. 14). Number of class cards per character not stated in the text. | SAME (2022 gives no per-character count) |
| Event deck | Not found in the text. | Shuffle the 30 event cards and place them face down in the event deck slot (TG p. 6, 14). | NEW |
| How encounters are chosen | Separate encounter cards by level and shuffle. | Layout of the play area | Bonfire tile placed, then four of the six shuffled exploration tiles laid around it, doorways aligned; Fog Gate token on a wall of a tile farthest from the Bonfire tile; the other two tiles go back in the box. | Boss selection | Players choose which mini boss to fight first (four available) and later a main boss (two available); set that boss's model, dial, data card, behaviour cards and treasure aside (p. 5, 8, 9). | No choice: the Black Knight (mini boss) and Gravelord Nito (main boss) encounter cards are placed face up in their slots on the encounter track (TG p. 14). | CHANGED |

After killing the Black Knight: boss treasure to the inventory; shuffle the legendary, Nito and transposed cards into the treasure deck; spark dial goes up by 1; each character gains an ember token; the Black Knight card is flipped to its bonfire side and becomes the new rest location; one free return to the bonfire (TG p. 31). | CHANGED |
| Number of characters | 1 to 4 players (p. 3). | 1 to 3 players in the box, up to 4 with characters from the 2017 game or an expansion (TG p. 3). | CHANGED |

## 3. Character turn

| Rule | 2017 | 2022 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure of the turn | Start-of-activation steps, then move and attack, then the activation ends (p. 22). | An upkeep phase followed by an action phase (TG p. 20). | CHANGED (naming and structure; steps below) |
| Stamina at the start | Gain 2 stamina (p. 22). | Recover 3 stamina in upkeep (TG p. 20). | CHANGED |
| Other start-of-turn steps | Gain the aggro token; may swap items between backup slot and hand slots (p. 22). | Gain the aggro token; may swap any number of items between backup and hand slots; and "each other character can move one node", which is the wording printed in the upkeep list (TG p. 20). The extraction gives no further detail on that last step. | CHANGED (extra step; wording unclear) |
| Walk | Walk costs 0 stamina, once per activation, one node (p. 22). | A character may move onto an adjacent node as the basic move; no stamina is charged (TG p. 20). | SAME |
| Run | Run costs 1 stamina per node, any number of times (p. 22). | After moving, the character can run: any number of extra nodes at 1 stamina each (TG p. 20). Both examples cost 3 stamina for a walk and three runs. | SAME |
| Movement relative to attacking | Move before attacking or after attacking, but not both (p. 22). | Move then act, or act then move; either may be skipped (TG p. 20). | SAME |
| Attacks per turn | One attack with each weapon in the hand slots (p. 22). | One action; an attack uses one weapon, and a weapon in the other hand slot may then also attack as part of the same action (TG p. 20). | SAME in effect |
| Attack resolution | Spend the option's stamina, target an enemy in range, roll the dice, each pip is 1 damage, subtract Block (physical) or Resist (magical), place wounds (p. 22). | Same sequence, listed as seven steps; extra black die against a boss weak arc is added at step 3 (TG p. 20, 29). | SAME |
| Swapping equipment | In the start-of-activation step between backup and hand slots (p. 22). Other changes only at Blacksmith Andre (p. 14). Backup slot holds several weapons, up to three weapons in total (p. 12). | Same upkeep swap (TG p. 20). The backup slot holds up to two cards of any type, and cards in it are never equipped (TG p. 8). Equipment can also be exchanged between dashboards and the inventory before starting the next encounter without resting (TG p. 18) and at the bonfire (TG p. 27). | CHANGED |
| Estus flask | During activation, flip to empty to remove all black and red cubes from the endurance bar; refilled on resting (p. 11). | At any time during their turn, flip to used to remove all stamina and wound tokens; ready again on resting (TG p. 9, 26). | SAME |
| Luck | Flip to reroll one die in the character's attack, block or dodge roll; ready again on resting or by paying 1 soul at the Firekeeper (p. 11, 15). | Flip at any time to reroll one die in one of the character's attack or defence rolls; ready again on resting (TG p. 9, 26) or through a "Refresh" reward (TG p. 19). Paying a soul to restore it not found in the text. | CHANGED |
| Heroic actions | Class special ability, limited use; flip the token to used; ready at game start and after each rest (p. 11). | Flip at any time during their turn to resolve the dashboard's heroic action; ready after resting or with a Refresh reward (TG p. 9, 19, 26). | SAME (timing wording is wider in 2022) |
| Interacting with terrain | Not found in the text (chests open automatically at the end of the encounter; barrels cost extra stamina to enter). | A character can spend their action on a chest, an illuminated gravestone or a lever; doing so prevents an attack that turn (TG p. 12, 20). | NEW |
| Torch | Not found in the text. | A character on the torch's node can pick it up or put it down at any time in their turn; picking it up gains the aggro token (TG p. 12). | NEW |
| Equipment modifiers | Fixed values added to or subtracted from the dice total (p. 12). | Applied in step 4 of the attack sequence (TG p. 21). | SAME |
| Weapon abilities | Magical, option range, Shift, Node, Shaft, Repeat (p. 23). | Same set; repeat extra attacks need not hit the same enemy and cost no additional stamina (TG p. 22). | SAME |

## 4. Defence

| Rule | 2017 | 2022 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall method | The player chooses between a block or resist roll and a dodge roll; a dodge replaces the block or resist roll (p. 25). | A single roll: dodge dice and block (physical) or resist (magical) dice are gathered and rolled together (TG p. 25). | CHANGED |
| Block and resist dice | Roll as many dice as the block (physical) or resist (magical) icons on equipped items (armour and hand slots); subtract the total from the attack; at or over the attack value means 0 damage (p. 25). | The same dice are added to the dodge dice; after a failed dodge the face-up pips on those other dice reduce the damage total (TG p. 25). | SAME for the dice counted; CHANGED for how they are rolled |
| Which dice count | Dodge: only dodge icons. Block or resist: the block or resist dice, summed (p. 25). | Dodge: face-up dodge results. Damage reduction: pips on the other dice (TG p. 25). The pips of block or resist dice are counted only if the dodge fails. | CHANGED (inferred from step 5 to step 7) |
| Dodge cost | Pay 1 stamina first and move 1 node before rolling (p. 25). | No cost to roll the dodge dice. If the dodge succeeds the target may then spend 1 stamina to move one node (TG p. 25). | CHANGED |
| Choosing to dodge | Optional, chosen before the dice are rolled. | Not a choice; dodge dice are always rolled with the defence dice. | CHANGED |
| What dodge success means | The character suffers 0 damage and is not hit, so no push and no condition (p. 25). | The attack misses; later steps (damage, conditions, pushes) are not resolved (TG p. 25). | SAME |
| What dodge failure means | The character is hit and suffers the attack's full damage (p. 25). | The target is hit; the block or resist pips reduce the damage, then conditions and pushes apply even at 0 damage (TG p. 25). | CHANGED (a failed dodge no longer means full damage) |
| Dodge dice with 0 dodge value | A character with 0 dodge can still choose to dodge, moves one node and is automatically hit if the difficulty is 1 or higher (FAQ). | Not found in the text (no dodge dice means no dodge successes at difficulty 1 or higher; inferred). | not found in 2022 |
| Movement on a dodge | One node in any direction, before the roll (p. 25). Against a boss a dodging character on the boss's node may move to any arc or any adjacent node (p. 28). | After the roll, only if the dodge succeeded and the player pays 1 stamina; against a boss the move may ignore arc rules, onto any adjacent node, or stay and take any arc (TG p. 25, 29). | CHANGED |
| Dodging non-attacks | Cannot dodge to reposition when an enemy's movement has a push without damage, nor when the attack does not reach (FAQ). | Out-of-range attacks miss at step 1 and later steps, including the stamina move, do not happen (TG p. 25, inferred). | SAME in effect (via different mechanism) |
| Traps | A trap's damage cannot be blocked; the character takes the damage or attempts a dodge (p. 18). | Roll only dodge dice against the token's dodge value; failure means the token's damage (TG p. 12). | CHANGED |
| Ember effect | Damage of 3 or more from an attack is reduced by 1 (p. 12). | Damage of 3 or more from a single attack is reduced by 1 (TG p. 9). | SAME |
| Luck in defence | One die reroll in a block or dodge roll (p. 11). | One die reroll in an attack or defence roll (TG p. 9). | SAME |

## The 2022 changes in one page

- Defence is a single combined roll: dodge dice plus block or resist dice are rolled together; a dodge success is automatic and not chosen; dodge costs nothing, a success lets the target spend 1 stamina to move one node afterwards; a failed dodge still lets the block or resist pips reduce damage, and pushes or conditions apply even at 0 damage (TG p. 25). In 2017 dodge was an optional alternative that cost 1 stamina and a one-node move first, and a failure meant full damage (p. 25).
- The campaign dashboard replaces tile exploration: one encounter per encounter-track space, no Fog Gate, one fixed map per encounter, and the party returns to the first space after each rest (TG p. 13, 16, 27).
- Encounter choice: draw two cards from the level deck and choose one, shuffling the other back; replay the stored card on a repeated space (TG p. 16). In 2017 the encounter cards were drawn at random by level and placed face down on tiles (p. 8).
- Every return to the bonfire costs a spark, including voluntary returns after a win; at 0 sparks returning loses the game; Andre and the Firekeeper can only be visited after the resting steps (TG p. 15, 26). In 2017 visits were possible without resting and a rest at 0 sparks was merely forbidden (p. 8, 13 to 15).
- Treasure purchase: draw the top four cards, buy any number at 1 soul each, return the rest to the bottom, once per visit (TG p. 27). In 2017 one soul bought one card drawn blind with no limit (p. 14).
- Encounters are driven by card objectives and keywords: kill, Survive, Exit, Occupy, Onslaught, Darkness, Timer, Respawn, Trial, plus a timer track and a number-of-players symbol (TG p. 16 to 18). Only enemies on active tiles act (TG p. 24).
- Rewards come from the card: Souls, Draw, Search, Refresh, Trial, Shortcut; a drawn event card follows each completed encounter (immediate, consumable, rendezvous) (TG p. 18, 19). 2017 gave a flat 2 souls per character and chests gave two cards on victory (p. 17, 19).
- Failure: all cache souls are placed on the failed encounter card and are recovered when it is later completed; other cards' souls are discarded (TG p. 18). In 2017 the souls dropped on the node of death and one character walked onto them (p. 19).
- Stamina at the start of a character's turn is 3 (it was 2); upkeep and action phases; an action may be spent on a chest, gravestone or lever instead of an attack (TG p. 20).
- Frostbite on a character now means recovering 1 less stamina at the start of its turn (it was 1 extra stamina per walk, run or dodge); conditions are discarded at the end of the model's turn (TG p. 21).
- Death occurs when a token would be added to a full endurance bar (2017: all ten boxes covered); the bar is cleared after both wins and failures (TG p. 9, 18).
- Mini boss payoff: +1 spark (2017 reset the dial), embers for every character, legendary, Nito and transposed cards shuffled into the deck, the Black Knight space becomes the new bonfire, and one free return to the bonfire; no tile or encounter reset (TG p. 31).
- Boss setup is fixed to the Black Knight and Gravelord Nito; players do not pick bosses, and gravestones are no longer used to reveal behaviour cards (TG p. 14, 12).
- The first-activation token is gone; the players choose who goes first in every encounter (TG p. 17). Aggro at the start goes to a chosen character, not the one who led the way (TG p. 17).
- Terrain overhaul: barrels are discarded on entry and pay souls at the end; chests are opened with an action and each gives one card into the inventory; gravestones peek at decks; traps are dodge-only and placed under a different rule; new lever, shrine and torch (the torch grants aggro and stops others gaining it) (TG p. 12).
- Equipment and upgrades: backup slot holds up to two cards of any type; equipment can be swapped between encounters; weapon upgrades can now be removed at the bonfire (TG p. 8, 18, 27).
- Enemies: any enemy can be placed on its target's node; skeletons resurrect via the Necromancer; enemy movement pushes attack every character pushed, starting node included (TG p. 24, 25).
- Ember cards: the drawing players either resolve the card at once or store it in the inventory (TG p. 10).
- Pushes cannot move a model onto a different tile; a pushed or displaced model is placed on an adjacent node (TG p. 11, 21).
- Upkeep includes a line stating that each other character can move one node; the extraction gives no more detail, so treat this as unverified (TG p. 20).
- Removed from 2022 (or not found in the text): the 16-soul solo start, paying a soul to restore Luck, the whole 2017 campaign ruleset (extra sparks, joining or leaving, dashing through, selling treasure, higher level-up costs, Tier 4), the Fog Gate and mid-game reset, and the Poison-triggers-Bleed FAQ ruling.
- Unchanged in 2022: spark table by player count, level-up costs of 2/4/8, 1 soul per treasure, bleed and poison, enemy order by threat level, targeting by aggro and nearest, boss arcs, weak arcs, heat-up, behaviour deck cycling and Estus, luck and heroic-action resets on resting.
