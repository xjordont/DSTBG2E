# Spending souls, the bonfire, and treasure set-up: local-source survey

Scope: two surveys made only from local files. Survey 1 covers spending souls and the bonfire. Survey 2 covers treasure details in the community rulebooks, and only adds to `work/phase6/treasure_split.md` (read first; its contents are not repeated).

Conventions:
- "n/s" means the source does not state it. "Quoted" text is copied from the extraction or from a survey report; where a figure comes from a survey report rather than the original text, the report is named.
- Codes A1 to C9 are those of `work/phase5/house_a.md`, `house_b.md`, `house_c.md` and `work/phase6/house_matrix.md`. For C8 (Hollowed Edition v1.3) and C9 (Extended Rules V45) I also read the full text in `work/text/`. The four files in `work/text/house/` are called H-Bare (DSTBGBasucChanges), H-Zed (DSTBGZed), H-Jolly (JollySouls2021) and H-Combined (DSBG_Combined_Edition_V1.0_compressed).
- "Rest" means the official bonfire rest. In the 2017 core, a rest turns the spark dial down one. The 2022 book turns it down on every return to the bonfire.
- The expansion books (work/text/DS-*-Rules*.txt) and the FAQ were checked for "spark", "soul", "shop" and "Andre". They only repeat the core spark reset or the "spend a spark to rest before the mega boss" step, and add nothing to the tables below.

# Survey 1. Spending souls and the bonfire

## 1.1 Official rules and the author's manuscript

### 2017 core rules (work/text/DS-Core-Rules-lores.txt; p. numbers are the book's)

- (a) Levelling and shopping are not tied to a rest. Visiting is separate from resting, and resting is an optional act.
  - Andre (p. 14): "When the party returns to the bonfire between encounters, they may visit Blacksmith Andre to purchase treasure, change equipment, and upgrade equipment." "To purchase treasure, remove 1 soul from the soul cache. Draw the top card from the treasure deck and reveal it... The only limit to the number of times players may purchase treasure is the number of souls in the soul cache."
  - Firekeeper (p. 15): "The party may visit the Firekeeper to level up and restore luck." Luck restore costs 1 soul.
  - Rest (p. 15): "The party may also choose to rest at the bonfire any time they are on the Bonfire tile."
  - The 2017 versus 2022 comparison in `official_diff.md` records the same reading: "Visiting Andre or the Firekeeper without resting: Allowed... resting is a separate optional act."
- (b) Sparks: 1 player 5, 2 players 4, 3 players 3, 4 players 2 (p. 8). "When the players are defeated in an encounter (p. 19) or choose to rest at the bonfire (p. 15), turn the dial down one number. Once the dial reads 0, the characters can no longer rest there, and the next time a character is killed, the game is lost." Regained: after the mini boss the dial is reset to the starting number (setup step 2 repeated, p. 9; per `official_diff.md`). Campaign (p. 33): "Each time a mini boss, main boss, or mega boss is defeated, the party gains 1 spark instead of resetting the bonfire's sparks." Extra sparks may be bought "at a cost of 2 souls per character in the group, up to a maximum of the starting number of sparks". A joining player costs 1 spark; a leaving player gives 1.
- (c) Rest (p. 15): "1. Turn the spark dial down one number. 2. Flip all Estus Flask tokens to the filled side. 3. Flip all Heroic Action tokens to the ready side. 4. Flip all Luck tokens to the ready side. 5. Turn all encounter cards face down." Resting is the only thing that resets encounters ("it also resets the encounter cards"). The rest after the mini boss is free ("without using a spark", p. 15). Black and red cubes come off after each won encounter, not through resting (p. 19).
- (d) Death (p. 19): "If any character is killed, the party is defeated. When the party is defeated, place all characters on the Bonfire tile and place all soul tokens from the soul cache on the node where the character was killed. The party must now rest at the bonfire... If a character dies before these souls are retrieved, the souls are discarded." Souls sit in one shared cache. The 1-player cache starts with 16 souls (p. 13).
- (e) Standard costs (p. 15): Base to Tier 1 2, Tier 1 to 2 4, Tier 2 to 3 8. No Tier 4. Campaign (p. 33): 4, 8, 16, and "each stat on a character board can be upgraded to Tier 4 at a cost of 20 souls. The value of any stat at Tier 4 is considered to be 40." Campaign shop: treasure "increased to 2 souls per treasure card"; selling back for 1 soul each, sold cards discarded for the campaign.
- (f) Reason: no author reason given. Framing (p. 16): "They'll want to rest at the bonfire along the way, and they're sure to find themselves sent back to the bonfire in defeat from time to time. Until their sparks are exhausted, however, they'll still have a chance to enhance their party." On resets (p. 15): "it also means they can collect additional souls from those encounters!"

### 2022 Tomb of Giants rulebook (work/text/DS-TG-Rulebook-Compressed_compressed.txt)

- (a) Both gated behind a spark-costing rest.
  - Setup page: "Characters can only upgrade when they rest at the bonfire."
  - "Returning to the Bonfire" (p. 26): "When they do so, they can access their inventory, and if they have souls to spend, visit Blacksmith Andre and the Firekeeper." Resting steps come first, "After performing these steps, the party can visit the Firekeeper and/or Blacksmith Andre."
  - Treasure (p. 27): "draw the top four cards from the treasure deck, and place them face up... Players can purchase any number of these cards by spending one soul from the soul cache per card... return the remaining cards to the bottom of the treasure deck. This step can only be done once per visit to the bonfire."
- (b) 1 player 5, 2 players 4, 3 players 3, 4 players 2 (p. 14). "Each time they return to the bonfire, they'll use up a spark. If the spark dial has reached 0, and the players return to the bonfire, they lose the game." Regained: only after the Black Knight (mini boss): "Increase the spark dial by 1, then each character gains an ember token" and "the party can now choose to return to the bonfire without spending a spark" (p. 31). No purchase of sparks found.
- (c) Rest (p. 26): "1. Place the party token in the bonfire slot. 2. Turn the spark dial down one number. 3. Flip each character's heroic action, Estus Flask, and luck tokens to the ready side. 4. Return each encounter card to its normal orientation." The party restarts at the first space of the encounter track, so cleared encounters must be replayed ("By replaying encounters, players can collect the rewards for completing them again too").
- (d) Failure (p. 18): "Place any souls in the soul cache on the encounter card the players failed" and "If any encounter card on the dashboard has one or more soul tokens on top of it, discard the tokens." Completing a card gives its soul tokens back: "If the completed encounter card has one or more soul tokens on top of it, add them to the soul cache." So only the most recent drop survives. The cache is shared; no personal-soul drop rule was found.
- (e) 2, 4, 8 souls (p. 26): "Stats must be upgraded in ascending order... A character can upgrade multiple times in a single visit." No Tier 4 and no campaign costs found (per `official_diff.md`).
- (f) None stated. Nearest wording (p. 15): "the fire has burned too low and they are forever lost to the darkness."

### Author's manuscript (work/phase2a/source/)

- (a) Both gated behind a spark-costing rest. 11_returning_to_the_bonfire.md: "The characters must collectively spend one *spark to* visit the *bonfire* to rest, improve stats, upgrade equipment, and shop... Characters cannot spend their hard-earned *souls* without resting at the *bonfire*." Firekeeper: "Characters may only spend *souls* to improve character stats when resting at the *bonfire*." Andre: "Only when the characters spend a spark at the bonfire to rest, they may visit Blacksmith Andre to upgrade equipment or visit Andre's Shop." Also 03_characters.md: "Characters may only ever upgrade their stats while resting at The Bonfire". Exception: "Characters rest at the *bonfire* without needing to spend a *spark* after defeating a boss or completing player-versus-player (PvP)". In campaign play the free rest after a boss applies only to "the final boss of each section" (18_campaign_play.md).
- (b) Six minus the characters, which gives 5, 4, 3, 2 for 1 to 4 players (06_setup.md, 07_gameplay.md). "Defeating a mini-boss or main-boss provides one additional spark. Sparks can not be gained by any other means." Setup after the main boss also adds one spark (before a mega boss). Campaign: starting number is also the maximum; +1 on each mini, main or mega boss; "Sparks cannot be purchased or gained otherwise"; a joining player costs one spark, a leaving player gives one.
- (c) Rest costs one spark and gives: remove all damage and stamina; remove all hollow cubes; flip luck, Estus and heroic tokens; pay the Firekeeper; buy and sell at Andre's Shop; revive "lost" characters (those who died while hollow). Cleared and failed encounters reset: "*Cleared* encounters are reset when characters rest at the *bonfire*" (07_gameplay.md). Unlike the official rest, hollow status is only removed by a spark or an ember.
- (d) Death (07_gameplay.md): "Drop all *souls* from the dashboard onto the current encounter tile"; the model lies on its side next to the bonfire. A character who died is revived hollow if any survivor clears the encounter. Souls are personal: "*Souls* cannot be transferred between characters." "The character who dropped them by successfully clearing or mastering the encounter on which they were dropped can reclaim dropped souls. Dropped *souls* are lost forever if the character who dropped them dies before reclaiming the *souls*." Failed encounter: all characters drop souls on the tile. After the main boss: "Return any dropped souls to the characters."
- (e) Basic game: 2, 4, 8, 8 (Tiers 1 to 4). "Tier four stats are all considered 5 greater than the tier three stat value"; marked with a red cube replacing the tier three white cube. Campaign: 4, 8, 16, 20 (18_campaign_play.md), "increased in Campaign play to slow the characters' progression to scale better across a longer journey."
- (f) Reasons: "Resting at the *bonfire* is paramount to restoring the characters' strength and keeping them from being *lost* to the *hollow.*... *Sparks* are the most precious resource in this game and the players must carefully weigh the timing of each *spark* spent." (11_returning_to_the_bonfire.md). Front matter (00_front_matter.md) names the problems the whole manuscript answers: "long playtime due to grinding" and "an unthematic store which sold players items they never wanted... Shopping remained random". Neither sentence is tied to the rest gate specifically.

## 1.5 Stated reasons for gating or not gating spending

None of these is an author's stated reason for the rest gate in the official books.

- Manuscript, for gating: "*Sparks* are the most precious resource in this game and the players must carefully weigh the timing of each *spark* spent."
- B9, for not gating: "a steady and rapid character progression, eliminating the need to grind encounters and significantly shortening playtime from the original rules." Rule 12: "players can freely perform bonfire actions without having to move to the bonfire or use bonfire sparks (bonfire sparks are only used in the event of character death)."
- C3, for not gating: "There are no regular enemies or grinding."
- H-Zed, for removing the whole spark and reset system (Problems to fix): "Overly grindy, particularly after you have cleared the rooms twice already" and "The sparks mechanic, while thematic to a degree, is not fun to play." Solution: "Refresh Flask, Heroic and Luck tokens after each and every room/attempt. Consider yourself at the Bonfire for all game effects."
- H-Jolly: "Grind, randomness and repetitiveness are not what made Dark Souls great." Optional "Grind / Bonfire Ascetic rule: Players can choose to reset encounters but must replace one lowest tier encounter with a one level higher encounter and add an invader."
- C8: "less grindy" and "more focused on player decision making and resource management, instead of random chance and grinding" (survey report, section 8). In the text: "Keep in mind that sparks are the only way to reverse hollowing, so use them wisely."
- B10: "the game is too long, too grindy/repetitive and that the treasure deck is too random." B5: "the main flaw of the game is it can take up to 6hrs for a game." A5: "ensures stat upgrade progress is balanced regardless of player count."
- A1 and A6 use a soul streak that ends when the party goes back to spend. A6 states the cost: "this bonus is lost when players go back to the bon fire and level up, buy items, die, ect." C8 and A1 do the same. These authors give a cost, not a reason.
- The phrases "push your luck", "tension" and "gamble" were searched in the manuscript. They do not appear there as reasons. The only "push your luck" reading found for a spending rule is the survey report's own description of the A1 soul streak, which is not the author's wording.

# Survey 2. Treasure details in the community texts (not in treasure_split.md)

Sources read in full: Hollowed Edition v1.3 (C8), Extended Rules V45 (C9), H-Bare, H-Zed, H-Jolly. H-Combined could not be read.

## 2.1 Hollowed Edition v1.3 (work/text/Hollowed_Edition_v1.3.txt)

- Decks: "Shuffle the treasure cards into three piles, according to the rules listed on page 3." Weapon Deck (all weapons, one- and two-handed, ranged and throwable), Armor Deck (armours and shields), Magic Deck (sorceries, miracles, pyromancies, weapon upgrades, armour upgrades). Embers: "Separate all available embers equally into three piles, and shuffle these into each deck before the game begins. In case of uneven distribution, the Magic Deck gets priority."
- Tiers: no tiers by stat requirement. Two phases instead: "The game has two phases: Early Game and Late Game. Players begin in the Early Game phase, and go to the Late Game phase after defeating the first boss." Tiers 3 and 4 of stats are locked in the Early Game.
- Transposed and legendary: "When the party reaches the Late Game phase, shuffle all active transposed class items, and all legendary items into their respective decks." Quick reference: "In the Late Game phase, transposed items, and legendary items become available." They are shuffled into the matching kind deck (weapon, armour or magic), not a separate deck.
- Class cards: "When the game begins, shuffle all active basic class items into their respective decks." "Active" appears to mean the classes in play; the text does not define it.
- Shop: "Andre has three item decks to purchase from. These decks are sorted before the game begins." "Purchasing an item from any of the decks costs 1 soul." "Buy from the shop when at the Bonfire." No face-up row, shop size or restock rule is given. It does not say whether the buyer sees the card first or draws blind. Embers "can be found when buying items at the shop".
- Selling: n/s.
- Treasure per encounter: none. Encounters pay souls only (see Survey 1). Treasure sources: a chest, "receive one random boss treasure, amongst the unused boss treasures of any tier of boss"; a mimic counts as "opening two chests"; crystal lizards ("optional chances for treasure"); invaders ("their specific items"); each boss "also reward[s] their respective boss items". Players "choose whether to open or forgo the chest"; a forgone chest "can only be opened again if the encounter is repopulated".
- Errata: boss items are only received from the last of Ornstein and Smough to fall.

## 2.2 Extended Rules V45 (work/text/DS-Extended-Rules-V45.txt, "Treasure", p. 3)

- Decks: "Before the game, set up two treasure decks."
  - Deck 1, used "only during the dungeon leading up to a mini-boss": "All the available Titanite Shards", "Half the available Ember cards (rounded down)", "Character treasure cards", "All non-gem, non-Ember cards that have stat requirements of less than 25."
  - Deck 2, for the dungeon "leading up to a main boss or mega-boss": "Half the available Ember cards (rounded up)", "Character Transposed treasure cards", "5 random Legendary treasure cards", "Remaining gem cards", "All non-gem upgrade, non-Ember cards that have at least one stat requirement of greater than or equal to 25."
- Tiers: the 25 requirement split and a mini-boss versus main-boss split. The text says "Character treasure cards" without saying whether all classes or only those in play. This matches the Deck 1 and Deck 2 split already noted in treasure_split.md; the extra detail here is where the gems and upgrades fall.
- Shop, prices, selling: not stated. The Revised Rules say only "Gain/pay souls as though playing with standard core rules for every other purpose", so the 2017 blind 1-soul purchase applies.
- Pre-boss starter kits: Main boss setup: "Assemble Deck 1 and randomly deal each player 3 weapons, 2 armors, 1 shield, and 2 items that are not a weapon, armor or shield. From that pool of cards, each player may equip up to 4 of the items and immediately raise their stats to be able to equip those items. Re-draw an item if a player cannot legally equip it. The remaining items go to the shared inventory." Mega-boss setup repeats this with Deck 2 after the main-boss step. These are the only deal-out rules for treasure found in the text.
- Per chest or encounter: no change to the core count in the revised rules. Individual encounters add one-off rules. Examples: Withered Thicket: "The chest has a number of treasures equal to the number of players." A different chest keeps "drawing treasure until you draw a legendary or class-specific transposed treasure", shuffling the rest back. Gravestone effects let a player pay souls to search the deck for a named card, for example "3 souls to search the Treasure Deck for a Crystal Gem, or 4 souls to search the Treasure Deck for a Crystal Shield", or "4 souls to search the deck for Great Magic Weapon". "When a player finds an item, search through the Treasure Deck for that item, place it in the inventory, then shuffle the Treasure Deck."
- Scenarios (p. 23 onward): "Set each player's class-specific non-transposed treasure aside (do not shuffle into the main Treasure Deck). Players cannot use souls to draw treasures or increase stats unless otherwise noted." Completion rewards are "Each player draws a treasure from their class-specific treasure deck at random. Increase their stats such that it is legal for them to equip the treasure", or from the "class-specific transposed treasure deck" in later scenarios. This is the only place in C9 where class cards are held in their own per-player deck.
- Transposed timing: Deck 2, so only before a main or mega boss. Legendary timing: same (5 random cards).

## 2.3 H-Bare (work/text/house/DSTBGBasucChanges.txt, "Bare Essential Rule Changes")

- Decks: "Set aside all boss treasure cards. Then separate the rest of the equipment cards" into four piles: Legendary (all legendary cards); Level 1 ("highest stat requirement is lower or equal 24 and two Ember cards"); Level 2 ("25 to 31 and one Ember card"); Level 3 ("32 or higher and one Ember card").
- Treasure per encounter: "place a random face down treasure card per character to each map tile following that encounter level", from the matching level deck, rewarded when the party wins.
- Chest: "draw two treasure cards from Treasure Deck level following the encounter level + 1", with legendary for a level 3 encounter.
- Shop: "choose level 1 - 3 Treasure Deck, remove souls equal to the Deck level from the soul cache. Draw the top card from that Treasure Deck." So price is 1, 2 or 3 souls by deck, drawn blind. Legendary cannot be bought.
- Legendary timing: "Setup after Mini Boss: Draw 5 random legendary cards. Add them to level 3 Treasure Deck." Transposed: not mentioned (they would fall into a level deck by stat requirement). Class cards: not mentioned. Selling: not mentioned.

## 2.4 H-Zed (work/text/house/DSTBGZed.txt, "Zed Mod v1.1")

## 2.5 H-Jolly (work/text/house/JollySouls2021.txt, optional "Recommended market")

- Decks: three. "All unused boss items go into a separate 'Boss' deck. - Shop: "A completed encounter reveals 4 items from 'Market' at the bonfire, available to purchase for 2 souls each. For each boss defeated, 1 of the bonfire cards can come from 'Treasure'. ... You cannot pay to reveal items at the bonfire".
