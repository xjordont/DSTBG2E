# Cross-ruleset matrix: fan-made house rules for Dark Souls: The Board Game

Source: the three survey reports `work/phase5/house_a.md` (codes A1-A10), `house_b.md` (B1-B11) and `house_c.md` (C1-C9). Every cell comes from those reports; "not stated" means the report is silent. Counts are mine, made from the reports. "Verbatim" quotes are copied from the reports (which themselves quote the rulesets).

Caveats that affect every count:
- Two files could not be read by the surveyors (A2, C7), so they appear in no count.
- The official "Classic Dungeon Crawl" variant appears three times (A3, B10 section, C9 section) and the "double souls / half sparks" ideas recur across B1, B10, C9 and A5, so some convergence is shared ancestry, not independent invention.
- The brief gives "about 31" rulesets; the reports contain 30 file entries (10 + 11 + 9).
