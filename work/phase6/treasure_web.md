# Dark Souls: The Board Game - treasure handling, web research

Date of research: 15 November 2026. Method: web search plus page fetches. Rulebook PDFs were downloaded and read as text. Reddit and BoardGameGeek pages mostly could not be fetched (HTTP 403/404/503), so community findings below are second-hand (search-engine summaries or articles) unless stated. Treat those as unverified.

## (a) Confirmed official rules

### 2017 core rulebook (Steamforged, "DS Core Rules")
Source: DS-Core-Rules-lores.pdf, https://gamers-hq.de/media/pdf/97/3a/29/DS-Core-Rules-lores.pdf (read directly), cross-checked with the RulesPal summary https://rulespal.com/dark-souls/rulebook.
- Treasure Deck component list (p. 9): 13 starting equipment, 40 class-specific, 60 common, 10 legendary, 18 boss. The rulebook pictures class starting equipment, class treasure and transposed treasure per class. The 40 class-specific figure is quoted; I did not verify whether transposed cards are counted inside it.
- Setup step 6: "Find the common treasure cards and each chosen character's five class treasure cards. Shuffle them together to form the treasure deck." So class cards for absent classes are NOT in the deck. Transposed, legendary and boss cards are set aside at setup.
- Buying: "To purchase treasure, remove 1 soul from the soul cache. Draw the top card from the treasure deck and reveal it." Anyone meeting stat requirements may equip it; otherwise it goes to the inventory. No limit except souls.
- Chests: opening a chest draws two cards from the treasure deck.
- Mini boss: on defeat, "place that boss's treasure cards in the inventory" (boss treasure is a guaranteed reward, not in the deck). Then reset for the main boss.
- Setup after the mini boss: instead of step 6, "Find the five transposed treasure cards for each character. Then find the legendary weapon cards, shuffle those, and randomly select five of them. Add the transposed treasure cards (five per player) and the five selected legendary weapon cards to the treasure deck and shuffle it."
- Main boss treasure is used only in campaign play.
- Campaign: Andre's price rises to 2 souls per card; unwanted cards can be sold for 1 soul and are removed.
- Uncertain: I found no 2017 rule that makes transposed cards a purchase with boss souls; they simply enter the deck after the mini boss.

### 2022 core sets (revised ruleset)
Sources: Tomb of Giants rulebook, https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0602/0156/6449/files/DS-TG-Rulebook-Compressed_compressed.pdf (read as text); Sunless City rulebook, https://steamforged.com/cdn/shop/files/DS-TSC-Rulebook-download-min.pdf (read as text). Painted World of Ariamis rulebook not read (only a RulesPal link seen); assume the same system, unverified.
- Deck contents, Tomb of Giants: 30 class-specific, 45 common, 5 legendary, 5 boss. Sunless City: 30 class-specific, 45 common, 4 legendary, 6 boss.
- Setup: "Take the class specific treasure cards for the characters being used, and shuffle them together with the common treasure cards to create the starting treasure deck." "Place the transposed, legendary, and boss treasure cards ... face up to one side ... used later in the game." (Same wording in both rulebooks.)
- Buying (Blacksmith Andre, "Purchasing Treasure"): "draw the top four cards from the treasure deck, and place them face up in the slots next to the deck. Players can purchase any number of these cards by spending one soul from the soul cache per card ... After the players have made their purchases, return the remaining cards to the bottom of the treasure deck." Once per bonfire visit. So the 2022 "shop" is a four-card row drawn fresh each visit, price still 1 soul. It is not a persistent display.
- Mini boss (Black Knight in Tomb of Giants): its treasure cards (Black Knight Halberd, Black Knight Shield, Blue Titanite) go straight to the inventory. Then "shuffle the legendary treasure cards, Gravelord Nito treasure cards, and transposed treasure cards for the characters in the party into the treasure deck." Sunless City equivalent: Titanite Demon treasure to the inventory, then legendary, Dragon Slayer Ornstein and Executioner Smough treasure, and transposed cards shuffled in (rulebook lines around "Demon treasure cards (Titanite and Titanite Catch Pole)"). Note that in 2022 all legendary cards are added (not a random five), and the final boss's treasure cards are also shuffled into the deck.
- "Search" reward text: search the treasure deck for the named card and place it in the inventory; if it is not in the deck, the reward cannot be claimed.

## (b) Complaints (what I could actually find)
Direct forum complaints could not be retrieved. Findings:
1. Randomness of blind purchase. Miniature Market review (https://miniaturemarket.com/reviewcorner/dark-souls-review), via search summary: most souls go on random draws, equipment comes from a "random lottery". Tabletop Gaming review (https://tabletopgaming.co.uk/reviews/dark-souls-the-board-game-review), via search summary: progress depends heavily on drawing the right treasure early. Paraphrased, not quoted verbatim; I did not read these pages in full.
2. Treasure grinding. Steamforged's own blog (https://steamforged.com/blogs/brands/dark-souls%E2%84%A2-the-board-game-the-new-campaign-system): optimal play was to "farm souls and treasure by using each spark available" before facing the boss, causing fatigue.
3. Gamerant (https://gamerant.com/dark-souls-board-game-house-rules/, Anthony James Puleo Jr., 9 Feb 2021) says players spend souls "only to draw armor they don't need".
4. Not found: any dated complaint specifically about class cards of absent classes (the 2017 rules already exclude them), or about expansion dilution. Only the Darkroot rulebook swap (remove 15 random common cards and replace with Darkroot's 15) was seen via search summary, https://desktopgames.com.ua/games/5951/DS_Darkroot_Rules_web.pdf, not read directly.

## (c) Proposed fixes and how often seen
Small sample (about 6 distinct items), so frequencies are rough.
- Split by item type into stacks (weapons/shields, armour, spells, upgrade materials): seen 2 times (Gamerant article; a 2017 r/DarkSoulsTheBoardGame megathread comment per search summary). Gamerant says it improves reliability but adds setup time and makes the game easier.
  https://gamerant.com/dark-souls-board-game-house-rules/
- Split by game stage (early vs late items): seen 1 time. A commenter called graystoak on the Beasts of War/OnTabletop article: "I've always split the treasure deck into early and late game items." No detail given. https://www.ontabletop.com/?p=1778336 (formerly beastsofwar.com/?p=1778336).
- Look-at-more-cards shop: "Tiny Shop" (look at top two, keep one, bottom the other; commenter noted it does not fix early grind) and a trading-row shop where cards nearer the deck cost more; seen 2 times, both via search summaries of the 2017 megathread (https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/DarkSoulsTheBoardGame/comments/6bz1x1/house_rules_megathread). Could not fetch the page to verify wording.
- Pay extra to keep both looted cards: 1 mention, 2019 "wave 3" megathread, search summary only.
- Tiered treasure by stat requirement, tier prices 1 to 3 souls, higher tiers added as encounters are cleared (labelled a Git Gud / Unpredictable Enemies fan variant): 1 mention, from a search summary only. I could not find or open the document. Uncertain; do not rely on it.
- RPGGeek thread "Treasure House Rules!" (https://rpggeek.com/thread/1778839/treasure-house-rules): per search summary, shop resets only after a new encounter; chest rolls tied to encounter level. Page returned 403, unverified.
- No treasure buying at all; treasure earned per room cleared, with tables by encounter level and player count; boss treasure only from bosses: the "1 Spark Variant - Final" Google Doc (title from PDF metadata, author not shown; search summary attributes to "Greema", unverified). Quote: "Treasure cannot be purchased - treasure cards are earned by clearing a room." Mini and main bosses give "Boss treasure cards only". Goal stated: "% of treasure deck revealed is balanced regardless of player count". Read directly from the exported PDF. https://docs.google.com/document/d/11EuLdAClCHn84xpkhqWK5mbmKb_mrmHTqixGX2ADbAI
- Official fix: 2022 four-card face-up row (see (a)); Steamforged's blog says "making treasure card draws a row of cards to choose from" (https://steamforged.com/blogs/brands/dark-souls-the-board-game-working-with-the-community).
- Classic Dungeon Crawl (official variant, one attempt per encounter): treasure details not retrieved.

Named variants: "Praise the Fun", "Grindless", "Dark Souls Revised Rules" and "Hollowed Edition" returned no hits under those names. A Scribd/idoc "Dark Souls Board Game Variant Rules v.2" (https://www.scribd.com/document/418185797) exists per search but could not be opened.

## (d) Bearing on a three-way split (common+class / transposed / legendary+boss)
- The official rules already separate these groups at setup: transposed, legendary and boss cards start outside the deck. The 2017 rulebook adds transposed and 5 of 10 legendary after the mini boss; 2022 adds all legendary, transposed, and the final-boss cards. Boss cards from the mini boss never enter the deck and go to the inventory.
- The 1 Spark variant is the closest to "legendary and boss as rewards": bosses give boss treasure only, and the deck is not bought from.
- Early/late split by stage (graystoak) matches a pre-/post-mini-boss split, but the official rules already do this for transposed and legendary.
- Nothing found that proposes a separate transposed deck, or buying transposed cards with boss souls.
