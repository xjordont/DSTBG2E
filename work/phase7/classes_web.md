## 2. Starting equipment (2017)

NOT ESTABLISHED for any of the ten classes. What the sources do say:
- [R17] core set contains "13x Starting Equipment Cards" (the 2017 core set: 4 classes, so the split is not stated in the text I read). [R17C] says the Characters Expansion has "23x Starting Equipment Cards" ("three or four starting equipment cards" per class, wording from the rulebook's per-class summary).
- Setup text [R17] p. 9: "Place the armour card on the character board's armour slot and the other cards in the hand slots and/or backup slot." No source I could reach states which card starts in the backup slot.
- [UG] V1: Mercenary starts with the Sellsword Twinblade (two one-handed weapons in 2017; "You could equip both, or one alongside the Wooden Shield the Mercenary also starts with") and a Wooden Shield. Community transcription.
- [GR]: Herald "will start with a Talisman" (paraphrase; Herald's weapon is weak, armour and shield "serviceable").
- Individual card names for Knight, Warrior, Assassin, Herald, Cleric, Deprived, Pyromancer, Sorcerer, Thief: not found. I deliberately do not list names from memory.
- DanDuhon/DSBG-Shuffle: read-only check of lib/dsbg_shuffle_characters.py shows soul-cost stat tables only, no equipment or heroic-action data. The GitHub API was not accessible for a full tree listing.

## 3. Hand/backup slot rules, 2017 (official, verbatim from [R17], "Equipment Cards")

- "While equipped, an item takes up an equipment slot on the character board. Armour cards go into the armour slot. One-handed weapon cards go into either the left-hand slot or the right-hand slot. Two-handed weapon cards go into either the left-hand slot or the right hand slot and require the other slot to be empty. A character can hold up to three weapon cards at a time; all of these weapon cards that are not in hand slots go into the backup slot, which is the only slot that can hold more than one weapon. Weapons in the backup slot provide additional options during encounters (see 'Character Activations', p. 22)."
- Inventory text: "A character can hold only one armour card (in the armour slot) and up to three weapon cards (in the hand and backup slots) at a time."
- Activation start, "Character Activations" p. 22: "When a character starts their activation: They gain 2 Stamina. They gain the Aggro token. They may swap items between their backup slot and hand slots (see 'Equipment Cards', p. 12)."
- Attacks: "a character can make up to one attack with each weapon they are holding in their hand slots."

So: 2 hand slots + 1 backup slot (which can hold more than one weapon, up to three weapons in total) + 1 armour slot. Swapping happens at the start of the character's own activation. A two-handed weapon occupies one hand slot and requires the other to be empty, so in effect both hands.

## 4. 2022 core sets (official, verbatim from rulebook dashboard pictures; text extracted from [TSC] and [TG])

Same heroic actions? No: names differ for some classes and all texts are shortened and rewritten, and for several classes the 2022 action is a different effect.

| Set | Class | 2022 name | 2022 text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunless City | Herald | Perseverence (sic) | "Each character gains 2 stamina." |
| Sunless City | Pyromancer | Explosive Firepower | "This character's next attack with the [magic] symbol gains an additional [dice icon]." (icons not extracted) |
| Sunless City | Warrior | Berserk Charge | "This character's next attack gains [node attack icon] and does not cost stamina. Before making the attack, they can be placed in an adjacent node." |
| Tomb of Giants | Cleric | Keep the Faith | "This character and each other character on the same or an adjacent node heals 2 damage." |
| Tomb of Giants | Pyromancer | Explosive Firepower | same as above |
| Tomb of Giants | Thief | Lucky Break | "This character heals 2 damage and recovers 2 stamina, then flip their luck token to the ready side." |
| Painted World of Ariamis | (roster and texts) | not found | rulebook PDF not located (guessed URLs 404). [UG] V2 text for Sorcerer: "This character's next attack with the magic symbol gains infinite range and costs 0 stamina." Mercenary V2: "This character makes an attack with one of their weapons. This attack costs 0 stamina." |

Other 2022 rule differences visible in [TSC]:
- Equipment slots: armour slot, hand slots, and "The backup slot can hold up to two cards of any type." (2017: up to three weapons total, backup holds the rest.) "Weapon cards have either the [one-handed] or [two-handed] symbol and go in either of the hand slots. If a [two-handed] card is placed in a hand slot, the other hand slot must be empty." "Cards in the armour and hand slots are always considered to be equipped. Cards in the backup slot are never considered to be equipped."
- Heroic action: "At any time during their turn, a character can flip their heroic action token to the used side to resolve the heroic action on their dashboard." (2017: a limited-use ability "used during encounters", ready again at bonfire.)
- Swapping: upkeep phase, "The character can swap any number of items between their backup slot and hand slots." (and 2022 recovers 3 stamina, not 2).
- Starting equipment counts in 2022 sets: Herald 4, Pyromancer 4, Warrior 3 (Sunless City); Cleric, Pyromancer, Thief 4 each (Tomb of Giants).
- 2022 Mercenary: Sellsword Twinblade becomes a two-handed weapon and cannot be used with the Wooden Shield [UG].
