# PDF visual review v2: `build/book_screen.pdf` (128 pp.)

Independent art-direction and proof pass. Spreads read at 85 dpi, suspect pages re-read at 130 dpi. Layout and graphics only; rules content not assessed.

Format: `p.N · SEVERITY · what is wrong · suggested fix`
