# 6. Setup

## 6.1 Character Setup

1. Each player places the dashboard of each character they have chosen in front of them.
2. Equip each character with the *starting equipment* that bears the matching icon.
3. Place the white stat cubes on the base tier spaces of each character's stat progression track.
4. Place the *luck*, *heroic action* and *estus flask* tokens face up on each character's dashboard.
5. Place an *ember* token beside each character's dashboard.
6. Set the red damage cubes and the black stamina cubes within reach of every player.
7. Give the *aggro* token to the character with the highest *threat*.
8. A character adventuring alone starts with six *souls* on their dashboard.

## 6.2 Treasure Deck Setup

All treasure cards of every set the party owns are used. Only the *starting equipment* of characters not being played returns to the box.

- From Tomb of Giants and Painted World of Ariamis, only the treasure cards that are new in those sets are added (see Appendix B). A reprint is not added twice.
- Where Appendix A lists a weapon, the stat requirements given there are used in place of those printed on the card (see Appendix A).

Every treasure card is of one of six types.

### Starting Equipment

Each character starts with three or four equipment cards, already equipped. Starting equipment joins neither treasure deck.

### Common Treasure

*Common treasure* is not marked as starting, class, transposed, legendary or boss treasure. It is the most versatile equipment available. Common treasure is divided between the *tier one* and *tier two* treasure decks. A common treasure card that asks for 25 or more in any stat is tier two. One that asks for less than 25 in every stat is tier one.

### Class Treasure

*Class treasure* is marked with a small white emblem in the top right corner of the card, which shows the class that gains most from it. The emblem has no effect on play. Every class treasure card is tier one.

### Transposed Treasure

*Transposed treasure* is marked with a blue light at the top right of the card, together with a small white class emblem. Every transposed treasure card is tier two.

### Legendary Treasure

*Legendary treasure* is marked with an orange light under a treasure chest at the top right of the card. These are the rarest of relics. They do not appear in every game and are never stocked in the *shop*.

- At setup, shuffle all the legendary treasure cards and set them aside.
- When the *mini-boss* falls, and after the tier two shop is stocked, five of them are drawn unseen and shuffled into the tier two treasure deck (see §6.6).
- The other legendary treasure cards are not used.

### Boss Treasure

Each mini-boss, *main-boss* and *mega-boss* has its own *boss treasure* cards, marked with the boss's silhouette over an orange light at the top right of the card.

- Once the bosses are chosen (see §6.5), find their treasure cards and set them aside. Those of other bosses are not used.
- The mini-boss's and the main-boss's treasure cards are shuffled into the tier two treasure deck together with the five legendary treasure cards.
- From then on they are found as treasure and are never stocked in the shop.
- In the standard game no treasure card is handed over for defeating a boss.
- A mega-boss's treasure cards are kept for its own stage (see §6.7).

### Tier One Treasure Deck

1. Include every class treasure card.
2. Include every common treasure card that asks for less than 25 in every stat.
3. Exclude starting equipment.
4. Exclude *ember* cards.

### Tier Two Treasure Deck

1. Include every transposed treasure card.
2. Include every common treasure card that asks for 25 or more in a stat.
3. Exclude ember cards.
4. Exclude legendary treasure and boss treasure until the mini-boss falls.

Titanite Scale cards and the treasure cards of *invaders* join neither deck.

## 6.3 Shop Setup

Each tier of the *shop* holds the stock in this table.

| Weapons | Armour | Shields | Upgrades | Spells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

### At the Start of the Game

1. Shuffle the *tier one* and *tier two* treasure decks separately.
2. Draw tier one treasure cards one at a time until the tier one shop holds its full stock.
3. Add *ember* cards to the tier one shop equal to the number of characters.
4. Shuffle the cards that were drawn and not needed back into the tier one treasure deck.

The tier two shop is empty until the *mini-boss* falls.

### After a Boss Is Defeated

1. Draw tier one treasure cards one at a time until the tier one shop again holds its full stock. No ember cards are added.
2. Stock or restock the tier two shop from the tier two treasure deck in the same way. A *legendary treasure* or *boss treasure* card that is drawn is not needed.
3. Shuffle the cards that were drawn and not needed back into their decks.

The shops are restocked at no other time.

## 6.4 Bonfire Setup

1. Set the *spark* dial to five less the number of characters.
2. Place every character model on the *bonfire* tile.
3. Place the *tier one* and *tier two* treasure decks beside the bonfire tile.
4. Lay the tier one *shop* cards face up beside the bonfire tile, next to Blacksmith Andre.

## 6.5 Encounter Setup

### Boss Selection

1. The party chooses a *mini-boss* and a *main-boss* and collects their models, *data cards*, *behaviour cards*, health dials and treasure cards.
2. A party that owns a *mega-boss* and wants a longer adventure chooses a mega-boss as well.

### Encounter Tiles

1. Draw four encounter tiles at random. Lay them with the *bonfire* tile and the mini-boss tile in a grid of two tiles by three, with the bonfire tile and the mini-boss tile in opposite corners.
2. Turn each encounter tile so that two of its doorways open onto neighbouring tiles. This makes a single road from the bonfire tile, through the four encounter tiles one after another, to the mini-boss tile.
3. Place the mini-boss's model and data card on the mini-boss tile and build its behaviour deck (see §15.2).
4. Place the *fog gate* token at the doorway of the mini-boss tile (see §15.1).

### Encounter Cards

1. Shuffle the Level 1 {{icon:ast-0007-encounter-level-1}}, Level 2 {{icon:ast-0011-encounter-level-2}} and Level 3 {{icon:ast-0015-encounter-level-3}} *encounter cards* of the mini-boss's set into three decks.
    - Encounter decks hold only the encounter cards of the set the next boss comes from.
    - If the boss's set has no encounter cards of these levels, use those of the core set.
    - The enemies of Tomb of Giants and Painted World of Ariamis appear only on the print-and-play encounter decks of those sets. At the start of setup, players who have a print-and-play deck and the enemies of its set decide whether it replaces the printed encounter decks. If it does, every encounter card of that stage, at every level, comes from it. A print-and-play card and a printed card are never shuffled together.
    - With the Black Knight or Gravelord Nito, the print-and-play deck is the Tomb of Giants deck; with the Heavy Knight or Crossbreed Priscilla, the Painted World deck. For any other boss the players may choose either, or neither.
2. Deal one encounter card of the right level face down to each encounter tile.
    - The mini-boss's data card gives the levels.
    - The levels rise from the tile beside the bonfire tile towards the mini-boss tile.
3. Deal two *tier one* treasure cards face down onto each encounter card.
4. With the Events Expansion, shuffle the *event cards* of each level into a deck of their own and the trap cards into another, and set its treasure cards aside (see §7.5). Deal one event card of the encounter's level face down beside each encounter card.
5. With the Explorers expansion, deal one *ambush card* at random face down beside each encounter tile (see §13).
6. With the Phantoms expansion, *invader* tokens are placed on the encounter cards when an *embered* character provokes an invasion (see §12.2).

## 6.6 Setup After the Mini-Boss

When the *mini-boss* has been defeated and the party has *rested* (see §16.5), set up the road to the *main-boss*.

1. Return any dropped *souls* to the characters who dropped them.
2. Return the used *encounter cards* to the box, and every *event card* that does not lie beside the bonfire tile to the deck of its level. Gather any *ambush cards* and shuffle them.
3. Clear the remaining tokens from the encounter tiles.
4. Shuffle the four encounter tiles and flip each to its other side. Lay them in a straight line that starts at the *bonfire* tile and ends at the main-boss tile, which is the two boss tiles joined as one.
5. Place the *fog gate* token as before.
6. Place the main-boss's model and *data card* on the main-boss tile and build its behaviour deck (see §15.2).
7. Shuffle the Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 encounter cards of the main-boss's set and deal one of the right level face down to each encounter tile.
    - The main-boss's data card gives the levels.
    - The levels rise from the tile beside the bonfire tile towards the main-boss tile.
8. Restock the *tier one* *shop* and stock the *tier two* shop (see §6.3).
9. Shuffle five *legendary treasure* cards drawn at random, the mini-boss's treasure cards and the main-boss's treasure cards into the tier two treasure deck.
10. Deal two tier two treasure cards face down onto each encounter card.
11. Deal one event card beside each encounter card and one ambush card beside each tile, and place *invader* tokens, as in the first setup (see §6.5).
12. Place all character models on the bonfire tile. The party sets out again from the encounter tile nearest the bonfire tile.

## 6.7 Setup After the Main-Boss

This setup is used only if the party chose a *mega-boss*. It follows the defeat of the *main-boss* and the party's *rest* (see §16.5).

1. Return any dropped *souls* to the characters who dropped them.
2. Return the used *encounter cards*, encounter tiles and boss tiles to the box, and every *event card* that does not lie beside the bonfire tile to the deck of its level.
3. Restock both *shops* (see §6.3).
4. Place the mega-boss's game board beside the *bonfire* tile with its Level 4 encounter side up. Place the *fog gate* token at its entrance.
5. Shuffle the mega-boss's Level 4 encounter cards and deal one face down onto the game board. No event card is used for this encounter.
6. Shuffle the mega-boss's treasure cards and deal one face down onto the encounter card, as the treasure of this encounter.
7. Place all character models on the bonfire tile.
8. The party fights the Level 4 encounter as it fought the encounters before it.
9. Once the Level 4 encounter is *cleared*, the party has one last chance to rest at the bonfire, for a *spark*, before it challenges the mega-boss.
10. Flip the game board to its mega-boss side and place the mega-boss model on the marked *node*. Set its *data card* and *heat-up* cards beside the game board and build its behaviour deck (see §15.2).
11. The party fights the mega-boss, with the special rules of that boss (see §17).
