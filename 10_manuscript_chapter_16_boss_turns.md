# 16. Boss Turns

## 16.1 Overview

During a boss encounter the boss takes a turn before each character's turn. The characters take their turns in any order the party chooses, but none takes another turn until every other character has taken as many.

Draw the top card of the behaviour deck at the start of every boss turn. The boss's actions are decided by resolving each icon on the *behaviour card* from left to right. When the right-most icon is resolved, the boss's turn ends and the card is placed face up on the discard pile.

If no cards remain in the behaviour deck at the start of a boss's turn, flip the discard pile face down without shuffling it to renew the deck, then draw the top card as usual. Players gain an advantage over a boss by memorising the order in which its behaviour cards appear each time the deck is renewed.

## 16.2 Boss Movement and Symbols

A boss moves by the rules for enemy movement (see §9.2), with these exceptions.

- **Towards a target.** The boss first turns to line up the centre of its front *arc* with the adjacent *node* closest to the target character. It then moves forward into that node. The boss does this again for each node of movement the *behaviour card* shows.
- **Away from a target.** The boss first turns to line up the centre of its back arc with the adjacent node furthest from the target character. It then moves backwards into that node. The boss does this again for each node of movement the behaviour card shows.
- **A target on the boss's node.** The boss stays on its node and turns to bring the target into its front arc. The character model stays still while the boss model turns beneath it.
- **Onto a character's node.** The character is placed on the boss's base in the arc the character was in when the boss's movement began. If the character was on a node split between two arcs, the player chooses either.
- **No target.** The boss moves the number of nodes shown in the direction shown, without turning.
- **Leap.** A boss that *leaps* is placed directly on its target's node and keeps the facing it had. Each character on that node is *pushed* to any adjacent node of their player's choice, whatever the arc.
- **Turn.** The turn icon turns the boss 90 degrees to the right {{icon:ast-0034-turn-90-right}} or to the left {{icon:ast-0035-turn-90-left}}, as the quarter-turn arrow shows.
- **Rotate.** The rotate icon {{icon:ast-0036-turn-around}} turns the boss 180 degrees to face its rear.

## 16.3 Boss Attacks

A boss attacks by the rules for enemy attacks (see §9.3), but its attacks may bear further icons with more powerful effects. Each attack on a *behaviour card* shows the boss's four *arcs* as normal (black) {{icon:ast-0039-neutral-boss-arc}}, attack (green) {{icon:ast-0040-attack-boss-arc}} or weak (red).

- **Area.** The area icon {{icon:ast-0038-area-attack-icon}} shows that the boss attacks whole arcs and not one character. The boss does not change its facing when it attacks: it attacks every character within *range* who is on a *node* in one of its attack arcs. A node that is split, with only half of it in an attack arc, is still attacked.
- **Repeat.** With the *repeat* icon {{icon:ast-0061-repeat-behaviour-or-attack}} the boss resolves its whole behaviour card the number of times shown.

An attack without the area icon targets one character within range, in whatever arc that character stands.

Each character attacked defends as against an enemy's attack (see §9.4, §9.5).

## 16.4 Boss Aggro

A *behaviour card* may name as its target the character holding the *aggro* token (see §7.5).

## 16.5 Boss Death

When the boss's health dial is reduced to zero, the boss is defeated and the encounter ends. Carry out these steps in order.

1. Each character gains six *souls*.
2. If there are two or more characters, fight the player versus player (PvP) encounter (see §20).
3. If this was the final boss, the players win the game (see §7.1).
4. Add one *spark* to the spark dial.
5. Give each character an *ember* token: every character is now *embered*.
6. The party returns to the *bonfire* and *rests* without spending a spark.
7. Set up the next stage: after the *mini-boss* (see §6.6) or after the *main-boss* (see §6.7).
