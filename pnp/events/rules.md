# Events Expansion II

A print-and-play module: 48 *event cards* (16 each of Levels 1 to 3), 8 trap cards and 9 item cards. A card overrides the rulebook where they differ.

## Setup

1. Shuffle three event decks, one for each level, and the trap deck.
2. Lay the item cards face up beside the *bonfire* tile: the wares.
3. Deal one event card of its level face down beside each *encounter card*.

## Event Cards

- Reveal the event card with its encounter card. It applies from then on.
- **Lingering.** The card stays and applies whenever that encounter is fought.
- **Once.** Discard the card when the party leaves the tile, unless its text lays it elsewhere.
- A *reset* changes no event card. A defeated boss returns discarded cards and cards on tiles to their decks.
- A card's treasure deck is the current tier's.

## Trap Cards

- Below Level 4, the top trap card replaces an encounter's first flipped trap token that is not blank, its foot towards the *entry nodes*.
- It hits every model on a marked *node*, enemies included, at the encounter's level. An enemy subtracts its block or resist.
- A character *dodges* and stays on the node, or *blocks* or *resists* with the armour card and its *upgrades* alone.
- A model hit takes the condition shown, or is *pushed* to an adjacent node the players choose. Shuffle the card back.

## Visitors and Wares

- A visitor's card stays beside the bonfire tile until its text removes it.
- Item cards are gained only as event cards say, are never sold, and return to the wares when discarded.
- No module card enters a treasure, encounter or behaviour deck or the *shop*, and no other card a module deck.

## Campaign

- Event cards return to their decks only when a *section* ends. Ludleth deals no cards: with him at the bonfire, *transposition* costs no *souls*.
