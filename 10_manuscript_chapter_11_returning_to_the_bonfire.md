# 11. Returning to the Bonfire

## 11.1 The Bonfire

The *bonfire* is a safe haven in an otherwise treacherous world. Two strangers wait beside it: the Firekeeper, a blindfolded woman who raises the characters' stats in exchange for *souls*, and Blacksmith Andre, a massive smith toiling endlessly at his forge.

- The party goes to the bonfire together. No character rests alone.
- The characters set out from the bonfire at the start of the game and again after every *rest*.
- Each character pays the Firekeeper and Andre with their own souls.

## 11.2 Resting

- A *rest* at the *bonfire* costs one *spark*.
- The party may rest whenever it is not in an encounter and holds a spark.
- When an encounter is *failed*, the party must rest (see §7.5).
- After a boss is defeated, the party rests without spending a spark.
- A rest is resolved as follows.
  1. Place every character model upright on the bonfire tile. Dead and *lost* characters return to play.
  2. Remove all damage cubes and *stamina* cubes from every character.
  3. Remove all *condition* tokens.
  4. Remove all *hollow* cubes.
  5. Flip *luck*, *estus flask* and *heroic action* tokens to their ready sides.
  6. The characters may pay the Firekeeper to raise their stats.
  7. *Reset* every *cleared* and failed encounter (see §11.5).

## 11.3 The Firekeeper

- Characters spend *souls* to raise their stats only during a *rest*.
- Every stat starts at its base tier and is raised one tier at a time.
- Each tier is paid for in order. No tier is passed over.
- A character may raise any number of stats, by any number of tiers, in one rest.
- Advance the stat's cube one space along the stat progression track for each tier bought (see §3.3).
- A character who raises a stat to tier four replaces the white cube on tier three with a red cube. A tier four stat is the tier three value plus five.

### Stat Tier Costs

| Stat tier | Cost in souls |
|---|---|
| Tier one | 2 |
| Tier two | 4 |
| Tier three | 8 |
| Tier four | 12 |

## 11.4 Blacksmith Andre

- Blacksmith Andre may be visited whenever the party is not in an encounter, without resting: to buy, to sell and to fit *upgrades*.
- Andre attaches weapon upgrade cards {{icon:ast-0044-weapon-upgrade}} from a character's *inventory* to that character's *equipped* weapon cards, and armour upgrade cards {{icon:ast-0046-armour-upgrade}} to equipped armour.
- An upgrade is fitted only where the equipment has a free upgrade slot and the character meets the upgrade card's stat requirements (see §4.1).
- Andre charges no *souls* for fitting an upgrade.
- A visit to Andre resets nothing. The party goes on from the tile it had reached.

### Shop Rules

- The cards of the *shop* lie face up. The *tier one* shop is open from the start of the game and the *tier two* shop from the defeat of the *mini-boss* (see §6.3).
- A character may buy any card in the shop at the price in the table below. The card goes into the character's inventory.
- A card bought is not replaced until the shops are restocked (see §6.3).
- Any card that is not *starting equipment* sells for one soul and goes to the bottom of its treasure deck, or to the box if it came from no deck.
- Starting equipment cannot be sold.
- A sold *ember* card returns to the tier one shop.
- An upgrade fitted to a sold card stays on it or returns to the inventory as its rule states (see §4.2).

|---|---|---|

## 11.5 Resuming Exploration

Before the party sets out after a *rest*, *reset* every *cleared* and *failed* encounter.

1. Flip every face-up *encounter card* face down. A black cube stays on its card.
2. Set aside any enemy models left on the encounter tiles.
3. Do not move terrain. Return each destroyed *barrel* to its node and turn each face-up *trap* token face down.
4. Leave treasure cards and dropped *souls* on their tiles.
5. With the Events Expansion II, gather the revealed *event cards* (see §7.5).

The party then moves to the encounter tile closest to the *bonfire*, where the encounter begins (see §7.5). Once the *fog gate* token lies on the bonfire tile, the party may go straight to the boss instead (see §15.1).
