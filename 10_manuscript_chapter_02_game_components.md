# 2. Game Components

Everything needed to play is listed in this chapter: characters, enemies, bosses, phantoms, cards, dice, tokens, tiles, and the components particular to each expansion. Each entry names the set that supplies it. Two set codes are used throughout: PWA is the Painted World of Ariamis expansion, and ToG is the Tomb of Giants expansion.

## 2.3 Bosses

A *mega-boss* is fought on the *mega-boss game board* supplied with its expansion (see §2.9), after a Level 4 encounter (see §6.7).

## 2.5 Treasure Cards

**Treasure card.** Any card from a treasure deck: *starting equipment*, *class-specific treasure*, *common treasure*, *legendary treasure*, or *boss treasure*. A treasure card that may be equipped to a dashboard slot is an *equipment card* (see §4.1). The treasure decks are built at setup from the cards listed below (see §6.2).

| Treasure cards | Core | Expansions |
|---|---|---|
| *Starting equipment* | 13 | 45 |
| *Class-specific treasure* | 40 | 120 |
| *Common treasure* | 60 | 145 |
| *Legendary treasure* | 10 | 20 |
| *Boss treasure* | 18 | 63 |

In all, 534 treasure cards are available. Each character's ten class-specific treasure cards comprise five class treasure cards and five *transposed treasure* cards. Each boss has between two and four boss treasure cards, one of which is awarded when the boss is defeated (see §16.5). The 12 invader treasure cards of the Phantoms expansion are listed with the expansion components (see §2.9).

## 2.6 Encounter Cards and Ambush Cards

### Encounter Cards

An *encounter card* lists the level, enemies, terrain, and traps of one encounter. One encounter card is dealt face down to each encounter tile at setup (see §6.5) and is revealed when the characters enter the tile (see §7.5, Encounter Start). The *encounter level* is shown by the card's level icon: Level 1 {{icon:ast-0007-encounter-level-1}}, Level 2 {{icon:ast-0011-encounter-level-2}}, or Level 3 {{icon:ast-0015-encounter-level-3}}. A trapped encounter is marked with the trap icon {{icon:ast-0021-trapped-encounter}} (see §5.5, Traps).

| Encounter cards | Core | Expansions |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 12 | 31 |
| Level 2 | 12 | 31 |
| Level 3 | 12 | 31 |
| Level 4 | 0 | 40 |

In all, 169 encounter cards are available. Level 4 encounter cards are supplied only by the mega-boss expansions, four with each, and are used only for the encounter that precedes a mega-boss (see §6.7).

### Ambush Cards

- 5x Ambush cards (Explorers)

Each *ambush card* shows Treasure or Mimic on its face. When *mimics* are in play, one ambush card is dealt face down beside each encounter tile and is revealed only when the characters choose to open that tile's *chest* (see §13).

## 2.7 Dice

- 5x Black dice {{icon:ast-0051-black-dice}} (Core)
- 4x Blue dice {{icon:ast-0053-blue-dice}} (Core)
- 2x Orange dice {{icon:ast-0055-orange-dice}} (Core)
- 4x Green dice (Core)

Black, blue, and orange dice are rolled for attacks (see §10.3) and for *block* and *resist* (see §9.4); each face shows a value from zero to four. Green dice are rolled for *dodges* (see §9.5) and parries (see §9.6); each face is blank or shows one dodge icon {{icon:ast-0062-dodge}}. The faces of each die are as follows.

| Die | Faces |
|---|---|
| Black | 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2 |
| Blue | 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3 |
| Orange | 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4 |
| Green | blank, blank, blank, dodge, dodge, dodge |

## 2.8 Tokens and Terrain Elements

### Cubes and Dials

- 32x Damage cubes (red) (Core)
- 32x Stamina cubes (black) (Core)
- 16x Stat cubes (white) (Core)
- 8x Hollow cubes
- 1x Spark dial (Core)

*Damage cubes* and *stamina cubes* are placed on the *endurance bar* (see §3.4). *Stat cubes* mark each character's *stat tier* on the stat progression track (see §3.3). *Hollow cubes* are of a colour distinct from the damage, stamina, and stat cubes; two are placed on the endurance bar of a character who is *hollow* (see §7.2).

The *spark* dial records the party's sparks (see §7.4). Each boss's *health dial* is supplied with the boss (see §2.3); the summon, invader, and mimic health dials are supplied with their expansions (see §2.9).

### Tokens

- 1x Aggro token (Core)
- 4x Luck tokens (Core)
- 4x Heroic action tokens (Core)
- 4x Estus flask tokens (Core)
- 4x Ember tokens (Core)
- 6x Wound tokens of value 1, 3x Wound tokens of value 3, and 1x Wound token of value 5 (Core)
- 10x Soul tokens of value 1, 5x Soul tokens of value 3, 3x Soul tokens of value 5, and 1x Soul token of value 8 (Core)
- 36x Condition tokens: 7x each of bleed, poison, frostbite, and stagger (Core and expansions); 4x calamity (Black Dragon Kalameet); 4x corrosion (Gaping Dragon)
- 20x Trap tokens (Core)
- 1x Fog gate token (Core)

The *aggro token* marks the character with *aggro* (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order). *Luck*, *heroic action*, and *estus flask* tokens have a ready side and a used side; the *ember token* marks an *embered* character (see §3.5).

*Wound tokens* record the damage suffered by enemies (see §8.2). Soul tokens record each character's *souls* (see §7.6); the supply of soul tokens is not a limit, and when it is exhausted the players track souls with any other counters.

*Condition* tokens mark *bleed* {{icon:ast-0063-bleed}}, *poison* {{icon:ast-0065-poison}}, *frostbite* {{icon:ast-0064-frostbite}}, *stagger* {{icon:ast-0066-stagger}}, *calamity*, and *corrosion* (see §8.5). *Trap tokens* are dealt face down to the basic nodes of a trapped encounter (see §5.5, Traps). The *fog gate* token marks the entrance to a boss encounter (see §15.1).

### Terrain Elements

- 4x Chest tokens {{icon:ast-0009-treasure-chest}} (Core)
- 5x Gravestone tokens {{icon:ast-0017-gravestone}} (Core)
- 8x Barrel tokens {{icon:ast-0013-barrel}} (Core)
- 4x Pillar tokens (Asylum Demon)

*Chests*, *gravestones*, and *barrels* are placed on the terrain nodes shown by the encounter card (see §5.5). The Explorers expansion supplies 18x Terrain models, which stand in place of the corresponding tokens. *Pillar tokens* are placed only on the pillar nodes of the Asylum Demon mega-boss game board (see §5.5, Pillars).

### Tiles

- 1x Bonfire tile (Core)
- 6x Encounter tiles, double-sided (Core)
- 2x Boss tiles (Core), the mini-boss tile and the main-boss tile

The *bonfire* tile is the characters' haven and the start of every exploration (see §11.1). Encounter tiles are the sites of encounters (see §5.1). The *boss tiles* are the sites of mini-boss and main-boss encounters (see §15.3); each mega-boss expansion supplies its own game board (see §2.9).

## 2.9 Expansion Components

The components below are supplied by the expansions named and are used only with the rules of the sections cited.

### Phantoms

- 10x Summon models, 10x Summon data cards (five standard, five advanced), 40x Summon behaviour cards, and 1x Summon health dial (see §12.1)
- 11x Invader models, 12x Invader data cards (six standard, six advanced), 64x Invader behaviour cards, 12x Invader treasure cards, and 1x Invader health dial (see §12.2)
- 6x Standard invader tokens, 6x Advanced invader tokens, and 3x Blank invader tokens (see §12.2)

An *invader token* shows an invader icon or is blank. When invaders are in play, one invader token is placed face down on each face-down encounter card and is revealed when the characters enter that tile (see §12.2).

### Explorers

- 2x Mimic models, 2x Mimic data cards (Hungry Mimic and Voracious Mimic), 14x Mimic behaviour cards, 1x Mimic health dial, and 5x Ambush cards (see §13)
- 18x Terrain models (see §2.8)

### Iron Keep

- 3x Titanite Scale cards (see §14)

### Characters

- 10x Armour set models, which represent armour treasure cards and have no effect on play

### Mega-Boss Expansions

Each mega-boss expansion supplies one set of the following components.

- 1x Mega-boss game board, double-sided, with a Level 4 encounter side and a mega-boss side (see §6.7 and §15.3)
- 4x Level 4 encounter cards (see §2.6)
- 1x Mega-boss model (the Executioner's Chariot has two), 1x Mega-boss data card, 1x Health dial, the behaviour cards listed on the data card, and 2x Mega-boss treasure cards (see §6.7)
- Black Dragon Kalameet only: 8x Strafe cards and 4x Calamity tokens (see §17.8)
- Executioner's Chariot only: 4x Track behaviour cards and 1x Mega-boss setup card (see §17.9)
- Asylum Demon only: 4x Pillar tokens (see §17.3)

The *mega-boss game board* is used with its Level 4 encounter side face up for the Level 4 encounter and with its mega-boss side face up for the mega-boss encounter (see §6.7). The Executioner's Chariot expansion also supplies the Black Hollow Mage and Falchion Skeleton enemies (see §2.2) and six encounter cards of Levels 1 to 3. The Asylum Demon expansion also supplies the Asylum Demon mini-boss (see §2.3).
