# 2. Game Components

Each entry names the set that supplies it. PWA is the Painted World of Ariamis set and ToG is the Tomb of Giants set. Of those two sets this game uses the enemies, the bosses and the new treasure cards; their encounter cards and event cards are not used (see Appendix B).

## 2.1 Characters

Each character has a model, a character dashboard, three or four *starting equipment* cards and ten class-specific treasure cards: five *class treasure* and five *transposed treasure*.

- Herald (Core)
- Warrior (Core)
- Knight (Core)
- Assassin (Core)
- Sorcerer (Characters or PWA)
- Deprived (Characters or PWA)
- Mercenary (Characters or PWA)
- Cleric (Characters or ToG)
- Pyromancer (Characters or ToG)
- Thief (Characters or ToG)

The Characters expansion also holds 10x Armour set models, which represent armour treasure cards and have no effect on play.

## 2.6 Encounter, Event and Ambush Cards

An *encounter card* shows the level, enemies, terrain and *traps* of one encounter. Its level icon reads Level 1 {{icon:ast-0007-encounter-level-1}}, Level 2 {{icon:ast-0011-encounter-level-2}} or Level 3 {{icon:ast-0015-encounter-level-3}}. A trapped encounter carries the trap icon {{icon:ast-0021-trapped-encounter}}.

| Encounter cards | Core | Expansions |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 12 | 19 |
| Level 2 | 12 | 19 |
| Level 3 | 12 | 19 |
| Level 4 | 0 | 40 |

In all, 133 encounter cards. Of each of Levels 1 to 3, Darkroot and Iron Keep hold six cards each, Explorers five and Executioner's Chariot two. Level 4 cards come only with the mega-bosses.

- Events Expansion II, a community expansion printed at home: 49x *Event cards* in three levels, 8x Trap cards and 14x treasure cards
- Encounter decks, print-and-play modules of this book: 27x encounter cards for ToG and 27x for PWA (9 of each level), each with a key card
- 5x *Ambush cards* (Explorers), each showing Treasure or Mimic
