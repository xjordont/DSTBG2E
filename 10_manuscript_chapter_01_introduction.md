# 1. Introduction

Dark Souls: The Board Game, Second Edition is a cooperative dungeon crawl for one to four players. Each player controls one character. The party explores a chain of encounters, learns the behaviours of its enemies, returns to the *bonfire* to rest and grow stronger, and at last faces a boss.

The party must defeat a *mini-boss* and then a *main-boss*, both chosen during setup (see §6.5). If the party chooses, a *mega-boss* waits beyond the main-boss (see §6.7). Characters who die rise *hollow*, and regain their humanity only by resting at the bonfire or by consuming an *ember* (see §7.2). The bonfire's *sparks* are few, and every rest spends one, except the rest that follows the defeat of a boss (see §7.4).

Nameless accursed undead, seek strength, and prepare to die.
