## Foreword

This book is the complete rulebook for Dark Souls: The Board Game, Second Edition. It gathers the rules of the core game and of every expansion into one text. Nothing beyond this book and the game components is needed to play. Chapters 2 to 6 cover the components and setup, chapters 7 to 17 the standard game, chapters 18 and 19 campaign play, and chapter 20 player versus player (PvP).

Content from an expansion is in play whenever the party owns that expansion. Its rules are introduced by the name of the set. A few printed values are changed: weapon requirements (see Appendix A) and some other cards (see Appendix B).

Terms set in italics are defined in the glossary (see §21). An example begins *Example.* and is not itself a rule. Numbered steps are carried out in order. A reference such as (see §7.5) names the section that holds a rule.
