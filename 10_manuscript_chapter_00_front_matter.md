## Foreword

This book is the complete rulebook for Dark Souls: The Board Game, Second Edition. It gathers the rules of the core game and of every expansion into one text, resolves the conflicts between them, and incorporates the revisions that extended play has shown to be necessary. Nothing beyond this book and the game components is required to play.

The Second Edition alters the shape of play in a few deliberate ways. Encounters are shorter, and downtime between turns is reduced. Each defensive choice, whether to *block*, *resist*, *dodge* or *parry*, is viable. Andre's shop offers a chosen selection rather than a random one, and progression is paced across the whole adventure. Where the printed text of a card conflicts with this book, this book governs.

Terms set in italics are defined in the Glossary (see §21). Worked examples are labelled *Example* and are not themselves rules. Numbered procedures are followed in order. Where a rule is stated in one section and used in another, the using section refers to the owning section with a reference in the form (see §N.M).
