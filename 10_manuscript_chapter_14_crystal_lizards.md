# 14. Crystal Lizards

*Crystal lizards* are strange, glimmering reptiles that offer a powerful reward to a character who kills one before it escapes. With the Iron Keep expansion they are in play.

- When an *encounter card* shows the crystal lizard symbol, a crystal lizard appears. During encounter setup, place a crystal lizard on each *terrain node* {{icon:ast-0014-primary-terrain-node}} the card marks for one. Some encounters hold more than one.
- Crystal lizards take their turns like any other enemy, in descending *threat* order. Their behaviour is to run from the characters, which makes them hard to kill. They never attack.
- The character who kills a crystal lizard takes one *Titanite* Scale card into their *inventory* at once (see §4.2).
- The expansion holds three Titanite Scale cards. Once all three have been taken, a crystal lizard gives nothing.
- When the last other enemy of the encounter dies, every crystal lizard still on the tile is removed from play. Those lizards have escaped and give no treasure. The encounter is *cleared* all the same.
- If a crystal lizard dies during an encounter, no crystal lizard appears in later attempts at that encounter. Mark the tile with an upside-down crystal lizard model.
