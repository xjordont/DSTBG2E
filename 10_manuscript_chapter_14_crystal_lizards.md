# 14. Crystal Lizards

Crystal lizards are glimmering reptiles whose hides are studded with the rarest titanite. They want no part of a fight and are gone the moment the last blade is sheathed. A character quick enough to kill one before it escapes carries off a rare prize.

*Crystal lizards* are an optional rule. They require the Iron Keep expansion. The party decides during setup whether crystal lizards are in play.

When an *encounter card* shows the crystal lizard symbol, one crystal lizard model is placed on each *terrain node* {{icon:ast-0014-primary-terrain-node}} that the card marks with that symbol. The models are placed during Encounter Start (see §7.5, Encounter Start). A crystal lizard is an enemy model with its own *data card* (see §8.2), and the rules for enemy models apply to it except where this chapter says otherwise.

A crystal lizard takes its *turn* with the other enemy models, in descending *threat* order (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order). On its turn it resolves the *behaviour icons* on its data card, which move it away from the characters. A crystal lizard makes no attack.

A crystal lizard is not scaled. Neither enemy scaling (see §8.3) nor the *bonfire ascetic* (see §18.7) changes its health or its movement.

Crystal lizards do not count towards clearing an *encounter*. The encounter is *cleared* when every other enemy model has been killed (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution). Every crystal lizard still on the tile at that moment is removed at once: it has escaped, and it awards nothing.

When a character kills a crystal lizard, that character takes one Titanite Scale card from the supply at once and places it in their *inventory* (see §4.2). The Iron Keep expansion supplies three Titanite Scale cards. When none remains, a killed crystal lizard awards nothing, and no crystal lizard is placed in any later encounter.

Once a crystal lizard has been killed in an encounter, no crystal lizard is placed in that encounter again, however many times the encounter is *reset* and fought (see §11.5). The party marks the tile to record the kill, for example by laying the killed model upside down beside the tile's encounter card. Until a crystal lizard has been killed there, the crystal lizards are placed again each time the encounter is set up.
