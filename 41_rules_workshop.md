## 1. What the house rules tell us

All four attack the same three things in the published game: the grind of re-clearing rooms, defence that is all block and no skill, and bookkeeping. Your manuscript already answers each one, usually the same way they do:

| Problem | Your manuscript | House rules that do the same |
|---|---|---|
| Grind | treasure pre-dealt to tiles; cleared encounters can be skipped; reduced reward for repeats | Bare Essentials (pre-dealt treasure by level, dash through); Zed (never re-clear) |
| Slow rounds | all enemies act, then all characters | Bare Essentials and Combined Edition (the same round shape) |
| Flat defence | parry and riposte; failed dodge still blocks with armour; pay for an extra dodge die | Jolly Souls and Bare Essentials (parry; armour-only block after a failed dodge) |
| Flat progression | treasure tiers by stat requirement (25 and over is tier two) | Bare Essentials (same idea, three levels) |

So the design does not need new mechanics from them. What they do better than our book is **brevity**: the Combined Edition states a whole game in 18 pages.

## 2. Proposed ground rule

**Your rule stands unless a player would hit a hole in it in their first game. Where there is a hole, the fix is one sentence.** Under that rule the decisions fall into four blocks.

## 7. What to take from the Combined Edition's format (after the rules are settled)

- **Order of play as the order of the book**: setup, before an encounter, the tile, the encounter, enemy turn, party turn, after the encounter, bosses.
- **Pictures carry the anatomy**: a card or dashboard with numbered call-outs and the list beside it, in place of paragraphs.
- **The icon sits in the margin beside the rule it governs.**
- **Small framed tables** for every set of numbers; examples only as a captioned picture.
- **Expansion rules flagged by a margin symbol**, so the core reads clean.
- **One page of icons at the back.**
- About 300 words a page, 18 pages. Our book at the same density, with your campaigns and the boss entries, would be near 50.
