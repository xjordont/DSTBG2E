# 22. F.A.Q.

**Who holds the aggro token?** At the start of an encounter, the character with the highest *threat*. After that the *aggro* token passes to the last character to deal damage, and stays where it is if no one deals any (see §7.5).

**Does a failed dodge let the full damage through?** No. The attack hits, but the character still rolls the *block* or *resist* dice of the armour card and its *upgrades*, and keeps the two highest results (see §9.5).

**When may a character parry?** A *parry* needs a weapon or shield in the left weapon slot, so a character wielding a weapon *two-handed* cannot parry, and the attacker must be on the character's *node*. A boss cannot be parried (see §9.6).

**Does drinking estus clear stamina?** No. A character who chooses to *drink estus* removes all damage cubes, and stamina cubes stay. The *rest* action removes all stamina cubes (see §10.1).

**Does a character who dies lose their souls?** No. The character keeps their *souls* and, if the encounter is *cleared*, returns *hollow*. Souls are dropped only when an encounter is *failed*, which takes the death of every character (see §7.2, §7.6).

**Is damage healed when an encounter is cleared?** No. Each surviving character removes all stamina cubes and *condition* tokens, and nothing more. *Estus flask*, *luck* and *heroic action* tokens are not restored (see §7.5).

**Must a cleared encounter be fought again after a rest?** No. A *reset* encounter that has been cleared before may be skipped: each character suffers one damage and the party passes through. A failed encounter cannot be skipped (see §7.5).

**When may characters raise stats and visit Blacksmith Andre?** Stats are raised only during a rest at the *bonfire*. Andre may be visited whenever the party is not in an encounter, without resting, to buy, to sell and to fit upgrades (see §11.3, §11.4).

**Which treasure deck holds class treasure and boss treasure?** Every *class treasure* card is *tier one*. In the standard game no card is handed over for defeating a boss: the treasure cards of the *mini-boss* and the *main-boss* are shuffled into the *tier two* deck when the mini-boss falls (see §6.2).

**When does a boss heat up?** At once, when its health first falls to its *heat-up* point or below: one random heat-up card is shuffled together with its behaviour deck and discard pile to form a new deck (see §15.5).
