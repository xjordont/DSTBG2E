# 22. F.A.Q.

The questions in this chapter are those that arise most often at the table. Each answer restates a rule of this book and cites the section that owns it. Where an answer and its section disagree, the section governs.

## 22.1 Characters and Actions

**Q.** Which weapon slot is the left weapon slot, and which is the right?

**A.** The left weapon slot is the weapon slot on the left of the dashboard as its player reads it, and the right weapon slot is the one on the right. A character moves cards between them only by the Equip minor action during an encounter (see §3.2; §10.1).

**Q.** May a character Rest after walking?

**A.** Yes. Only the *Run* minor action prevents the Rest major action in the same turn. Walk, Equip, Heroic Action, and the movement of a *shift* icon do not (see §10.1).

**Q.** May a character run more than once in a turn?

**A.** Yes. Run may be taken any number of times in a turn, at one stamina for each node. Walk, Equip, and Heroic Action may each be taken once (see §10.1).

**Q.** Does Drink Estus remove *hollow cubes*, condition tokens, or stamina cubes?

**A.** No. Drink Estus removes damage cubes only (see §3.5, Estus Flask).

**Q.** May a *heroic action* be used during another model's turn?

**A.** Only if its printed wording says so. Otherwise it is a minor action taken during the character's own turn, and its printed wording overrides any rule with which it conflicts (see §3.5, Heroic Action).

**Q.** May a character pool *souls* with another character?

**A.** Only to burn the *bonfire ascetic*, which the party pays for together (see §18.7). Every other cost is paid from the acting character's own souls: the Firekeeper's stat tiers, Blacksmith Andre's cards, a *summon*, and *transposition* (see §7.6).

**Q.** May a new player join a game in progress?

**A.** Yes, while the characters are on the bonfire tile and at the cost of one *spark*. The new character begins with starting equipment, base stats, and no souls (see §18.4).

## 22.2 Attack and Defence

**Q.** May the *two-handed* reroll be used together with the *luck* token?

**A.** Yes. The two-handed reroll rerolls one die, and the luck token rerolls any or all of the dice. Both may be used on the same roll, in either order, and each new result stands (see §10.3; §10.4).

**Q.** Does the limit of one spell attack option per turn include *upgrade spells*?

**A.** Yes. Attaching an upgrade spell is done as the Attack major action and is the character's one spell attack option for that turn (see §4.2, Upgrade Spells).

**Q.** How is a *block* or *resist* roll resolved with one die, or with no dice?

**A.** With one die, the defence total is that die's result. With no dice, the defence total is zero, and the character suffers the full attack value. Fixed modifiers are applied in either case (see §9.4).

**Q.** May a character with no dodge dice attempt to *dodge*?

**A.** Yes. The character pays one stamina. Against a dodge difficulty of zero the dodge succeeds without a roll. Against any higher difficulty a roll of no dice fails, unless the character pays two further stamina to add one green die (see §9.5).

**Q.** After a failed dodge, do the modifiers of the armour dice still apply?

**A.** Yes. The character rolls only the block or resist dice of the armour card and of the upgrade cards attached to it, and applies the fixed modifiers of those dice. The dice and modifiers of weapons, shields, and spells are ignored (see §9.5, Failed Dodge).

**Q.** May a character with a two-handed weapon *parry*?

**A.** No. A parry requires a shield in the left weapon slot. A weapon that bears the two-handed icon requires the other weapon slot to be empty, and a one-handed weapon wielded two-handed has no shield beside it (see §9.6; §10.4).

**Q.** May an enemy dodge or parry a *riposte*?

**A.** No. A riposte is resolved as any character attack against the enemy's block or resist value. Against a boss, a riposte made from a *weak arc* gains the weak-arc die (see §9.6; §15.6, Weak Arcs).

**Q.** How is a weapon *scaled*?

**A.** A weapon is scaled while the character's stat exceeds the weapon's highest stat requirement by five or more. Each of its attack options then adds one to its attack value. Spells are never scaled (see §10.5).

**Q.** With four characters, does the increase of one to enemy attack values apply to a *push-move* that deals damage?

**A.** Yes. The increase applies to every attack icon on an enemy's data card and to the damage value of a push-move. It does not apply to a boss, a *mimic*, a *phantom*, or a *crystal lizard* (see §8.3).

**Q.** Do dead and *lost* characters count for enemy scaling?

**A.** Yes. Enemies are scaled for the number of characters in the party, whether or not every character is in play (see §8.3).

## 22.3 Conditions and Terrain

**Q.** How is *stagger* applied to a roll?

**A.** One is subtracted from the total of the attack, block, resist, or dodge roll, after the dice have been added and every reroll taken. It is not subtracted from each die. The token is removed at the end of the staggered model's next complete turn (see §8.5, Stagger).

**Q.** Does *frostbite* stop the one stamina recovered at the start of a character's turn?

**A.** Yes. A character with a frostbite token recovers no stamina by any means: the character does not recover one stamina at the start of the turn and cannot take the Rest major action (see §8.5, Frostbite).

**Q.** A character holds three *poison* tokens. How much damage does the character suffer?

**A.** Three damage at the end of the character's turn, one for each token. All three tokens are then removed (see §8.5, Poison).

**Q.** Does the *embered* reduction of damage apply to *bleed*, poison, and trap damage?

**A.** No. The reduction applies only to an attack that deals three or more damage. Bleed and poison damage are not attacks, and neither is the damage of a trap (see §7.3; §8.5; §5.5, Traps).

**Q.** May a character dodge into a node that holds a *barrel*?

**A.** Yes. The barrel is destroyed. A character cannot dodge into a node that holds a *gravestone*, a *chest*, or a *pillar token*. A character with no valid node to enter may still dodge, and stays on their node (see §5.5; §9.5, Successful Dodge).

**Q.** Does a character who dodges onto a face-down *trap token* trigger it?

**A.** Yes. A character who enters the node by a move, by the movement that follows a dodge, or by a *push* turns the token face up, and each character on the node must suffer its damage or dodge it (see §5.5, Traps).

**Q.** Do enemies trigger traps?

**A.** No. Only characters trigger traps, and only characters are affected by them (see §5.5, Traps).

## 22.4 Encounters, Souls, and the Bonfire

**Q.** Who decides when the rules leave a choice or a tie to the players?

**A.** The players decide together. A tie in *threat* between characters is settled at Encounter Start and is kept for the whole encounter, and a tie between enemy models is settled by the order stated for enemy turns (see §7.5). When the players cannot agree on which character receives a treasure card, the card goes to the character who holds the *aggro token* (see §7.7).

**Q.** Who holds the aggro token when its holder dies?

**A.** The token moves at once to the living character with the highest threat (see §7.5, Encounter Turn Order).

**Q.** May a *hollow* character skip encounters that are marked *cleared*?

**A.** Yes, if the character has room on the endurance bar. Each character places one stamina cube for each encounter skipped, to a maximum of three, and the party may skip only if every character has free spaces for those cubes. A hollow character has eight usable spaces (see §7.5, Encounter Start; §7.2).

**Q.** After resting at the bonfire, may a cleared encounter still be skipped?

**A.** Yes. The rest resets every encounter, but a cleared encounter keeps its black cube. When the characters next enter its tile, the party may skip it or fight it again for the *reduced reward* and no treasure. An encounter without a black cube must be fought (see §7.5; §11.5).

**Q.** What is the reduced reward for an encounter that is cleared again?

**A.** Half the full soul reward, rounded down. The reward table gives both values, and campaign play uses the same table (see §7.6; §18.5).

**Q.** An encounter is cleared, later failed, and then cleared once more. Does it pay the full reward again?

**A.** No. The full reward and the treasure cards are awarded only while the treasure cards dealt to the tile still lie on it. A later clear pays the reduced reward and no treasure, and marks the encounter cleared again (see §7.5, Encounter Resolution).

**Q.** The characters are required to rest, and the *spark* dial shows zero. What happens?

**A.** The players lose the game (see §7.1).

**Q.** Does a *Level 4 encounter* reset when the characters rest?

**A.** Not once it has been cleared: a cleared Level 4 encounter is not fought again. A failed Level 4 encounter resets as any other encounter does (see §6.7, Level 4 Encounters).

**Q.** Where does a sold boss treasure card go?

**A.** To the game box. Boss treasure enters neither treasure deck (see §11.4, Sales).

## 22.5 Bosses

**Q.** Are Ornstein & Smough one boss or two?

**A.** One boss with two models. The models share one data card, one health dial, and one behaviour deck, and the boss heats up once. Each behaviour card names the model that acts. A character who attacks either model attacks the boss, and the weak-arc die is judged against the arcs of the model attacked (see §17.2).

**Q.** A campaign section lists the Gargoyle twice. Are the two fights separate encounters?

**A.** No. The *Twin Gargoyles* are one boss encounter in two parts. The characters do not rest between the parts, and the souls, the boss treasure card, and the spark are awarded only when the second Gargoyle is defeated (see §17.1, Twin Gargoyles).

**Q.** In what order do the characters act in a boss encounter?

**A.** The players choose which living character takes each character turn. No living character takes a second turn in a round until every other living character has taken one (see §16.1).

**Q.** A character is pushed off a boss's node. Must the character stay in the same arc?

**A.** Yes. The character is pushed to a valid adjacent node in the arc in which the character is placed. If a wall leaves no such node, the character is pushed to a valid adjacent node that touches the wall (see §15.6, Characters and Arcs).

**Q.** Does a character who dies in a mega-boss encounter leave the game?

**A.** No. Death and hollowing are resolved in a mega-boss encounter as in any other encounter (see §7.2; §15.2).

**Q.** When does the aggro token move in a boss encounter?

**A.** At the end of the turn of each character who inflicted damage on the boss during that turn. Damage inflicted by a summon does not move it (see §16.4).

## 22.6 Phantoms, Mimics, and Crystal Lizards

**Q.** When does a *summon* act in a boss encounter?

**A.** After each character's turn and before the boss's next turn (see §12.1, Summon Turns).

**Q.** May an *invader* appear in a boss encounter?

**A.** No. Invader tokens are placed only on the encounter cards of encounter tiles (see §12.2, Invader Tokens).

**Q.** May a summon and an invader take part in the same encounter?

**A.** No. A summon is called only for a boss encounter, and no invader appears in one (see §12.1; §12.2).

**Q.** An invader is on the tile and a character dies. Does the encounter continue?

**A.** Yes. The invader vanishes at once and awards nothing, and the encounter continues against the remaining enemy models. The invader does not return (see §12.2, Combat with an Invader).

**Q.** Must an invader be killed for its encounter to be cleared?

**A.** Not always. While the invader is on the tile, the encounter is not cleared. It is cleared when every enemy model, the invader included, has been killed, or when the invader has vanished and every other enemy model has been killed (see §12.2, End of an Invasion).

**Q.** A chest proves to be a *mimic*, and every character dies. What happens?

**A.** The mimic encounter is failed, and with it the encounter that held the chest. The mimic returns to its chest, and the characters must rest at the bonfire. Each later time that encounter is cleared, the party again decides whether to open the chest (see §13.4).

**Q.** Do *crystal lizards* prevent an encounter from being cleared?

**A.** No. The encounter is cleared when every other enemy model has been killed, and every crystal lizard still on the tile escapes and awards nothing (see §14).

## 22.7 Campaign Play

**Q.** When may a character transpose boss treasure?

**A.** During any rest at the bonfire once the first *section* of the campaign has been completed. Each transposition costs one boss treasure card and four souls (see §18.8).

**Q.** Does the bonfire ascetic increase enemy attack values?

**A.** No. It increases the base health of every enemy that is subject to enemy scaling by two, in addition to the increase for the number of characters. Attack values and movement values are unchanged (see §18.7).

**Q.** Is the *fog gate* a shortcut in campaign play?

**A.** No. In campaign play the fog gate token marks the entrance to the boss encounter and nothing more (see §18.2).

**Q.** How many sparks may a party hold in campaign play?

**A.** One more than the number with which the party began. A spark is gained for each boss defeated, up to that maximum (see §18.3).
