# 22. F.A.Q.

**Does a failed dodge let the full damage through?** No. The attack hits, but the character still rolls the *block* or *resist* dice of the armour card and its *upgrades*, and keeps the two highest results (see §9.5).

**Does a character who dies lose their souls?** No. The character keeps their *souls*. Souls are dropped only when an encounter is *failed*, which takes the death of two characters or of the last one (see §7.2, §7.5).
